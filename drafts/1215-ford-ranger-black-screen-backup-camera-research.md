# RESEARCH: 1215-ford-ranger-black-screen-backup-camera-recall
**Journalist:** Clara Rollover (consumer advocate — owner-action guidance)
**Date:** 2026-10-19

## Story
NHTSA Campaign 26V605000 (Ford 26C42): 11,405 model-year 2026 Ford Rangers with the
10.1-inch SYNC 4 center stack screen. The screen may lose power and go blank/black;
when it does, the rearview camera image disappears when the truck is shifted into
reverse. Trucks fail FMVSS 111 "Rear Visibility" (S5.5.1, S5.5.3, S5.5.6).

## Verified facts (primary sources)
1. **NHTSA official record (api.nhtsa.gov, verified live 2026-10-19):** Campaign
   26V605000; 2026 Ford Ranger; component BACK OVER PREVENTION:DISPLAY FUNCTION;
   summary: "The screen may lose power and fail to display the rearview camera
   image. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with FMVSS 111, Rear Visibility."
   Consequence: rearview camera image that does not display reduces driver's view
   behind vehicle, increasing risk of crash. **Remedy: currently under development.**
   Interim owner letters mailed October 7, 2026. Ford customer service 1-866-436-7332,
   Ford number 26C42. VINs searchable on nhtsa.gov since September 25, 2026.
   Report received 22/09/2026.
2. **Ford Part 573 filing (via ranger6g.com forum post of the NHTSA filing, Sep 22, 2026):**
   Production dates Apr 21, 2026 – Sep 3, 2026. Root cause: suspect capacitor lot in
   the center stack printed circuit board assembly (PCBA). **Estimated 47% of the
   11,405-vehicle population has the defect.** Ford traced the capacitor lot through
   PCBA serial → center stack assembly serial → VIN; affected trucks are NOT in VIN
   order, so build date alone does not determine inclusion.
3. **Detroit Free Press (Sep 28, 2026):** This is Ford's **77th recall of 2026.**
   Ford had 15 warranty claims "potentially related to this condition" as of Sep 14.
   Ford is **not aware of any accidents** from a screen going black while reversing.
   Interim letters planned Oct 5; fix free once developed.
4. **US DOT / NHTSA rear-visibility final rule briefing (transportation.gov):** FMVSS 111
   required rear visibility tech on all new vehicles under 10,000 lbs by May 2018.
   ~210 fatalities and ~15,000 injuries per year from backover crashes. **Children
   under 5 = 31% of backover fatalities; adults 70+ = 26%.** The rule requires a
   10-ft by 20-ft field of view directly behind the vehicle to appear within 2 seconds
   of shifting into reverse.

## Kill test
- Fresh: interim letters mailed October 7, 2026 (this month); no remedy available.
- Newsworthy: 11,405 brand-new 2026 trucks; a federally mandated safety device
  disabled by an infotainment failure; owners received a "good luck, be careful" letter.
- Novel angle: your mandated backup camera lives on the same circuit board as your
  Spotify. The recall isn't a camera recall, it's an entertainment-system recall with
  a safety-device body count risk. Plus the 47% estimated defect rate is unusually
  high — this is not a "small batch" tail-risk recall, it's a coin flip across 11k
  trucks.
- No queue overlap: existing Ranger recalls (994, 1075, 1128) are counterfeit
  capacitor and part-tracing beats, not this; backup-camera recalls (969, 977, 1057)
  are GM glue and Rivian notification bugs, not Ford; FMVSS 111 is unaddressed.

## Owner action items (Clara Rollover beat)
- Check VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls (searchable since Sep 25) — do NOT rely on build
  date; Ford says affected VINs are not sequential.
- Interim letters began mailing Oct 5-7; the remedy is not yet available, so do not
  book service expecting parts — confirm remedy availability with the dealer first.
- Until repaired: treat the rearview camera as unreliable — do a physical 360 walk-
  around in driveways/parking lots; backover victims are disproportionately children
  under 5 and adults over 70.
