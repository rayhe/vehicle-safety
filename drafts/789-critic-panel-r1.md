## Critic Scores

### 1. 🔍 General Editor — **8.5 / 10**

The angle is genuinely distinct: a triple recall on the same cosmetic/structural part with an escalating population and no fix is a narrative arc, not just a data dump. The 503 → 18,163 → 74,578 progression is genuinely surprising and gives the piece its backbone. Structure follows template correctly (kicker → headline → lede with bolded stat → pull stat → body → references → disclaimer). Flow is excellent — the opening paragraph sets up the escalation cleanly, and the "recall without a fix" pivot in paragraph two lands.

**Weakness:** Zero crashes, zero injuries across five years means this is a road-debris/cosmetic hazard, not a life-threatening defect. The article leans on the Stellantis quality pattern (backup cameras, 2.6x recall ratio) to give it weight, which works but the core story on its own is lower-stakes than most Crash Report articles. The article knows this and owns it — the counterargument section states the low-risk reality at full strength.

### 2. 🗣️ Voice Coach — **8.5 / 10**

This reads as Clara Rollover: direct, practical, consumer-angry. The "your spoiler might fly off at highway speed, and we will contact you again when we know how to stop it" line is vintage Clara — paraphrasing a corporate letter with the veneer stripped off. The closing advice section is genuinely useful and sounds like someone who reads recall notices for a living. The kicker about "shipped it broken and we will keep shipping it broken until someone notices" has editorial edge without crossing into polemic.

**Weakness:** The sentence rhythm gate fails on short-sentence percentage (30.6% vs 15% max). Several adjacent short fragments create a choppy, staccato pattern that reads more like bullet points than prose. Specifically: the Limitations paragraph is three short sentences in a row; the actionable section has back-to-back 5-6 word fragments. These need merging without losing Clara's punchy cadence.

**Revision needed:** Combine "Dealers received notice on August 20. Owner letters start going out September 10." into one sentence. Combine "Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. Do not wait for the letter." into one. Merge the Limitations micro-sentences into 1–2 longer constructions.

Limitations are honest. No fabrication risk detected. The "seven camera recalls" and "2.6 recalls per vehicle sold" claims are attributed.

No ethical concerns. The piece advocates checking your VIN, which is responsible.
