# Research: #1003 — Toyota Camry 7-Inch Cluster Recall (Trim-Gated Turn Signals)

**Slug:** 1003-toyota-camry-7-inch-turn-signals-recall
**Journalist:** Clara Rollover (consumer safety advocate — "before you sign that lease" energy)
**Kicker:** The Gap
**Angle in one sentence:** Toyota's cheapest 2025-2026 Camry trims route turn signals and hazard lights through a buggy 7-inch screen that can black out at startup; the $33,700+ XLE/XSE with the 12.3-inch screen are immune — working turn signals are effectively a trim perk.

## Kill test
Genuinely newsworthy? Yes. 655,000 vehicles globally (announced Sep 27, 2026, yesterday); 508,354 in the US. Novel angle: every outlet covered the recall; nobody framed the trim split or counted the FMVSS violations. Site has zero Camry-recall coverage. Verdict: PROCEED.

## Verified facts (from Part 573, verbatim where noted)
- NHTSA campaign **26V511**, Toyota **26TB16 / 26TA16**. Population: **508,354** (2025-2026 Camry Hybrid), built Aug 15, 2024 – Jun 9, 2026, Georgetown KY.
- Supplier: **Visteon Corporation (Mexico)**; parts 83800-AQ020 / 83800-AQ021.
- "Estimated percentage with defect: **100%**" — every unit carries the buggy software; it "may only occur randomly at startup."
- When it blanks: "the turn signal lamps, the hazard lamp function, and certain warning buzzers (including the key left in ignition and the driver/passenger seat belt reminders) **do not function**."
- FMVSS noncompliance: **101** (controls/displays), **108** (lamps), **114** (theft/rollaway — key reminder), **208** (occupant crash protection — seatbelt reminder). One screen bug breaks FOUR federal standards.
- Chronology: first field reports **July 2024**; could not be replicated; Oct 2025–Apr 2026 deeper investigation; bench replication failed; **late July 2026** replicated on a complete vehicle; recall decided **July 31, 2026**. Two years from first complaint to recall.
- Remedy: free dealer software update; owner notifications Sep 21 – Oct 5, 2026.
- Global figure (VNAuto, Sep 27, 2026): ~**655,000** Camrys, Dec 2023–Jul 2026 production, US/Japan/Thailand plants.
- Trim split: LE ($28,700), SE ($31,000), Nightshade ($32,300, 2026) get the 7-inch unit → recalled. XLE ($33,700), XSE ($34,900) get 12.3-inch → unaffected. SE→XLE step: **$2,700**. LE→XLE: $5,000.

## Original contribution
1. The **trim-perk framing** with the price arithmetic: $2,700 (SE→XLE) or $5,000 (LE→XLE) separates working-from-software-glitched turn signals.
2. **Four-FMVSS count** — no outlet noted a single screen bug violates 101, 108, 114, and 208 simultaneously.
3. The **two-year replication gap** as the scary part: an intermittent software defect that no bench test could reproduce for 24 months is the hardest kind to trust a "fixed" notice on.
4. **100% defect rate + random manifestation** — the worst combination: universal exposure, unpredictable occurrence.

## Counterargument (full strength)
The failure only happens at startup — a blank screen in your driveway is noticeable, and a restart likely clears it. The two-year replication struggle suggests it's rare. Similar Toyota/Lexus meters with different software were never affected. The fix is a free software flash, not a parts shortage wait. No injuries or crashes appear in the filing. This is a software glitch with a software fix, not a structural defect.

## Limitations
- Per-startup failure probability is unknown (random; filing gives no rate).
- No injury/crash data in the Part 573 report; "increased risk of crash" is NHTSA's language, not an observed count.
- The 655,000 global figure comes from VNAuto, not a Toyota/NHTSA filing; treat as reported, not verified.
- Unknown whether a restart reliably clears a blank meter — filing says only that the dealer flash is the remedy.

## Actionable takeaways
- Own a 2025-2026 Camry LE/SE/Nightshade? Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls (recall 26V511 / Toyota 26TA16) and get the free software update.
- If the cluster is blank at startup, treat the car as signal-less — the lamps don't light, not just the dashboard icons.
- Shopping used? Verify the recall flash was done before you buy.
