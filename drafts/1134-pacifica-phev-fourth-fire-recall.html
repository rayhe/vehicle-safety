Your Minivan Can Catch Fire While Parked. This Is the Fourth Recall That Says So.

Clara Rollover • Clautomotive Desk
October 11, 2026

Before you sign that lease, you might want to see this. Chrysler is recalling 17,277 Pacifica plug-in hybrid minivans because the high-voltage battery can catch fire while the van is parked with the ignition off.[1] Not while fast-charging. Not after a crash. In your driveway, overnight, doing nothing at all.

17,277
Pacifica PHEVs recalled under NHTSA campaign 26V362. Owners must park outside and stop charging, because the fire risk exists even with the ignition off.

Inside the pack hide battery cells made on an alternative assembly line at LG Energy Solution. Chrysler estimates about one percent of the recalled vans carry the defective cells, which works out to roughly 173 vehicles, and the company has logged four fires since August 2023.[2] Nobody was hurt. That is the good news, and it is about where the good news ends.

Here is the part that should make you angry. This is the fourth fire-related recall for the Pacifica hybrid, and every one of them found a different way to burn. In 2018, more than 10,000 of the 2017-2018 models were recalled for a fuel-system fire risk. A later campaign pulled about 19,516 of the 2017-2021 vans after seven fires surfaced in warranty data, every one of them with the vehicle switched off. Then came 27,634 vans recalled for a corroding 12-volt connector behind the driver seat, with Chrysler warning owners not to let a wet umbrella touch the second-row floor. Now it is the high-voltage battery again, through a completely new failure pathway.[3][4]

Chrysler's remedy tells you how seriously it takes this one. Beyond the free software update and pack replacements, the company extended the battery warranty to unlimited years and unlimited mileage.[2] Carmakers do not hand out infinite warranties for fun. They hand them out when the alternative is worse.

If the script feels familiar, that is because Stellantis keeps reading from it. The company builds America's three best-selling plug-in hybrids: the Jeep Wrangler 4xe, the Jeep Grand Cherokee 4xe, and the Pacifica. All three have been recalled for battery fire risk, all three with the same humiliating instruction: park outside, because the thing in your garage might burn it down.[5]

If you own a 2020-2022 Pacifica PHEV built between August 2020 and May 2022, check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls today. Do not wait for the letter. Until the repair is done, park outside, away from structures and other vehicles, and stop charging the battery. The repair is free, and a battery fire in an attached garage can reach your house in minutes.

Fairness requires the other side, so here it is at full strength. Four fires in three years across 17,277 vans, with roughly 173 estimated to carry the defect, makes the risk to any single owner small. Chrysler opened its own investigation on April 30, 2026 and determined a defect existed by May 28, which is prompt by industry standards, and the unlimited warranty is a genuinely generous remedy. Nobody has died, and no house has burned. The park-outside guidance is maximally cautious, and maximal caution is cheap compared to a garage fire at 3 a.m.

Limitations: Chrysler's one-percent defect estimate is just that, an estimate, because the root cause is still unknown. Engineers recovered exactly one battery pack involved in a fire, and LG Energy Solution is still investigating its alternative assembly line. The fire counts come from Chrysler's own field reports; four fires could be all of them or the tip of a quieter pile. And nothing here measures crashworthiness: by fatality data, the Pacifica remains a far safer crash than the Grand Caravan it replaced. Fire risk and crash risk are different axes, and this recall moves only one of them.

A minivan's job is to keep your family safe while doing the most boring driving imaginable. It keeps acing the crash tests and failing the parked-in-the-driveway ones. Check the VIN. Park outside. Get the free fix. Then go back to ignoring your minivan, which is the highest compliment a minivan can earn.
