## Verified facts (3+ primary/primary-adjacent sources)
1. IIHS ratings page, 2026 Chevrolet Trailblazer (Test ID CEF2630, tested vehicle: 2026 Trailblazer LT 4-door 4WD). Rating applies to 2026 models built after February 2026. IIHS test note: "Beginning with 2026 models built after February 2026, improvements were made to the rear seat belt load limiters to improve rear occupant safety in moderate overlap front crashes." Overall evaluation: P (Poor). Structure and safety cage: G. Driver injury measures all G; driver restraints and dummy kinematics G. Rear passenger: head/neck G, chest M, thigh G; **rear passenger restraints and dummy kinematics: P**. (iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/chevrolet/trailblazer-4-door-suv/2026 — primary source.)
2. GM Authority, Oct 27, 2026: confirms the Poor overall moderate-overlap rating and the Feb-2026 load-limiter note; adds Trailblazer got Good in every portion of small overlap front and Good overall in the updated side test; headlights "acceptable"; front crash prevention vehicle-to-vehicle 2.0 "poor"; front crash prevention pedestrian "moderate"; child seat anchors "acceptable" (lower anchors too deep in the seat). Notes the Trailblazer earned IIHS Top Safety Pick+ five years ago (2021 model year, before the updated test existed). 2027 refresh incoming.
3. The Drive (Stephen Edelstein, ~Oct 23, 2026) + Motor1/allmotornews (Oct 27, 2026): the roundup context. Nissan Kicks initially scored Poor on the same test; Nissan revised rear seat belts on 2026 and 2027 Kicks built after June 2026; retest earned Good and a Top Safety Pick award — the cheapest contender fixed it with one running change. Redesigned 2026 Audi Q3: Poor in moderate overlap despite Audi modifying rear belts after May 2026 (rear lap belt slipped off the pelvis); Good in every other category. Redesigned 2026 Toyota RAV4: hybrid Acceptable, plug-in hybrid Marginal (heavier plug-in performed worse). VW Jetta (2027): Good in moderate overlap. IIHS added the rear-seat dummy to moderate overlap in 2022 and tightened scoring in 2024 to emphasize second-row protection.
4. Background (STORY_GUIDE key references): IIHS updated moderate overlap front test explainer (iihs.org/ratings parent topic page). The updated test puts a second dummy behind the driver representing a small woman or 12-year-old child; it measures whether the rear occupant submarines under the lap belt (restraints/dummy kinematics) and chest loading.
