# Research: Elantra Hybrid Fire Recall + Elantra FARS Death Profile

## News Hook
Hyundai recalled 54,337 Elantra Hybrid sedans (MY 2024-2026) in May 2026 (NHTSA recall 26V308) due to a MOSFET transistor in the Hybrid Power Control Unit (HPCU) that overheats under high electrical loads. Software didn't provide sufficient cooling. 4 incidents, 1 fire, 0 injuries. Fix: free software update.

- Root cause: HPCU software logic doesn't adequately manage cooling under high current loads
- Part numbers: 36600-2BBG0 (Blue trim), 36600-2BBG1 (SEL/Limited trims), supplied by Hyundai Mobis
- Hyundai first aware December 2024 via "Speak Up For Safety" report
- Multiple teardowns in July 2025 confirmed consistent MOSFET failure location
- Owner notification scheduled July 13, 2026

## FARS Data — Hyundai Elantra
- **Total deaths (2014-2023):** 2,407
- **Estimated fleet:** 1,400,000
- **Fatality rate:** 1.50 per 100M VMT (above average for sedans)
- **Drivers in fatal crashes:** 5,513
- **Impairment rate:** 18.6% any, 13.8% alcohol, 8.2% drug
  - Compare: sedan average is 20.4% any impaired
  - Elantra drivers are LESS likely to be impaired in fatal crashes than average sedan drivers
- **Model year death profile:**
  - 2013: **338 deaths** (massive spike — 2.5x the next highest year)
  - 2017: 199
  - 2016: 138
  - 2015: 138
  - 2014: 132
  - 2012: 128
  - 2010: 155
  - 2020: 95 (pandemic spike)
  - 2021: 17, 2022: 22, 2023: 20 (recent MY data limited by exposure)

## Original Analysis: The Elantra's Sober-Crash Problem
- At 18.6% impairment, the Elantra is 1.8 percentage points BELOW the sedan class average
- This means 81.4% of Elantra fatal-crash drivers tested clean
- For context: Corvette (26.2%), BMW 3 Series (22.0%), Camaro (23.0%), Mustang (21.8%)
- The Elantra's below-average impairment rate combined with above-average death rate (1.50 vs segment) suggests the vehicle's crashworthiness or the driving environments/demographics bear more of the blame than driver behavior
