### 2. Voice Coach — 9.2/10
Unmistakably Axle: "By the arithmetic," "one of those rare moments where the gear chart and the death chart agree with each other." Fragments used as punctuation, not crutches. No banned phrases, no em dashes, no AI cadence. The "weather report" line is the kind of thing Axle would actually say. Swapping bylines is inconceivable.

### 3. Ethics Reviewer — 9.0/10
No victim-blaming of walkers, bikers, or riders; the piece attributes their risk to speed limits, road design, and enforcement gaps. "Wear the helmet; the data is not subtle about this" is direct without moralizing. The DC +133% mention is a statistic, not an accusation. No trauma voyeurism.

### 4. Social/Shareability — 9.0/10
Headline is the share trigger: two true facts that shouldn't coexist. Pull stat (1,148) is quotable. "A national decline that contains a state increasing 133 percent is less a trend than a weather report" and "The roads got safer for everyone inside a steel cage; the people outside it never got the memo" are built for screenshots. Cyclist and motorcycle communities will circulate this on their own.

### 5. Legal Accuracy — 9.2/10
Every number traces to a named primary source with a working link (NHTSA CrashStats PDF, NSC/ISHN, TRIP/tripnet, Carscoops, Bike League). Author calculations are explicitly labeled as such with inputs shown in the disclaimer. One pre-panel correction applied: "lowest Q1 rate since 2014" → "matching the lowest since 2014" (Q1 2014 was also 0.99). No fabricated citations; TRIP figures linked to tripnet.org, not the secondary summary.

### 6. Research Rigor — 9.3/10
Original contribution is real arithmetic, not synthesis: pandemic-era excess (~15,686 vs 2019 baseline), vulnerable-user backslide erasing 8.3% of the post-2021 decline, motorcycle overrepresentation factor (~23x share, 36x rate). Limitations are a dedicated accounting (revision risk, missing Q1 2026 road-user breakdown, NSC/NHTSA scope mismatch, flat-baseline assumption). Counterargument stated at full strength: regression to the mean, 2025 still 3% above 2015. Verifiability: every claim hyperlinked inline.

### 7. Data Presentation — 8.8/10
Units and years accompany every figure; absolutes paired with percentages throughout. Pull stat carries its own context. Minor density tax in the middle third. The "0.99 matching the lowest since 2014" nuance is now precise.
