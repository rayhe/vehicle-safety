# Research: Nobody Writes About the Kia Optima. 611 People Died in It Anyway.

## Slug: optima-invisible-611-dead
## Journalist: Vin Wreckage
## Kicker: Existential Dread

## Core Data (FARS 2014-2023)

- **Total deaths:** 611
- **Death rate:** 0.58 per 100M VMT
- **Estimated fleet:** 1,050,000 (approximate from rate calc)
- **Crashes:** ~1,050 (implied)
- **Impairment:** 22.0% any, alcohol only data not available in summary
- **Class:** Sedan

## Model Year Distribution
- 2005: 5, 2006: 7, 2007: 17, 2008: 19, 2009: 17, 2010: 11
- 2011: 24, 2012: 41, 2013: 75, 2014: 36, 2015: 110, 2016: 58
- 2017: 31, 2018: 51, 2019: 62, 2020: 38

## Key Finding: MY 2015 Spike
- 110 deaths — 20% of all Optima deaths from a single model year
- Nearly matches Camaro MY 2015 (115 deaths)
- 4.6x the Corvette's deadliest single model year (24 deaths)
- Preceded by MY 2014 dip (36 deaths) — 3x spike year-over-year

## Comparisons
- Kia Optima peak MY (110) ≈ Chevy Camaro peak MY (115)
- Kia Optima peak MY (110) >> Corvette peak MY (24) by 4.6x
- Kia Optima peak MY (110) >> Challenger peak MY (69) by 1.6x
- Sedan class avg impairment: 21.0% → Optima at 22.0% (slightly above)
- Death rate 0.58 is moderate for sedans (Camry 2.03, Civic 2.25, but these have much larger fleets)

## Context
- 3rd generation Optima (2011-2015) was Kia's design breakthrough under Peter Schreyer
- MY 2015 was the final year of the 3rd gen before the 4th gen launched in 2016
- Rebranded as K5 in 2021 (MY 2021) — Optima nameplate discontinued
- Kia Boys theft epidemic (2021-2023) affected 2011-2021 models without engine immobilizers
- NHTSA recall campaigns for engine fires across multiple Kia models

## Angle
The Optima is the invisible sedan — never dangerous enough to investigate, never exciting enough to write about, never recalled for the thing that's actually killing people (crashes, not fires). Its MY 2015 killed as many people as a Camaro. Nobody noticed because Kia Optimas don't make headlines when they wrap around telephone poles.
