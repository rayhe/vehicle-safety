### 5. Legal Accuracy — 8.4
**Below gate.** One clear error and one imprecision. The error: "That left an 'Adjusted Award' of $119.5 million, a 9.4-to-1 ratio, just above the single-digit line the Supreme Court sketched." 9.4-to-1 is *inside* the single-digit range, not above it — *State Farm v. Campbell* blesses single-digit multipliers as more likely to comport with due process, and 9.4 is a single-digit multiplier. The sentence as written tells the reader the court landed outside the guidepost when it landed inside it. The imprecision: "Seven months later" for March 20 → October 9, 2025 is 6 months 19 days; defensible by rounding, but the article elsewhere is precise to the day, so a rounded month count stands out. Everything else checks: case number, filing date, award figures, remittitur language ("did not comport with the Company's constitutional rights" is a direct quote from the 8-K), FMVSS 223/224 effective date, Missouri no-statute-of-repose and evidence-exclusion claims (both sourced to the defense-side analysis). Fix the 9.4x sentence; the rest holds.

### 7. Data Presentation — 8.8
