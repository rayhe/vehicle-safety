### 3. FARS Cross-Tabulation (original contribution)
**Ford deaths by design era:**
- Pre-2013 model years: 27,737 deaths (80.4%)
- 2013-2020 model years: 6,539 deaths (19.0%) ← the "problem era"
- 2021+ model years: 217 deaths (0.6%)
- TOTAL: 34,493 Ford deaths (FARS 2014-2023)

**Top Ford 2013-2020 models by FARS deaths:**
1. F-150: 1,543 deaths
2. Fusion: 964 deaths
3. Escape: 849 deaths
4. Focus: 819 deaths
5. Mustang: 536 deaths
6. Explorer: 419 deaths
7. Fiesta: 292 deaths
8. Taurus: 237 deaths

**Key novel finding:** Ford's 2013-2020 era accounts for 19.0% of all Ford FARS deaths vs 23.8% for non-Ford manufacturers in the same era. Ford's problematic era is actually LESS deadly proportionally — but that's because the pre-2013 fleet (80.4% of deaths) is where the real carnage lives, and Ford's legacy fleet is massive.

**The real story:** Ford's quality chief publicly admitted to a 7-year design deficiency window. FARS data attaches 6,539 deaths to that window. The question isn't whether Ford knows the problem — they just told us. The question is whether recalls can fix design-era defects, or whether those vehicles just need to age out of the fleet.
