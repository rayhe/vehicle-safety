# Research: 2025 Traffic Deaths Down, Pedalcyclists Up

**Kicker:** By The Numbers
**Date:** 2026-08-30

## Story Angle

NHTSA early estimates show 2025 traffic deaths dropped to 36,640, down 6.7% from 39,254 in 2024, with fatality rate 1.10 per 100M VMT (second lowest ever, behind 1.08 in 2014). It's the 15th consecutive quarterly decline since Q2 2022. VMT up 0.9% (29.8B miles). 39 states + DC + PR saw declines.

Across 20+ subcategories, every single one is down except one: pedalcyclists (bicyclists) up 4%. Even pedestrians down 8%, motorcyclists down 8%, large truck crashes down 7%, unrestrained down 13%, speeding-related down 11%, rollover down 11%, ejected down 13%.

The only group getting worse rides a bike.

Original contribution: Cross-tabulate NHTSA 2025 subcategory data against FARS 2014-2023 trends to show this is not a blip - cyclist deaths have been climbing while overall deaths fall, creating a divergence. Calculate the implied absolute numbers and rate divergence.

## Kill Test

Is this genuinely newsworthy? Yes - first time NHTSA has reported 15 consecutive quarterly declines, second-lowest rate ever, total back to pre-pandemic 2019 levels. Novel angle: while everything improves, cyclists are the outlier. No existing Crash Report story covers this specific 2025 divergence (existing stories: ebike-fars-data-black-hole, ebike-zero-to-97-deaths, senior-zero-improvement-2025, rural-urban-safety-divide-2025, but none combine the full subcategory sweep).

## Data Points

- 2025 est: 36,640 deaths (↓6.7% vs 39,254 in 2024)
- Rate 2025: 1.10 per 100M VMT (↓ from 1.19, second lowest ever behind 1.08 in 2014)
- VMT 2025: +29.8B miles (+0.9%)
- Q1 2025: 8,055 deaths (↓6.3%), rate 1.05, 12th consecutive quarterly decline
- H1 2025: 17,140 deaths (↓8.2% from 18,680), rate 1.06, lowest mid-year since 2014
- 39 states + DC + PR down in 2025
- Subcategories: 20 down, 1 up (pedalcyclists +4%), 1 flat (65+ 0%)
- Pedestrians ↓8%, motorcyclists ↓8%, pedalcyclists ↑4% - divergence within vulnerable road users
- Unrestrained ↓13%, ejected ↓13%, rollover ↓11% - occupant protection working
- Speeding-related ↓11%, roadway departure ↓9% - behavioral improvements?

## Limitations

- 2025 data is early estimate, not Final File or ARF - will be revised early 2027
- Subcategory data from ROSA abstract, not full report tables - need to cite abstract clearly
- FARS only captures fatal crashes, not injuries - cyclist injuries likely higher
- Pedalcyclist definition includes e-bikes? NHTSA definition may be evolving
- VMT estimates preliminary from FHWA
- Correlation not causation for decline reasons

## Counterargument

Best case against thesis: 4% increase could be noise in small sample (cyclist deaths ~1k vs 36k total, so 4% = ~40 deaths, within year-to-year variance). Also 2025 is still estimate, could revise down. And overall decline could be due to improved vehicle safety (newer vehicles <10yr ↓12%) not behavioral change, so cyclists left out because they don't benefit from car safety tech.

We will state this explicitly.

## Actionable Insights

- If you ride: 2025 is worst year relative to other road users - protected bike infrastructure matters more than ever
- Check local bike lane investments - IIHS vehicle-to-vehicle and pedestrian front crash prevention doesn't help cyclists in same way
- For drivers: newer vehicles <10yr down 12% - ESC, AEB, side airbags working
- For policymakers: cyclist deaths diverging from pedestrian deaths (↓8% vs ↑4%) suggests bike-specific countermeasures needed, not just shared vulnerable-user programs
