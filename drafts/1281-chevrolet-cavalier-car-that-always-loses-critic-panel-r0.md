## 1. General Editor — 9.0
Lede does the job in two sentences: every fatal crash has a loser, and the Cavalier loses 86% of the time. ## 3. Ethics Reviewer — 9.0
The wealth-gap framing is handled with care: it names the structural trap (the cheapest cars protect worst) without mocking the buyers. The demographic counterargument is presented fairly rather than straw-manned. No victim-blaming, no naming of private individuals, no gore. The "loser" framing targets a machine, not people.

## 4. Social Shareability — 8.5
Headline plus 86% is a ready-made share card, and the Ram contrast gives commenters something to argue about. Deduction: the Cavalier is a discontinued car, so the "check your car" urgency is weaker than a recall story; the used-car-shopping advice partially compensates. Solid but not the site's most viral shape.

## 5. Legal Accuracy — 8.5

Not a false claim, but one sentence could be tighter.
