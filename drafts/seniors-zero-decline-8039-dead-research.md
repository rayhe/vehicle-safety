# Research: The 65+ Traffic Death Plateau

## Angle
NHTSA's July 2026 sub-category report (DOT HS 813 829) reveals that Americans aged 65+ had **zero decline** in traffic deaths in 2025 — 8,039 deaths vs. 8,060 in 2024. Every other age group improved: the 25-34 bracket dropped 13%, children under 15 dropped 12%. Overall deaths fell 6.7% to 36,640. The elderly are being left behind by the safety revolution.

## Primary Sources
1. **NHTSA CrashStats DOT HS 813 829** (July 2026) — "Early Estimates of Motor Vehicle Traffic Fatalities and Fatality Rate by Sub-Categories in 2025"
   - URL: https://crashstats.nhtsa.dot.gov/Api/Public/ViewPublication/813829
2. **NHTSA FARS database** (2014-2023 bulk data) — vehicle-level death rates, impairment, lethality ratios
3. **NHTSA Q1 2026 estimate** — 7,770 deaths, -4.3%, fatality rate 0.99/100M VMT
4. **Carscoops July 2026** — cyclist deaths 40-year high context

## Key Data Points

### Age Group Decline Rates (2024 → 2025)
| Age Group | 2024 Deaths | 2025 Deaths | Change |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|--------|
| <15       | 1,037       | 918         | -12%   |
| 15-24     | 6,658       | 6,113       | -8%    |
| 25-34     | 6,956       | 6,065       | -13%   |
| 35-44     | 6,283       | 5,715       | -9%    |
| 45-54     | 4,988       | 4,672       | -6%    |
| 55-64     | 5,272       | 5,117       | -3%    |
| **65+**   | **8,060**   | **8,039**   | **-0%**|
| TOTAL     | 39,254      | 36,640      | -6.7%  |

### 65+ Share of Deaths Rising
- 2024: ~20% of monthly deaths
- 2025: up to 24% in Nov/Dec 2025
- Full year: 8,039 / 36,640 = 21.9%
- 2024 full year: 8,060 / 39,254 = 20.5%

### FARS Cross-Tab: "Retiree Vehicle" Lethality
Vehicles commonly associated with older demographics have extremely high crash lethality ratios (deaths per FARS crash):
- Saturn S Series: 0.924 (92.4% of FARS crashes kill the occupant)
- Buick Park Avenue: 0.882
- Buick LeSabre: 0.823
- Lincoln Town Car: 0.805
- Ford Five Hundred: 0.811
- Mercury Grand Marquis: included in Taurus family
Compare to modern crossovers:
- Ram 2500: 0.205
- Toyota RAV4: 0.428
- Honda Pilot: 0.449
- Chevrolet Traverse: 0.393

### Original Finding
The vehicles with the LOWEST death rates (modern crossovers/SUVs) have lethality ratios around 0.35-0.45. The vehicles with the HIGHEST lethality ratios (0.80+) are disproportionately older sedans favored by elderly buyers. The 65+ plateau isn't just about fragile bodies — it's about which vehicles that population drives.

### Limitations
- The 65+ population is growing (Baby Boomers aging in), which increases the denominator. A flat death count with a growing population could mean the per-capita rate IS improving — but NHTSA doesn't break out population-adjusted rates by age in this report.
- FARS data is 2014-2023; 2025 sub-category data uses FARS methodology but more recent projections.
- Vehicle choice is a proxy — we don't have direct data linking driver age to FARS crashes by vehicle model.

### Counterargument (Required)
The strongest counterargument: the Baby Boomer generation is enormous. Census data shows the 65+ population grew from ~52M (2018) to ~60M+ (2025). If 8,039 deaths from a larger population represents a lower RATE, then the 0% headline is misleading — it's actually improvement masked by demographics. This is a real possibility, and the article must state it honestly. However: even granting demographic growth, the 65+ group's improvement (if any) significantly lags every other age cohort.
