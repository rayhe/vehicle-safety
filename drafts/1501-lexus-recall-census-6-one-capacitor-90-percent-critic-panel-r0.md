Structure holds: lede with the 90 percent concentration, method paragraph, the capacitor engineering deep dive, the TX section, the RZ pattern, the GX550 label quirk, comparison tables, counterargument, limitations, actionable section. The H1 is a complete argument in one line and the best headline in the census series since #1498. Two knocks: the two data tables sit back-to-back mid-article and read as a speed bump between the comparison paragraph and the counterargument; and the TX section carries four recalls plus the haystack/needle contrast, making it the longest single block in the piece. Neither is fatal. The OTA-flag paragraph earns its place as a series correction, not a tangent.

## 3. Ethics Reviewer — 9.0/10

No victims, no injuries to sensationalize, and the article says so plainly rather than mining the zeros for drama. The counterargument is granted at full strength (the TX's four appearances are partly a platform artifact; the camera trigger is narrow; the brand's independent record is genuinely small). The limitations paragraph discloses every place the count flatters or understates, including the two excluded 2025 filings by number. No self-congratulation. The people materially affected are TX/RX/RZ owners, and the article tells each group exactly what to check and what the fix costs them (nothing).

## 4. Social / Shareability — 8.8/10
