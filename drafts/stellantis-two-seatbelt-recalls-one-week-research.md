# Research: Stellantis Two Seat Belt Recalls in One Week

## Hook
In six days (Aug 4-10, 2026), Stellantis issued TWO separate seat belt recalls covering 1.32 million vehicles — and the two recalls have completely different root causes. One is a factory assembly error. The other is a supplier design flaw.

## Primary Sources

### Recall 1: Ram 1500 (Aug 4, 2026)
- **NHTSA Campaign:** 26V-67D
- **Vehicles:** 1,271,294 model-year 2019-2026 Ram 1500
- **Defect:** Second-row seat belt buckle anchors improperly installed (not bolted to body structure)
- **Root cause:** Vehicle assembly error — bolts not properly attached during factory production
- **Defect rate estimate:** ~0.1% of recalled vehicles
- **Production dates:** Feb 17, 2018 - Apr 27, 2026 (8+ years of production)
- **Source:** NHTSA recall notice, USA Today (Aug 4), Fox Business (Aug 10)
- **Remedy:** Dealers inspect + re-attach buckle anchors to body structure

### Recall 2: Dodge Hornet + Alfa Romeo Tonale (Aug 10, 2026)
- **NHTSA Campaign:** 26V510 / FCA recall 84D
- **Vehicles:** 48,777 (40,386 Dodge Hornet 2023-2025 + 8,391 Alfa Romeo Tonale 2023-2026)
- **Defect:** Rear outboard seat belts may become twisted, preventing retraction
- **Root cause:** Design weakness in seat belt retractor — Autoliv ASP, Inc. (supplier)
- **Warranty claims:** 484 related claims
- **No injuries or accidents reported
- **Stellantis Europe began investigation May 2026
- **Remedy:** Replace rear outboard seat belt retractors, notifications begin Sept 24
- **Source:** USA Today (Aug 10), NHTSA recall database

## Context: Stellantis + Seat Belts in 2026

### What's NEW here
The Hornet/Tonale recall drops Aug 10 — AFTER the article about the Ram/Ford/Kia cluster. This is a SECOND Stellantis seat belt recall in the same calendar week. Different platforms, different plants, different suppliers, different failure modes:

| | Ram 1500 | Hornet/Tonale |
|---|---|---|
| Root cause | Assembly error | Supplier design flaw |
| Component | Buckle anchor bolts | Retractor mechanism |
| Supplier | In-house assembly | Autoliv ASP |
| Affected row | Second row | Rear outboard |
| Fleet size | 1.27M | 48K |
| Platform | DS (Ram pickup) | STLA Medium (shared w/ Jeep Compass) |
| Production span | 8+ years | 3 years |

## Novel Finding
The seat belt is the single most important safety device in any vehicle — responsible for an estimated 14,955 lives saved per year (NHTSA, 2017). When the belt fails because the anchor wasn't bolted down or the retractor can't hold tension, the occupant becomes effectively unbelted in a crash. One manufacturer produced two distinct ways this can happen and discovered them within the same calendar week.
