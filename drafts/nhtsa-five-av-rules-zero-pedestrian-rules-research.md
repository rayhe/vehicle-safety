# Research: NHTSA Issued Five Rules to Help Robots Drive. The Pedestrian Rule Still Isn't Done.

## Angle
Regulatory priority gap: NHTSA has fast-tracked five FMVSS modernization rulemakings for autonomous vehicles in 2026, while the Congressionally mandated pedestrian head protection standard (proposed September 2024) has not been finalized. On June 26, 2026 — the same day NHTSA proposed removing brake pedals for ADS-equipped vehicles — the agency withdrew AV STEP, its own voluntary transparency and reporting framework for AV developers.

## Kill test
Yes — newsworthy. Novel juxtaposition of regulatory speed for industry vs. stall on pedestrian protection. Data-driven, verifiable through Federal Register.

## Primary Sources

### 1. NHTSA FMVSS 135 Brake Pedal NPRM (June 26, 2026)
- Source: NHTSA press release "Trump's Transportation Department Launches Commonsense Updates to Brake Pedal Requirements for AVs"
- URL: https://www.nhtsa.gov/press-releases/fmvss-updates-brake-pedal-requirements
- Key fact: Removes mandate for manual brake pedals in vehicles designed exclusively for ADS. Comment period closed July 27, 2026.
- NHTSA Administrator Jonathan Morrison quote: "We are at the cusp of the greatest technological revolution in vehicle technology since the innovation of the Model T."

### 2. AV STEP Withdrawal (June 26, 2026)
- Source: Rates Chaser / Federal Register
- On the same day as FMVSS 135 NPRM, NHTSA formally withdrew AV STEP (proposed January 15, 2026). AV STEP would have required AV manufacturers to disclose safety data in exchange for FMVSS exemptions.
- Result: Brake pedal barrier removed + transparency framework removed = less regulation + less oversight simultaneously.

### 3. Five AV-Accommodation Rulemakings in 2026
From NHTSA's own press release listing:
- FMVSS 102: Transmission shift (March 16, 2026)
- FMVSS 103/104: Windshield defrost/wipe (March 16, 2026)  
- FMVSS 110: Tire placards (April 1, 2026)
- FMVSS 135: Braking systems (June 26, 2026)
- Plus announced intent: FMVSS 101 (controls), 108 (lighting), 111 (mirrors), 126 (ESC), 201/208 (sun visors/labels)

### 4. Pedestrian Head Protection Standard — FMVSS 228 (Proposed September 19, 2024)
- Source: NHTSA NPRM, Federal Register
- URL: https://www.NHTSA.GOV/press-releases/nhtsa-proposes-new-vehicle-safety-standard-protect-pedestrians
- Mandated by Bipartisan Infrastructure Law (2021)
- Compliance cost: ~$3 per vehicle
- Estimated lives saved: 67 per year
- Pedestrian fatalities: 7,522 in 2022 (up 57% from 4,779 in 2013)
- Comment period extended to December 18, 2024. No final rule published as of August 2026.
- Status: Silent for 8 months since comment period closed.

### 5. GHSA Pedestrian Fatality Data (2025 Report)
- Source: GHSA Spotlight report with 2024 national data
- URL: https://www.ghsa.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/Pedestrian%20Traffic%20Fatalities%20by%20State%202025%20Data.pdf
- Key stat: Proportion of pedestrian crashes resulting in fatality doubled from 4% (2015) to 10.1% (2024)
- Pedestrian fatalities remain 14% above 2019 pre-pandemic levels despite 2023 slight decline

### 6. Zoox Exemption (July 2026)
- Source: NHTSA press release "Cutting Red Tape to Safely Fast Track Automated Vehicle"
- URL: https://www.nhtsa.gov/press-releases/cutting-red-tape-safely-fast-track-automated-vehicle
- NHTSA granted Zoox the first full-production AV exemption
