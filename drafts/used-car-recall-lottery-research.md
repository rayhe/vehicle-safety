# Research: The Used Car Recall Lottery

## Angle
When you buy a used car, there's roughly a 1-in-4 chance it comes with an open, unrepaired safety recall. No federal law requires used car dealers to fix recalls before selling. The used car buyer inherits the manufacturer's known defect.

## Self-Critique Gate
**Proposed angle:** The used car market has a hidden safety defect — literally. One in four vehicles has an open recall, no federal law requires dealers to fix them before sale, and the recall completion rate just hit a near-record low of 50.3%.

**Challenge:** Is this genuinely surprising after 700+ articles? Yes — this site has covered recalls extensively but always from the fleet/manufacturer/NHTSA perspective. This is the BUYER's perspective. Nobody buying a $31K used car expects to inherit a known seatbelt, airbag, or fire defect that the manufacturer already filed paperwork about.

**Verdict:** Proceed. Consumer angle is fresh for this site.

## Primary Sources (3+ required)

### 1. NHTSA 2025 Annual Recalls Report (published March 2026)
- 670 total recall campaigns (447 mandated, 223 voluntary)
- Covering 28 million vehicles
- Software/electronics: 119 campaigns, 8.19M vehicles
- Powertrain: 87 campaigns, 7.99M vehicles
- Source: NHTSA official data, cited in Easy Lemon / EIN Presswire analysis (July 7, 2026)

### 2. NHTSA / Recall Masters Recall Completion Rates
- 50.3% of recalled vehicles repaired by February 2026
- Estimated 14 million recalled vehicles still unrepaired on US roads
- NHTSA attributes suppressed completion to parts shortages and supply-chain delays
- Carfax estimate: 1 in 4 vehicles on US roads has at least one open recall (~72 million vehicles)
- Source: NHTSA Recall Completion Rates Report; Recall Masters 9th Annual State of Recalls Report

### 3. FARS Data (2014-2023)
- Vehicles with highest death rates are predominantly older models
- Class death rates: Sports Car 2.69, Sedan 1.39, Pickup 0.89, Van 0.65, SUV 0.55 (per 10M VMT)
- Older vehicles in FARS = overlap with used car market
- Models with 50+ deaths: 337 tracked

### 4. Edmunds Q1 2026 Used-Car Market
- Average 3-year-old used vehicle: $31,548 (second-highest Q1 on record)
- Source: Edmunds Q1 2026 used-car report

### 5. FTC Used Car Rule / Federal Law Gap
- FTC Buyers Guide required on all used vehicles at dealerships
- NO federal requirement for dealers to disclose or repair open recalls before sale
- New car franchise dealers cannot sell NEW vehicles with open recalls (per manufacturer agreements)
- Used car dealers (CarMax, Carvana, independent lots) have NO such restriction
- Some states have introduced legislation; no comprehensive federal mandate exists

### 6. S&P Global Mobility / Tariff Impact
- $22.4B in added aftermarket parts duties
- 47% of US parts imports from Mexico
- 5-6% revenue impact on repair operations
- Parts shortages extend time-to-fix for recalls

## Limitations
- Carfax's "1 in 4" is an estimate; methodology not fully public
- FARS data covers fatal crashes only, not all crashes
- Recall completion rates vary widely by manufacturer and recall type
- Not all open recalls pose equal risk (software vs structural vs fire)
