# Research: Dodge Hornet / Alfa Romeo Tonale — More Recalls Than Sales

## Angle
Stellantis built two compact crossovers on the same Italian platform (Pomigliano d'Arco). Together, they've been recalled for more individual defects than many vehicles accumulate in a decade — and they've barely sold 35,000 combined units in the US. The latest: 48,777 vehicles recalled August 8, 2026 for rear seatbelt retractors that can twist and fail to retract. This is their 5th+ NHTSA recall. Production of the Hornet is halted due to tariffs. The platform is dying, but the recalls keep coming.

## Key Data Points

### Recall History (NHTSA)
1. **Brake pedal collapse** (Oct 2024) — PHEV models, brake pedal arm could disengage during braking. 21,069 US vehicles. Recall B7B.
2. **Brake pedal expansion** (Dec 2025) — 5,974 more PHEVs added. Same brake pedal arm defect.
3. **Reverse camera failure** (2025) — Camera display goes blank when backing up. Recall 48C, NHTSA 25E038000.
4. **Brake pedal 3rd round** (Jan 2026) — Further expansion of brake recall.
5. **Rear seatbelt retractor twist** (Aug 8, 2026) — 48,777 vehicles. 2023-2025 Dodge Hornet and 2023-2026 Alfa Romeo Tonale. Rear seatbelt retractors may twist, preventing proper retraction. Plus Mopar replacement seatbelt assemblies also recalled.

### Sales Data
- 2024 Hornet US sales: 20,559
- 2024 Tonale US sales: 3,383
- H1 2025 Tonale: 1,156 (down 28% YoY)
- H1 2025 Hornet: sales declining sharply, production halted July 2025 (tariff impact — built in Italy)
- Combined production: 82,300 (2023 peak) → 36,980 (2024) → ~20,000 pace (2025)

### Platform Details
- Both built at Stellantis Pomigliano d'Arco plant, Naples, Italy
- Shared STLA Medium platform (formerly CMP)
- Hornet starting at $31,585 (GT), Tonale at $36,495 (AWD)
- Same assembly line, different badges, same defects

### The Math
- ~48,777 vehicles recalled for seatbelts alone = nearly ALL Hornets/Tonales ever sold in US
- Total recall touchpoints across all campaigns may exceed total US sales
- 5+ recalls on a platform that's sold ~35K combined US units
