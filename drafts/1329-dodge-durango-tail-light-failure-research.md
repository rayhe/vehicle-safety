# Research Notes — #1329: Dodge Durango Tail Light Recall

**Slug:** 1329-dodge-durango-tail-light-failure
**Journalist:** Clara Rollover (consumer safety advocate; last byline #1320)
**Kicker:** The Gap

## The story

Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling 13,330 model-year 2026 Dodge Durango vehicles because
the terminals in the tail light wiring harness may be damaged, causing the tail lights to
fail. The vehicles fail to comply with FMVSS 108 (Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated
Equipment). NHTSA campaign 26V701000, filed October 22, 2026.

Remedy: dealers inspect and replace the tail light assemblies free of charge.
Chrysler's number A2D. Chrysler customer service: 800-853-1403.
Owner notification letters expected December 3, 2026.
VINs became searchable on NHTSA.gov on October 29, 2026 (yesterday).

## The angle (kill test: PASS)

Tail light failure is the one lighting defect the driver can never detect. Headlights die
and you see darkness; brake lights die and the driver behind you sees nothing. There is no
dashboard tell, no visible symptom from the driver's seat. The first symptom is someone
rear-ending you at night, and the crash report will blame the follower. The defect rides
in front; the blame lands behind. That asymmetry is the whole article.

Secondary hook: the information gap. VINs went searchable October 29; mailed letters do
not go out until December 3. Five weeks where owners can check now or drive blind (from
behind) until the mail arrives. Actionable, urgent, concrete.

## Actionable takeaway (hard gate)

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls TODAY — do not wait for the December 3 letter. If
you own a 2026 Durango, have someone stand behind the car while you hit the lights, or
back up to a storefront window at night and look at the reflection. The dealer fix is
free. Recall A2D, Chrysler 800-853-1403.
