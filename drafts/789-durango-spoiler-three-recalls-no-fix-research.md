# Article #789 Research — Durango Spoiler Triple Recall

## Angle
Dodge Durango rear spoiler recalled for the THIRD time (2021 → 2023 → 2026). Each recall covers more vehicles. They STILL don't have a fix. The pattern mirrors Stellantis's broader quality crisis.

## Key Data Points

### Recall History
- **2021 recall (Y73):** 503 vehicles
- **2023 recall (20A):** 18,163 vehicles  
- **2026 recall (61D / NHTSA 26V528000):** 74,578 vehicles
- Population grew **148x** from first to third recall

### 2026 Recall Details
- Affected: 2021-2023 MY Dodge Durango
- Build dates: July 13, 2020 – March 28, 2023
- NHTSA Campaign: 26V528000
- Filed: August 13, 2026; dealer notification August 20
- Root cause: "over-flush rear spoiler" — spoiler sits too high, contacts roof edge when liftgate cycles, loosens over time, detaches
- 161 total field reports as of July 29, 2026
  - 116 warranty claims
  - 8 customer assistance records
  - 37 additional service records
  - Reports span May 10, 2022 – June 30, 2026
- Estimated affected: less than 1% (per Stellantis)
- Crashes/injuries: 0 reported
- **Remedy: "under development"** — recalled without a fix
- Previous fixes: dealers inspected and replaced spoilers. They kept detaching.
- Vehicles built after March 2023 no longer have the over-flush condition

### Stellantis Context
- Backup camera recalled 7 times (story #771 in queue)
- 2.6 recalls per vehicle sold in 2024 (existing story)
- Two seatbelt recalls in one week (existing story)
