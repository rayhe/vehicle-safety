# Research: 1252-do-not-drive-club-porsche-subaru

## Story angle
In the same week (VINs searchable on NHTSA.gov October 20, 2026), two automakers issued
DO NOT DRIVE warnings: Porsche for 548 center-lock supercars whose wheels could detach,
and Subaru for 8 Crosstreks/Foresters whose front ball joint can separate from the
control arm. 548 vs 8 is a 68.5-to-1 ratio, and both failures trace to fasteners.
The story: what actually earns the auto industry's scariest warning, and how two
completely different defects arrive at the same sentence: "do not drive."

## Kill test

- Data-backed: yes. Population figures, model counts, campaign mechanics all sourced.
