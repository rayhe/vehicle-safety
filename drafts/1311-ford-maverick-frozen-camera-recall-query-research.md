# Research: #1311 — Ford Maverick Frozen Camera Recall Query (RQ26004)

## Story slug
`1311-ford-maverick-frozen-camera-recall-query`

## News peg
NHTSA's Office of Defects Investigation opened **Recall Query RQ26004 on October 23, 2026** (5 days ago), covering ~**273,301** 2022-2024 Ford Maverick and Maverick Hybrid trucks. The question under investigation: did Ford's 2024 recall remedy actually fix the frozen rearview camera images?

## Kill test
- Genuinely newsworthy: freshest NHTSA action on the open-investigations list (Oct 23, 2026).
- Novel angle: a *frozen* camera is worse than a blank one. A blank screen is an obvious failure; a frozen frame is a lie. The driver backs up confident the path is clear while the screen shows a picture of yesterday.
- Distinct from queue coverage: the recall-query genre was covered 5x (all Sierra grille RQ26003). This is a different maker, different component, different failure mode. Jeep coil-spring triple-recall covered 6x but that's suspension, not this.
- Data: 273,301 vs 144,516 (query population 1.89x the original recall), 5 post-remedy VOQs, ODI verified complete failures, 2 crashes in the original campaign.

## Facts (all sourced, no fabrication)

1. **RQ26004 opened Oct 23, 2026.** Component: Back Over Prevention, Sensing System, Camera. Summary: "Frozen Rear-view Camera Image." (OEMDTC investigations list)
2. **Original recall: NHTSA 24V684000 (Sept 2024), Ford 24S59.** 144,516 2022-2024 Mavericks with Connected Touch Radio (CTR). Memory leak in CTR software → frozen rear camera display in Reverse. At recall time Ford had no fix; the remedy (ACM software update, version G2.2) arrived March 2025. (Ford.com recall page; Ford Authority; Consumer Reports)
3. **Five VOQs allege frozen images AFTER the remedy was performed.** ODI verified several complete failures. (Autoblog, Oct 27, 2026; Ford Authority, Oct 26, 2026)
4. **Real complaint example:** NHTSA ID 11662294 — 2024 Maverick, Schoharie NY, recall repair performed at dealer, camera froze again weeks later at ~8,000 miles. No warning light. Not re-diagnosed. (oemdtc.com)
5. **Original campaign crash record:** Ford aware of 2 crashes, no injuries. New query lists no crashes. (Consumer Reports; Autoblog)
6. **The failure is silent.** The recall complaint notes "no warning light illuminated." The driver has no indication the image is stale.
7. **Context — Ford's camera recall stack (2024-2026):** 25V-315 (1,075,299 vehicles, SYNC 4 APIM software bug → camera lag/freeze/blackout, May 2025); 25S72 (short ignition cycles → freeze/last-image display); 25V-270, 25V-273 (hardware); 20V-575 (~620K, 2020). Plus 26S73 (Transit Connect camera fix that itself failed, Oct 2026). (recallbrief.com; Ford Authority)
8. **FMVSS 111** requires rear visibility when in reverse; a frozen image satisfies the letter while defeating the purpose.
9. **Recall queries** ask whether a manufacturer's recall worked — whether the remedy fixed the defect and whether the right vehicles were covered (AutoWire's RQ26003 framing applies identically).

## Actionable takeaways
- 2022-2024 Maverick (Connected Touch Radio, no SYNC): check VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls; confirm the 24S59/24V684 remedy was actually performed.
- The failure is silent (no warning light) — treat a suspiciously static image as a fault, use mirrors.
- Recall queries are the canary: if RQ26004 escalates, a second recall could follow. Owners who already visited a dealer once may have to go back.
