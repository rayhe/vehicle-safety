## 4. Social Shareability — 8.5
Headline is the share: "85 MPH" plus "You Still Own the Crash" is a complete argument in

## 5. Legal Accuracy — 9.0
SAE Level 2 vs Level 3 distinctions are correct and sourced to the MBUSA release and the
California DMV permit. The liability framing is accurate: Level 2 keeps liability with the
driver; Mercedes accepts responsibility while Drive Pilot is engaged. The Drive Pilot
discontinuation claim is explicitly attributed to a trade-press report, not stated as a
Mercedes announcement. California 65 mph maximum is correctly stated as applying to most
highways. No legal errors found.

## 6. Research Rigor — 9.0
Original contribution is real and threefold: the takeover-distance math (124.7 ft/s, ~250 ft

the driver is), and the ceiling-vs-limit observation. Limitations are stated in their own
paragraph, including that no crash data exists yet and that IIHS tested Volvo/Tesla, not
Mercedes. Counterargument is stated at full strength before being answered. Seven sources,
all linked, all verified live in this run. Methodology for derived numbers is shown inline.
