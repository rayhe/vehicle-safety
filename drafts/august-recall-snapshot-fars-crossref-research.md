## Key Recalls (July-August 2026)

### 1. Ram 1500 Seatbelt Anchor — 1,271,294 vehicles
- **Recall 67D**, FCA US, Aug 2026
- **Defect**: Second-row seat belt buckle anchor may not be properly attached to body structure
- **Affected**: 2019-2026 Ram 1500 (plus 245K international)
- **Source**: NHTSA via USA Today (Aug 4, 2026), Reuters (Jul 31, 2026)
- **FARS data**: Ram 1500: 714 deaths, 0.13 rate per 100M VMT, fleet 4.2M
  - Also Dodge RAM (older): 4,407 deaths, 0.78 rate
  - Combined Ram truck deaths: 5,121

### 2. Jeep Wrangler/Gladiator Power Steering Fire — 1,076,999 vehicles
- **Recall 26V363**, Stellantis, Jun 9, 2026
- **Defect**: Power steering pump wiring may overheat and cause fire, EVEN WHEN PARKED
- **Affected**: 2021-2025 Jeep Wrangler and Gladiator
- **Park outside advisory**: Yes
- **Source**: Reuters (Jun 9, 2026)
- **FARS data**: Jeep Wrangler: 1,842 deaths, 0.84 rate, fleet 1.75M
  - 51 reported fires, 1 injury

### 3. Ford Focus/Fiesta/EcoSport Timing Belt — 135,000 vehicles
- **NHTSA Engineering Analysis upgrade**, Aug 3, 2026
- **Defect**: 1.0L EcoBoost timing belt degrades, clogs oil pump, causes loss of power or engine seizure
- **Affected**: 2014-2021 models with 1.0L engine
- **Key stat**: Average failure at 70,000 miles; 98% fail before 150K maintenance interval
- **355 incidents reported**; Ford discontinued all 3 models; "no immediate comment"
- **Source**: Reuters (Aug 3, 2026)
- **FARS data**: 
  - Ford Focus: 3,046 deaths, 2.52 rate, fleet 1.05M
  - Ford Fiesta: 513 deaths, 1.02 rate, fleet 437K
  - Ford EcoSport: 86 deaths, 0.14 rate, fleet 481K
  - Combined: 3,645 deaths

### 4. Ford F-250/F-150 Rearview Camera — 47,587 vehicles
- Aug 4, 2026
- **Defect**: Software causes rearview camera image to be covered by infotainment home screen menu when in reverse
- **Affected**: Certain 2023-26 F-250 Super Duty + 2021-23 F-150 with 8" SYNC screens
- **Source**: Courier-Journal (Aug 4, 2026)
- **FARS data**: 
  - Ford F-250: 909 deaths, rate unavailable in main dataset (but see model year data: 2008 spike at 71)
  - Ford F-150: 9,194 deaths, 1.04 rate, fleet 6.56M

### 5. Subaru Forester Moonroof — 69,663 vehicles
- May 28, 2026
- **Defect**: Moonroof glass not properly bonded, can detach while driving
- **Affected**: 2026 Subaru Forester and Forester Hybrid
- **Source**: USA Today (Jun 4, 2026)
- **FARS data**: Subaru Forester: 396 deaths, 0.26 rate, fleet 1.225M

## Aggregate Numbers
- **Total vehicles recalled (this window)**: ~2.6 million
- **Total FARS deaths across recalled models**: 15,000+ (combined all models)
- **Most dangerous recalled model (by rate)**: Ford Focus at 2.52 per 100M VMT
- **Biggest recall population**: Ram 1500 at 1.27M
- **Most alarming defect**: Wrangler fire (can ignite when parked and off)
