# Research: #1129 — Nova Bus Bike Rack Recall of a Recall (26V624000)

## The facts
- **NHTSA Campaign:** 26V624000, issued week of Oct 5, 2026.
- **Manufacturer:** Nova Bus (US) Inc. (Volvo Group subsidiary), recall number **CR5936**.
- **Vehicles:** certain 2022-2023 LFS transit buses. **18 units** potentially affected.
- **Defect:** mounting screws for the front bike rack may be too short; short screws strip the threads, and a stripped mount lets the bike rack detach from the bus. A detached rack becomes a road hazard.
- **Recall of a recall:** 26V624000 **supersedes NHTSA Recall 24V873**. Vehicles repaired under the previous recall must be brought back in for the new repair. The first fix failed at the level of a screw: replacement screws (or the original spec) were too short, so the threads stripped.
- **Remedy:** dealers inspect and re-secure; owner notification letters expected **November 2, 2026**.
- **Contact:** Nova Bus Customer Service 1-800-350-6682 (Mon-Fri, EST); NHTSA Vehicle Safety Hotline 1-888-327-4236.

## Kill test
- Newsworthy? City transit buses actively carrying commuters and cyclists' bikes; the bike rack meant to protect cyclists can fall off into traffic. Yes.
- Novel? Zero prior Nova Bus / bike rack coverage in 334 queue entries. "Recall of a recall" is a site beat (Ridgeline #1089, GM L87 #864/#1025), but this instance is new and has the sharpest mechanical absurdity yet: the repair's screws were literally too short.
- Novel contribution: frames the failure as a *thread engagement* story (screw length vs. threaded depth), and counts the pattern: this is the site's third documented recall-of-a-recall beat this quarter. Counterargument included (18 buses, small population; transit agencies inspect frequently; rack failure is low-energy hazard relative to engine fires).

## Limitations
- We don't know how many (if any) racks actually detached; NHTSA filed no incident count in the public summaries.
- We don't know whether 24V873's remedy installed short screws or the screws loosened post-repair; the recall record says the remedy must be redone, which is the operative fact.
- 18 units is small; no fleet-wide fatality rate can be derived.

## Actionable takeaway
- Transit agencies running Nova Bus LFS (2022-2023): schedule the CR5936 re-repair; do not assume the 24V873 repair closed the issue. Cyclists: if the bus's front rack looks loose or misaligned, keep distance and tell the driver.
