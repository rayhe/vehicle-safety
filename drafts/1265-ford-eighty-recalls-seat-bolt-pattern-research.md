# Research: #1265 Ford eighty recalls / seat-bolt pattern

## Angle (1-2 sentences)
Ford reached 80 U.S. recalls in 2026, more than double runner-up Stellantis at 39, and the milestone 80th campaign was a 2,276-truck F-150 seat-bolt check. The count itself is the story: it measures Ford's detection appetite as much as its defect rate, and it was already out of date within days.

## Verified facts (3+ primary sources)
1. **80 recalls, Stellantis 39 (Autoblog, Oct 19, 2026):** "Ford has officially hit 80 recalls in the U.S. in 2026 and once again leads all other automakers. The next-closest automaker is Stellantis, with less than half the number of recalls at 39." Source: https://www.autoblog.com/news/ford-hits-80-recalls-in-2026-as-f-150-faces-another-safety-issue
2. **The 80th campaign (same Autoblog piece + NHTSA report as quoted there):** 2025-2027 F-150, front seat-to-body bolts may not be properly secured; 2,276 vehicles built Aug 25, 2025-Sep 23, 2026; 56 warranty reports + 16 field reports; no crashes or injuries known; owner letters Oct 26-30, 2026; Ford 26S74; Ford customer service 1-866-436-7332; VINs searchable on NHTSA site. Symptom: seat movement or noise.
3. **First-half scale (USA Today / BizzyCar, Oct 13, 2026):** Ford led with 55 campaigns spanning over 12 million vehicles in H1 2026; largest was a software glitch affecting trailer brake lights/brakes covering 4,380,609 vehicles, with only 1% estimated to actually have the defect. Source: https://www.usatoday.com/story/cars/recalls/2026/10/13/car-problem-recall-check/92215195007/
4. **Breakdown + Ford defense (Autoblog, Oct 19, 2026):** Of the 80, powertrain 11, electrical system 10, backover protection 9, structure 7, visibility 7. Largest powertrain item: unexpected downshifts on 2015-2017 F-150, nearly 1.4M vehicles, fixed by PCM software update. Another 741,195 Ford/Lincoln vehicles recalled for rollaway risk via PCM software. Ford's position: high recall rate reflects improved quality control / proactive identification, not declining quality; Ford ranked top-rated mainstream brand in J.D. Power 2026 U.S. Initial Quality Study. Counterweight in same piece: many Ford quality recalls have taken over five months to fix; the seat-bolt fix itself is expected to be quick.
5. **Count already stale (Reuters via syndication, Oct 20, 2026; AutoNotion summary):** Ford recalled 102,159 F-Series trucks over stuck/erratic analog speedometer/tachometer (NHTSA 26V653), covering certain 2025-2026 F-250/F-350/F-450/F-550/F-600 and 2026 F-150/F-750 SD; dealers replace instrument cluster modules free. This is effectively campaign 81 within days of the 80th.
6. **Method caveat (stated in article, not hidden):** A "recall" counts campaigns, not vehicles and not defect rates. One campaign can cover 4.38M vehicles at 1% defect, another 2,276 trucks. Campaign counts also reward detection: a company that finds more issues files more campaigns. The article says this explicitly rather than treating 80 as a quality score.
