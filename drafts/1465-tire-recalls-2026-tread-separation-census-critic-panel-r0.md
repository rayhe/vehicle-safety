## 2. Voice Coach — 9.0

## 3. Ethics Reviewer — 9.0
Zero crashes, injuries, or deaths are claimed for any of the six campaigns, because none are reported. No victims are named. No brand is smeared beyond its own documented recall. The Yokohama caveat (no NHTSA campaign number published) is handled honestly in the table and the disclaimer. The draft's one rhetorical overreach ("old enough to kill you") was cut before filing.

## 4. Social Shareability — 8.5

The kicker is workmanlike rather than viral, and the table is informative rather than outrageous. Strong enough; not the piece that breaks the site.

## 5. Legal Accuracy — 9.0
Campaign numbers, counts, and dates all trace to Part 573 records and manufacturer notices: Kumho's "remains uncertain" is a direct quote from the 26T009 filing; NHTSA's January 14 email to VeeRubber is in the 26T006 chronology; Toyo's 567 was verified against the live campaign record. The 11% completion figure is computed from NHTSA's own quarterly reports (4,054 of 36,919). "Last week" for the November 5 Yokohama announcement is accurate on November 10. G159's 173,237 comes from a secondary source (Schmidt & Clark) but matches the published 22T009 figure.

## 6. Research Rigor — 8.5
The census is the novel contribution, and the research file documents the 26T-series search method plus the RCL.txt download failure that limits completeness to "verified 2026 filings." The article itself states the FARS limitation (a tire failure shows up as a vehicle crash) and carries the counterargument in full. One gap: the census-completeness caveat lives in the research file and disclaimer, not in the article body. A single sentence would close it, but the disclaimer covers the material point.

## 7. Data Presentation — 9.0
Table is clean: six rows, campaign, tire, units, failure mode. Every number checks out: the total adds to 12,643, Kumho is 88.3% of it, the 2026 census is 7.3% of the G159 recall, and the 11% completion rate is computed, not asserted. Pull stat is the second-most important number in the piece. The Yokohama row shows an em dash for the campaign number with no inline footnote; the disclaimer explains it.
