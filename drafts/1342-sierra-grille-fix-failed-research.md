# Research: #1342 — GM's Sierra Grille Fix Didn't Hold. NHTSA Is Looking Again.

## Story proposal (1-2 sentences)
NHTSA opened recall query RQ26003 (Oct 9, 2026) to audit GM's February 2025 Sierra 1500 grille-deflector recall (25V-060) — because 21 of 118 owner complaints say the deflector detached AFTER the recall repair was performed, and the query covers 535,639 trucks, 7.6x the original recall's population.

## Kill test
- **Genuinely newsworthy?** Yes. A federal regulator formally auditing its own recall because the remedy didn't hold is rare and inherently adversarial — the manufacturer said "fixed," owners said "no."
- **Novel on vehicle-safety.org?** Yes. Site has covered Sierra grille nothing; L87 engine probe yes, grille zero. No prior article on a recall QUERY as the news object.
- **Novel angle?** The story is the recall process itself failing: scope too narrow (70,768 chrome-deflector 2022s vs. 535,639 trucks in 2022-24 including non-chrome) and remedy inadequate (reinforced old clips instead of replaced parts; 21 post-repair detachments).

## Primary sources (3+)
1. **NHTSA ODI Recall Query RQ26003** (opened Oct 9, 2026): estimated population 535,639 (2022-2024 GMC Sierra 1500), 118 complaints of upper grille deflector detachment while driving, 21 post-recall-25V-060-remedy. No crashes, injuries, or fatalities in the opening summary. Purpose stated: "to assess the adequacy of the scope and remedy for Recall 25V-060."
2. **NHTSA campaign 25V-060 (GM recall, filed Feb 6, 2025; GM campaign N242490120)**: covered 70,768 2022 Sierra 1500s with chrome deflectors built Nov 9, 2021 - Sep 29, 2022. Root cause: attachment points (8 per deflector) can fracture over time; all eight failing lets the bar leave at highway speed. GM estimated 3% of recalled trucks actually defective. Remedy: dealers "reinforce or repair" all attachment points, ~1 hour; repair instructions said do NOT replace the front grille unless necessary.
3. **GM internal investigation timeline (via Part 573 chronology reported by press)**: GM employee flagged a detached deflector on a driven 2022 Sierra through the internal Speak Up for Safety program Jan 19, 2024; investigation opened Feb 27, 2024, closed May 23, 2024 finding no defect trend; reopened Dec 11, 2024 when warranty claims climbed; recall filed Feb 6, 2025. GM's scope rationale: 2023+ trucks got deflectors from outside the supplier's suspect production window.
4. Secondary/press cross-checks: GM Authority (Oct 12), MotorBiscuit, gm-trucks.com, theautowire.com (Oct 15), Daxstreet (Oct 12) — all corroborate 118 complaints / 21 post-remedy / 535,639 population.

## Key numbers
- 118 complaints of deflector detachment while driving (NHTSA file)
- 21 of 118 (17.8%) allege detachment AFTER the 25V-060 repair
- 535,639 trucks in RQ26003 (2022-2024 Sierra 1500)
- 70,768 trucks in original recall 25V-060 (2022, chrome deflector only)
- Ratio: query population is 7.6x original recall population
- 8 attachment points; GM said 3% of recalled population actually defective
- 0 crashes, 0 injuries, 0 fatalities in the opening summary (so far)

## Counterargument (at full strength)
This is a trim bar, not a brake failure. Zero crashes or injuries after ~118 incidents suggests the real-world risk is modest — debris can swerve around, and a warning rattle may precede detachment. A recall query is not a defect finding; NHTSA audits recalls routinely and many close without expansion. The 21 post-remedy complaints are owner allegations, not verified failures, and repair records weren't confirmed at opening. GM may yet show the scope was right.

## Limitations
- Post-remedy complaint count (21) is allegation-level; NHTSA had not verified repair completion on those VINs at opening.
- 18% of complaints is not a failure rate — the denominator of actually-repaired trucks is unknown.
- No verified injury/crash data exists; lethality of a loose trim bar at highway speed is asserted by NHTSA ("without detectability," risk to motorcyclists/pedestrians) but unquantified.
- FARS cannot verify: zero fatalities involved.

## Actionable insight (required)
If you drive a 2022-2024 Sierra 1500: check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls for 25V-060 and whether the repair is complete. If you already had the recall repair, the deflector can still let go — listen for the rattle (GM's own filing says a loose deflector rattles before departure). 2023 and 2024 owners are NOT covered by any recall today; if your deflector detaches, file at nhtsa.gov — owner complaints are literally what opened this query.
