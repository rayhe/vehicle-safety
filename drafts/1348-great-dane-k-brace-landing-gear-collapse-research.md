# Research: #1348 — Great Dane K-Brace Landing Gear Collapse (26V708)

**Slug:** 1348-great-dane-k-brace-landing-gear-collapse
**Journalist:** Mia Crumplezone (Safety Engineering Editor — vehicle design analysis, safety tech; slightly judgmental about bad design)
**Kicker:** Investigation
**Date researched:** 2026-10-31
**Working title:** "Great Dane's Trailers Started Collapsing Inside Its Own Factory"

## Angle (1-2 sentences)
Great Dane is recalling 6,810 semi-trailers because the welds holding up their landing gear can fail and drop the trailer. The defect was discovered the hard way: two K-brace assemblies collapsed inside Great Dane's own production facilities on September 30 and October 1. A robot welder, a human clamp, and a fixture that let parts shift mid-weld.

## Kill test
- **Genuinely newsworthy?** Yes. Filed October 27, 2026 (four days ago). Landing-gear collapse drops a loaded trailer without warning; the failure mode specifically endangers dock workers during loading/unloading, when people are inside the trailer. Estimated 10% defect rate across 6,810 trailers.
- **Novel angle?** Yes. The discovery story is the hook: the failures happened on Great Dane's own factory floor, not in the field. The root cause is a manufacturing-process failure (supplier's manual clamping let components shift inside a robotic welding fixture, producing off-seam welds with poor fusion) — a design-for-manufacturing story, not a wear-and-tear story. Timing collision: NHTSA opened defect petition DP26012 on October 23 evaluating landing-gear collapse on 2017 Great Dane reefers with Jost gear — a different defect, same failure family, same week. This site covered the petition (#1310) and Great Dane's coupler recall (#1298); neither touches the K-brace weld issue.
- **3+ primary sources?** Yes: (1) NHTSA Part 573 Safety Recall Report 26V708 (filed Oct 27, 2026); (2) CDL Life reporting (Oct 29, 2026); (3) Fleet Maintenance NHTSA commercial-vehicle recall roundup (Oct 27, 2026); (4) thetrucker.com recall coverage (Oct 26, 2026).
- **Data-driven?** Yes: 6,810 units, 10% estimated defect rate, production windows, two in-factory failures with dates, supplier fixture analysis.
- **Verdict: PROCEED.**

## Verified facts

1. **The recall:** NHTSA campaign **26V708000**, report received October 27, 2026. Great Dane Trailers recalling **6,810** trailers: certain 2026–2027 Champion dry vans (5,623) and 2026–2027 Everest reefers (1,187) equipped with K-brace assemblies from a specific supplier. Production windows: Champion Dec 9, 2025–Oct 5, 2026; Everest Jan 9, 2026–Oct 12, 2026. (NHTSA Part 573 RCLRPT-26V708-7506)

2. **The defect:** K-brace welds (landing-gear support bracing) may fail due to **misaligned/off-seam weld placement and poor fusion**. "Manual clamping and fixture variability at the supplier may have allowed components to shift from their intended position within the robotic welding fixture, causing off-seam weld placement and reduced weld integrity." (NHTSA Part 573)

3. **The consequence:** "Trailers with misaligned welds on the K-brace could experience a failure, including the possibility of a collapse of the support gear, while the trailer is parked." Risk concentrated when the trailer is parked **without a tractor attached and without safety stands**, worse during loading/unloading or if bumped by another trailer. "A failure of the trailer support gear may increase the risk of property damage as well as increase the risk of injury to personnel inside or near the trailer at the time of failure." (NHTSA Part 573)

4. **The discovery:** "September 30 and October 1, 2026, there were **two failures of K-Brace assemblies in Great Dane production facilities**." The failed assemblies were returned to the supplier; analysis showed "weld bead misalignment, poor fusion, and nonflush component fit-up." (NHTSA Part 573)

5. **Defect rate:** Great Dane estimates **10%** of the 6,810-trailer population has the defect. (NHTSA Part 573)

6. **The remedy:** Inspect and replace the landing-gear K-brace as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters expected by **December 22, 2026**. Owners: Great Dane customer service **1-877-369-3493**. (CDL Life, Oct 29, 2026; NHTSA Part 573)

7. **Same-week context:** NHTSA opened **DP26012** on October 23, 2026, evaluating landing-gear collapse on 2017 Great Dane refrigerated trailers with Jost landing gear — a separate petition, separate hardware generation, same failure family (this site's #1310). Separately, Great Dane recalled 69 Champion trailers for an upper-coupler kingpin issue with a do-not-use warning (this site's #1298; thetrucker.com, Oct 26, 2026).

## What this article must NOT claim
- That any field collapses, injuries, or deaths have occurred. The two known failures were in-factory; no field incidents are cited in the Part 573.
- That DP26012 and 26V708 are the same defect. They are not (2017 Jost gear vs 2026–2027 K-brace supplier welds).
- That all 6,810 trailers will collapse. Estimated defect rate is 10%.
- The supplier's name (not disclosed in the Part 573 excerpt).

## Actionable takeaways for readers
- Fleet owners/operators of 2026–2027 Great Dane Champion/Everest trailers: check VINs at nhtsa.gov/recalls; expect letters by Dec 22, 2026.
- Until repaired: always use safety stands when a trailer is dropped without a tractor, especially during loading/unloading. The Part 573 explicitly limits the collapse risk to un-tractored, un-stood trailers.
- Dock workers: a trailer that shifts or settles unexpectedly during loading is a get-out-now signal.
