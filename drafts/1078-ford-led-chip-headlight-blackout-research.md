# Research: #1078 — Ford's Contaminated LED Chip Can Black Out All Forward Lighting (Recall 26C43)

**Slug:** `1078-ford-led-chip-headlight-blackout`
**Journalist:** Rex Driverton (Senior Crash Correspondent — investigations, FARS deep dives)
**Kicker:** Investigation
**Started:** 2026-10-05T21:30:00-07:00

## The story

Ford recall 26C43, announced Sept 30, 2026 (NHTSA): 41,748 vehicles. 2025-2027 Expedition
and 2026 F-250/F-350/F-450/F-550 Super Duty. The headlight assemblies "may contain a
contaminated LED chip, which can result in a loss of daytime running lights, parking
lights, high beams or low beams." Vehicles fail FMVSS 108. Dashboard indicator lights
for dead low/high beams. Remedy: dealer inspects and replaces headlight assemblies free.
Owner letters mailed Oct 26. Ford: 1-866-436-7332.

## Kill test: genuinely newsworthy? Yes.
- Recall announced 5 days ago; affects brand-new model years (2025-2027). Owners haven't
  even been notified yet (letters Oct 26) — a Crash Report piece lands BEFORE most
  owners hear from Ford.
- Unusual failure mode: not a wiring harness, not a software glitch — a contaminated
  semiconductor chip. LED headlights are sold as sealed, maintenance-free "lifetime"
  lighting; a chip fab contaminant can take out ALL FOUR forward-lighting modes at once.
- FMVSS 108 noncompliance on 2026 work trucks: the vehicles Ford markets to job sites
  and tow operators can legally fail the federal lighting standard.

## Original contribution (FARS cross-tab, 2014-2023)
- Expedition: 1,515 deaths, rate 2.31 per 100M VMT, fleet 525,000.
- F-250: 909 deaths, rate 0.43 per 100M VMT, fleet 1,575,000.
- Expedition fatality rate is 5.37x the F-250's — the family-hauling SUV built on Ford's
  truck DNA kills 5x more often per mile than the actual work truck. Both nameplates now
  share this recall's headlight assemblies. The SUV side of Ford's truck family is where
  the risk already lives, and it's the side Ford marketed as the safe family choice.
- Math: 2.31 / 0.43 = 5.372.

## Strongest counterargument
FARS rates are 2014-2023 and the recalled vehicles are 2025-2027 model years — the old
Expedition's record does not prove the new one is dangerous, and the recall was issued
proactively (defect identified, remedy defined) before any reported injuries tied to
26C43. A dashboard warning light alerts the driver before total darkness. The Expedition
rate also reflects driver mix and use patterns, not just the vehicle.

## Limitations
- FARS covers 2014-2023; the 2025-2027 recalled vehicles have no crash history in the
  dataset. The 5.37x figure describes past Expedition vs F-250 risk, not the recalled fleet.
- No public count of field failures exists for 26C43 (no warranty-claim or injury numbers
  disclosed in the notice reporting); we do not claim any crashes caused by this defect.
- rate is deaths per 100M VMT using estimated VMT (NHTS annual miles), not odometer
  readings — uncertainty for low-volume trims.

## Actionable takeaways
- If you own a 2025-2027 Expedition or 2026 F-250/F-350/F-450/F-550: check your VIN at
  nhtsa.gov/recalls now, before the Oct 26 letters. The remedy is free.
- Watch the dashboard: the recall notice says an indicator light illuminates when low or
  high beams die. If your beams fail at night, that's the chip, not a fuse — tell the
  dealer about 26C43.
- Expedition shoppers: the outgoing Expedition's 2.31 rate is one of the worst among
  large Ford SUVs (Explorer 1.54, Edge 0.46) — price the used market accordingly.
