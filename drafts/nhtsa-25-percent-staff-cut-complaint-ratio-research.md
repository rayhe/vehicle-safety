# Research: NHTSA Lost 25% of Its Staff. It Was Already Investigating 0.05% of Complaints.

## Primary Sources

1. **NHTSA staffing data (Foley & Lardner, Nov 2025):** Staff reduced from ~780 to ~575, approximately 25% cut. Combination of buyouts, probationary terminations, and retirements.
   - Source: https://www.foley.com/insights/publications/2025/11/driving-into-2026-the-state-of-nhtsa-and-the-future-of-vehicle-safety-regulation/

2. **DevDiscourse (Jul 2025):** Data provided to Congress: reduction from 772 to 555 staff members. USDOT eliminating 4,100+ positions total. FAA losing 2,137.
   - Source: https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/health/3508553-us-auto-safety-agency-sharp-decline-workforce-cuts-and-budget-concerns

3. **AV safety team specifically (TechCrunch, Apr 2025):** Self-driving safety division went from ~7 people to ~4. Division formed in 2023. Probationary employees disproportionately affected.
   - Source: https://techcrunch.com/2025/04/10/nhtsas-self-driving-safety-staff-reportedly-disproportionally-affected-by-doge-cuts/

4. **NHTSA FARS 2024 final data:** 39,254 traffic deaths in 2024, rate 1.19 per 100M VMT.
   - Source: https://www.nhtsa.gov/research-data/fatality-analysis-reporting-system-fars

5. **NHTSA 2025 early estimate:** 36,640 deaths, 6.7% decline, rate 1.10 per 100M VMT — 15th consecutive quarterly decline.
   - Source: https://crashstats.nhtsa.dot.gov/Api/Public/ViewPublication/813800

6. **Our own prior reporting:** "One Investigation Per 2,008 Complaints: Inside NHTSA's Defect Pipeline" — NHTSA receives 20,000+ complaints per quarter, opens 5-14 investigations. 99.5% of complaints go uninvestigated.

## Kill Test
Is this genuinely newsworthy? YES.
- The agency responsible for investigating vehicle defects that kill ~37,000 Americans/year lost 25% of its staff
- The AV safety team tasked with overseeing Tesla/Waymo went from 7 to 4 people — right before Tesla launched robotaxi service
- NHTSA was ALREADY processing only 0.05% of complaints into investigations
- The 6.7% fatality decline is happening DESPITE the cuts, not because of agency action — it's fleet turnover and newer safety tech
- The math: 575 staff for 36,640 deaths = 63.7 deaths per staff member. Before cuts: 780 staff for 39,254 deaths = 50.3 deaths per staff member
- Cross-reference with $665 million in safety grants still being distributed — more money, fewer people to oversee it
