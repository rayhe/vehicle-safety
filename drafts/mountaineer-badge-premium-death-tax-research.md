# Research: Mercury Mountaineer Badge Premium Death Tax

## Angle
The Mercury Mountaineer is mechanically identical to the Ford Explorer — same U502/UN152 platform, same factories, same powertrains. But the Mountaineer's FARS fatality rate is 11% higher (1.71 vs 1.54). Same car, different badge, more death. Badge engineering as a safety variable.

## Primary Sources
1. NHTSA FARS 2014-2023 (embedded in fars_output.js)
2. NHTSA recall database (nhtsa.gov/recalls)
3. IIHS vehicle size/weight study (iihs.org)
4. NHTSA Firestone tire investigation history

## Key Data Points

### Mercury Mountaineer (FARS)
- Rate: 1.71 deaths per 100M VMT
- Total: 374 deaths (37.4/yr), 565 crashes
- Deaths per crash: 0.662
- Fleet: ~175,000 estimated, VMT: 2,188M
- Impairment: 20.8% any, 15.2% alc, 8.7% drug
- Model years in data: 1997-2008
- Peak death year: MY2002 (76 deaths — Firestone era)
- Brand discontinued: 2011

### Ford Explorer (FARS) — comparison
- Rate: 1.54 deaths per 100M VMT
- Total: 3,797 deaths (379.7/yr), 6,626 crashes
- Deaths per crash: 0.573
- Fleet: ~1,968,750 estimated, VMT: 24,609M
- Impairment: 19.5% any, 14.8% alc, 8.1% drug

### Lincoln Aviator (FARS) — upmarket comparison
- Rate: 1.15 deaths per 100M VMT
- Total: 189 deaths, 299 crashes
- Deaths per crash: 0.632

### Mercury brand total FARS deaths: ~1,840
- Grand Marquis: 1,153 (rate 2.29)
- Mountaineer: 374 (rate 1.71)
- Mariner: 133 (rate 0.61)
- Sable: 98 (rate 0.32)
- Milan: 82 (rate 0.41)

## Novel Analysis
1. Same platform different death rate: 1.71 vs 1.54 = 11% higher for Mountaineer
2. Deaths-per-crash ratio: 0.662 vs 0.573 = 15.5% more lethal per crash
3. Impairment: 20.8% vs 19.5% = slightly higher (1.3pp) but not enough to explain the gap
4. MY2002 spike: 76 deaths in one model year (Firestone tire crisis)
5. Mercury = older buyers, longer hold times, older vehicles in fleet
6. Badge engineering makes the vehicle MORE expensive but LESS safe

## Counterarguments (must address at full strength)
- Fleet estimation uncertainty ±15% for low-volume models like Mountaineer
- Different option packages/trim levels could mean different safety equipment
- Mercury buyers skewed older = more fragile in crashes (not a vehicle defect)
- MY mix differs — Mountaineer concentrated in older, less safe generations
- Small sample relative to Explorer (374 vs 3,797 deaths)
