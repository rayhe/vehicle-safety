# Research: GM L87 V8 Recall Fix — 7,000 Post-Fix Failures

## Angle
GM recalled 597,630 vehicles with the L87 6.2L V8 in April 2025 for engine seizure at highway speed. The "fix" for most owners was an oil viscosity change — not an engine replacement. NHTSA just expanded the probe to 997,743 vehicles after receiving 7,000 post-fix failure complaints. The oil-change fix isn't working.

## Kill Test
Yes — genuinely newsworthy. Breaking (Aug 21, 2026). The data point that 95% of NHTSA's post-fix complaints came from vehicles that got only an oil change (not engine replacement) is original analysis from the NHTSA complaint breakdown. The expansion to 2025-2026 MY means GM kept building the same engine even after recalling it.

## Primary Sources

### 1. NHTSA Engineering Analysis (Aug 21, 2026)
- Investigation expanded to 997,743 vehicles
- Models: Cadillac Escalade/ESV, Chevy Silverado 1500/Suburban/Tahoe, GMC Sierra 1500/Yukon/Yukon XL
- Engine: L87 6.2L V8, MY 2021-2026
- 499 NHTSA complaints of post-remedy engine failure
  - 473 received oil viscosity change only (95.5%)
  - 26 received complete engine replacement (5.2%)
- 191 additional complaints in engines from post-recall production period
- Previous investigation scope: 877,710 vehicles
- Source: Reuters (https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/nhtsa-opens-probe-into-nearly-1-million-gm-pickups-suvs-over-engine-failure-2026-08-21/), Detroit Free Press

### 2. GM Recall (April 24, 2025)
- 597,630 U.S. vehicles recalled (721,000 globally)
- MY 2021-2024
- Cause: connecting rod and crankshaft component manufacturing defects; multiple supplier quality issues
- Fix path 1: Engine passes diagnostic → higher viscosity oil + new oil fill cap + oil filter
- Fix path 2: Engine fails diagnostic → complete engine replacement
- Special coverage: 10 years or 150,000 miles for engines that passed
- GM had opened three internal investigations between 2022 and 2024 before recall
- Source: NHTSA recall database, Freep

### 3. GM Complaint Data
- GM received 6,953 complaints of post-remedy engine failure
- NHTSA received 499 complaints (subset of GM's total)
- 28,000+ total complaints identified by GM's investigation
- ~50% alleged loss of propulsion while driving
- 12 crashes, 12 injuries, 42 fire reports identified by GM in 2025
- Source: NHTSA investigation resume, Reuters, Freep

### 4. NHTSA Investigation Timeline
- Jan 2025: NHTSA opens initial investigation into 877,710 vehicles
- Apr 24, 2025: GM issues voluntary recall of 597,630 vehicles
- Oct 2025: NHTSA opens engineering analysis into recall effectiveness; covers 286,000 vehicles not in GM's recall
- Jan 16, 2026: NHTSA opens another recall investigation into the engine fixes
- Aug 21, 2026: NHTSA expands probe to include 2025-2026 MY, total now 997,743

## Novel Analysis
- **Oil-change-as-fix ratio**: 473 of 499 NHTSA post-fix failures (95.5%) came from vehicles that got oil, not new engines. The "fix" that most owners received isn't working.
- **Production continuation**: GM added the L87 to 2025 and 2026 vehicles AFTER recalling 2021-2024 models. NHTSA now investigating those newer vehicles too. 191 complaints from post-recall production period.
- **Complaint disparity**: GM reports 6,953 post-fix complaints; NHTSA shows 499. The 14:1 ratio suggests most owners complain to GM, not the government.
- **Three investigations before recall**: GM opened internal investigations in 2022, 2023, and 2024 but didn't recall until NHTSA started investigating in Jan 2025.
