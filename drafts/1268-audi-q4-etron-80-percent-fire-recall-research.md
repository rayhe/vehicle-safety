# Research: Audi's Electric SUV Gets the 80% Haircut. The Fix Does Not Exist Yet.

**Slug:** 1268-audi-q4-etron-80-percent-fire-recall | **Journalist:** Clara Rollover | **Kicker:** The Gap

## News peg
NHTSA campaign 26V698 (Audi 93SK), report received Oct 21, 2026; VINs searchable Oct 23, 2026:
- Volkswagen Group of America (Audi) recalls certain 2024-2025 Audi Q4 e-tron and Q4 Sportback e-tron vehicles.
- 9,048 vehicles potentially affected (single campaign total across both body styles; NHTSA gives no per-model split).
- Defect: the high-voltage (traction) battery may fail and cause a fire; a vehicle fire increases the risk of injury.
- Interim instruction: charge to a maximum of 80% capacity until the repair is completed.
- Remedy: under development. Interim owner letters expected Dec 18, 2026; further letters once a remedy is available.
- No do-not-drive or park-outside advisory in the NHTSA record for this campaign.
- Audi customer service: 1-800-253-2834.

## Verified facts
- Component: Electrical system, propulsion system, traction battery.
- Model years: 2024-2025 only. These are current-generation cars, not a legacy fleet.
- 9,048 is the potentially-affected population, NOT a count of confirmed battery failures. The NHTSA record as reported does not state a failure count, a fire count, or a defect rate.
- The 80% cap is an interim risk reduction, not a repair. Owners lose roughly a fifth of their usable range indefinitely, until a remedy Audi has not yet described is ready.

## Novel contribution (original to this piece)
1. **The 80% cap is becoming Volkswagen Group's house answer.** This site has covered the VW ID.4 battery recalls at 80% three times (#1105, #1172, #1194). Now the same cap lands on the premium Audi sibling, on the same MEB platform family. When the same interim measure migrates upmarket, it stops looking like a one-model problem and starts looking like a platform-level battery question Audi has not answered.
2. **The cap without a park-outside order is the tell.** Hyundai Genesis/G80 seat-motor fires (#1173, #1224) and Kalmar yard trucks (#1267) drew park-outside orders. Audi's Q4 draws only a charge cap and no parking instruction. That split is itself information: either Audi believes state-of-charge control meaningfully bounds this failure mode, or it is unwilling to tell owners of a premium EV to sleep their car on the street. The record does not say which. Readers should not mistake the absence of a park-outside order for proof the car is safe to charge indoors at 80%.
3. **A 2024-2025 car with no fix and a December letter.** Owners bought these cars new within the last two model years. Their first official letter about the risk arrives Dec 18, 2026, nearly two months after the NHTSA filing, and it will contain no repair. The timeline, not just the defect, is the consumer harm: months of reduced range with no end date.

## What this does not prove (limitations)
- No published failure count, fire count, or root-cause description (cell defect? module? software?) in the sources checked. Do not invent one.
- No per-model split between Q4 e-tron and Q4 Sportback e-tron.
- The Q4 e-tron has a separate, earlier recall history (2024-2025 onboard-charger / 12V issue, 13,769 vehicles incl. ID.4, recall 93FR) — a different defect (loss of drive power), not to be conflated with this traction-battery fire campaign. Mentioned only to prevent confusion.
- 80% charging does not guarantee no fire; it is a risk-reduction instruction from the manufacturer, not a tested safety threshold published in the record.

## Strongest counterargument
A proportionate reader could call this a small, cautious recall: 9,048 cars, no reported fires cited in the record, no park-outside order, and a conservative interim cap while Audi engineers a fix. Compared with the 94,622 Hyundai Genesis seat-motor fire recall the same month, Audi's footprint is a tenth the size. The honest version holds both: small population, real fire risk, no fix, indefinite range loss.

## Actionable insights
- Own a 2024-2025 Q4 e-tron or Sportback: check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls now (searchable since Oct 23, 2026); do not wait for the Dec 18 letter.
- Set the charge limit to 80% in the car or app today and leave it there until Audi issues a remedy.
- Shopping used: a Q4 e-tron at a discount is not a bargain until campaign 93SK shows a completed remedy on that VIN.
- Call Audi at 1-800-253-2834 and reference recall 93SK with questions.
