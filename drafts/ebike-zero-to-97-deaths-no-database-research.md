# Research: E-Bike Deaths — Zero to 97 in Seven Years, and NHTSA Still Can't Count Them

## Angle
The most rapidly growing vehicle death category in America isn't a car — and the government's flagship crash database can't track it. E-bike deaths went from 0 to 97 in seven years (CPSC data). Child ER visits exploded 671% since 2023 (Truveta). Ford's Bronco gets a 565K-vehicle recall for 15 fire incidents, but the vehicle actually killing more kids each year flies under the regulatory radar.

The cross-tabulation nobody ran: CPSC micromobility death data vs FARS bicycle totals vs Truveta injury data vs recall response proportionality.

## Primary Sources (5)

### 1. CPSC Micromobility Report (April 2026)
- Source: cpsc.gov
- 533 total micromobility deaths, 2017-2024
- E-bike deaths: 0 (2017) → 97 (2024), 310 total (58% of all micromobility deaths)
- E-scooter deaths: 206 (39%)
- ER visits: 37,300 (2017) → 149,100 (2024)
- 135 new deaths added in 2024 alone (115 e-bikes, 44 e-scooters)

### 2. Truveta / USA TODAY (July 29, 2026)
- 12,000+ ER visits analyzed across 30 US health systems
- Child/adolescent e-bike/e-scooter ER visits surged 7.5-fold (671%) between Jan 2023 and May 2026
- >1/3 of injured patients: head/neck injuries
- ~7% of child visits involved traumatic brain injury (TBI)
- Lead researcher: Karen Gilbert Farrar, PhD

### 3. Health Affairs Forefront (June 22, 2026)
- "When Bicycles Become Motor Vehicles: A Public Health Reckoning For The E-Bike Era"
- JAMA Surgery: 30-fold rise in e-bicycle injuries 2017-2022
- Pediatric study (Surgery Open Science): kids on e-bikes hospitalized at 11.5% vs 4.8% for regular bikes (2.4x)
- 10-13 age group most frequently injured
- Almost none of injured young e-bike riders wore helmets
- Compares pattern to e-cigarettes, opioids, ATVs: "product reaches mass market faster than regulated"

### 4. NHTSA FARS / NSC Injury Facts
- 1,392 bicyclist deaths in 2024 (includes e-bikes, undifferentiated)
- 1,155 bicyclists killed in motor-vehicle traffic crashes in 2023 (NHTSA)
- FARS does NOT distinguish e-bike from conventional bicycle
- No separate FARS coding for e-bikes despite 97 CPSC-tracked e-bike deaths in 2024

### 5. Illinois Hospital Study (PubMed, PMID 40876518)
- 34,073 patients with micromobility injuries, 2021-2023
- E-bike riders 2.39x more likely to need ICU vs cyclists
- E-bike riders 1.3x more likely TBI vs cyclists
- 45.4% of e-bike injuries from motor vehicle crashes
- E-scooter injuries primarily from falls (78.4%)

## Original Analysis
1. **Regulatory response proportionality:** Ford Bronco: 565,911 vehicles recalled for 15 fire incidents = 37,727 recalls per incident. E-bikes: 97 deaths in 2024, 149,100 ER visits, zero federal recalls, zero mandatory design standards.
2. **FARS invisibility math:** 97 CPSC-tracked e-bike deaths ÷ 1,392 total bicycle deaths (NSC) = e-bikes are ~7% of all bike fatalities. But FARS codes them identically. If the same growth rate continues, e-bike deaths will exceed motorcycle deaths in specific youth demographics within years.
3. **The split-jurisdiction problem:** CPSC counts e-bike deaths. NHTSA counts traffic deaths. Neither has full authority. CPSC can't mandate traffic behavior (helmets, age limits). NHTSA doesn't classify e-bikes as motor vehicles. Result: the deadliest new vehicle category falls through the regulatory crack.
