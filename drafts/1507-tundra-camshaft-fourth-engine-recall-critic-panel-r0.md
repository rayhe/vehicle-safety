## Pre-panel fact-check (DRAFT verification, before scoring)
The draft was checked against the site's V35A research file before the panel ran, and three errors were caught and fixed in the draft and the research file:
1. Nov 2025 bearing wave listed as ~124,000 / 2022-2023. Actual: 25V767, 126,691 vehicles, 2022-2024 Tundra + Lexus LX/GX. Table corrected.
2. "332,000 Tundras" total. The ~270,000 bearing-wave total includes Lexus LX/GX, so the four-wave total is ~332,000 vehicles, not Tundras. Corrected.
3. "Two engine recalls, zero overlap in model years" was wrong: the Nov 2025 bearing wave already covered 2022-2024 Tundras, so a 2022-2023 truck in the camshaft recall may be on its second engine recall. The mirror-image framing (everything-but-2024 vs. only-2024) now applies only to the two newest recalls, and the second-recall finding is now in the article.
4. Limitations paragraph wrongly claimed no FARS Tundra data exists. The V35A file has it: 1,223 deaths 2014-2023, 0.94/100M VMT, 4th-safest full-size pickup. Added to the article as a cross-tab; limitations now correctly note FARS counts deaths, not breakdowns.
