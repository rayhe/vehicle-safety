# Research: The Blood Gap (1201)

**Journalist:** Mia Crumplezone (safety tech editor)
**Kicker:** The Gap
**Slug:** 1201-prehospital-blood-gap
**Angle:** After 16 straight quarters of declining traffic deaths, the next big lever isn't in the car at all. It's in the ambulance. 43% of highway deaths were alive when first responders arrived; fewer than 3% of EMS agencies carry blood.

## Kill test
- Newsworthy? YES. ## Primary sources (3+)
1. **NHTSA GHSA Annual Meeting keynote** (Jonathan Morrison, NHTSA Administrator), nhtsa.gov/speeches-presentations/ghsa-annual-meeting-keynote — 43% alive at first-responder arrival (2019-2023); 32,992 crash patients met prehospital-blood criteria in 2025 (NEMSIS); 37% mortality-risk reduction; 430+ EMS agencies with programs, 50+ launched since P2SS unveiled April 2026; 15,000 EMS agencies nationwide; drone blood delivery announced August 2026; catastrophic blood loss = leading cause of death from injury.
2. **NHTSA Office of EMS August 2026 webinar FAQ** (ems.gov PDF) — every minute of delay in administering blood raises death risk 11%; rural: 39% of fatal crash victims take 1-2 hrs to reach hospital vs 10% urban; prehospital blood "can save up to 37 percent of trauma patients with severe bleeding."
3. **FICEMS RFI via AABB** (Oct 6, 2026) — goals by end of 2028: prevent up to 10,000 deaths annually; 5% reduction in overall mortality from severe bleeding; 2,028 EMS agencies with active programs; 28% of pediatric / 16% of all life-threatening-bleeding patients receive prehospital blood; docket NHTSA-2026-2113.
4. **FireRescue1** (Aug 7, 2026) — Hillsborough County Fire Rescue + Tampa General: nation's first EMS-driven drone whole-blood delivery; 70 sq mi; under 3 minutes average; FAA beyond-visual-line-of-sight waiver; Archer First Response Systems; live operations October 2026.
5. **NHTSA CrashStats DOT HS 813 833** (July 2026) — 2025 estimate 36,640 deaths; Q1 2026 7,770 (-4.3%); 16th consecutive quarterly decline.

## Original calculations (show the math)
- 36,640 x 0.43 = 15,755 people dead in 2025 who were alive when first responders arrived.
- 430 / 15,000 = 2.9% of US EMS agencies carry blood today.
- 2,028 / 15,000 = 13.5%: even the 2028 federal goal leaves ~86% of agencies without blood.
- 10,000 / 36,640 = 27.3%: the "up to 10,000 preventable deaths" aspiration is more than a quarter of all annual traffic deaths.
- 32,992 patients met criteria in 2025 (NEMSIS) vs ~430 programs: the screening net is ~77x wider than the treatment net.

## Limitations (must state in article)
- The 37% mortality reduction comes from studies of severely bleeding trauma patients in mature systems; it does not apply to all 32,992 patients who met criteria, and the per-minute 11% figure cannot be compounded linearly over an hour (it describes early-window risk).
- The 43% alive-at-arrival includes unsurvivable injuries (massive head trauma, etc.) where blood would not have helped.
- "Up to 10,000 preventable deaths" is a FICEMS aspiration in an RFI, not a measured outcome.
- Programs are early-stage: cold chain, blood supply/waste, cost, training, and rural capacity are real barriers; the agencies that need blood most (rural, long transport times) are least able to field it.
- Derived numbers (15,755; 27.3%; 2.9%) are arithmetic on cited inputs, rounded.

## Strongest counterargument (must state at full strength)
This may be a solution in search of scale. Whole blood has a ~35-42 day shelf life and needs strict cold chain; stocking it on 15,000 EMS agencies means enormous waste. The mortality data comes from elite trauma systems, not average county EMS. The rural agencies facing 1-2 hour transports are the ones that can't afford the program. And deaths are already falling for 16 straight quarters without a single unit of blood in an ambulance — maybe the money belongs in prevention, not in the last 30 minutes.

## Actionable insights
- Learn bleeding control (DHS "Stop the Bleed": direct pressure, tourniquets). The civilian equivalent of prehospital blood is not waiting.
- Ask your county EMS agency whether ambulances carry whole blood; the technology is a cooler, a protocol, and a medical director's signature.
- Rural readers: know your distance to the nearest trauma center; the 39% 1-2 hour stat is about you.
- NHTSA opened docket NHTSA-2026-2113 (Oct 2026) seeking public input on the national blood strategy; comments were due Nov 5, 2026.
