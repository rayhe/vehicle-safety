# Research: Ram 1500 Seat Belt Buckle Anchor Recall

## Core News
- **Date**: July 31, 2026
- **Recall**: Stellantis recalling 1,271,294 Ram 1500 pickups (MY 2019-2026)
- **Defect**: Second-row seat belt buckle anchors may not be properly attached to vehicle body structure
- **Scope**: Driver-side or center seat of second row
- **Impact**: In a crash, seat belts may not perform, increasing injury risk for rear passengers
- **International**: Additional ~245K (156K Canada, 15K Mexico, 74K other)

## FARS Cross-Reference (Novel Contribution)
From our FARS_BY_MODEL data:
- Ram 1500 lethality per crash: 0.341 (among the LOWEST — #3 safest)
- Ram 1500 death rate per VMT: 0.13 (among the LOWEST)
- Ram 1500 deaths: 714, crashes: 2,095
- For comparison: Ram 2500 is #1 safest at 0.205 lethality

**The paradox**: The Ram 1500 is statistically one of the safest trucks in fatal crash data. But Stellantis may have been building them for 7 years without the rear seat belts properly anchored. The truck's low lethality rate might partially reflect that its occupants survive crashes — but rear passengers in 1.27 million of them might not have had functioning restraints.

## Key Context
- Seat belts save ~15,000 lives per year (NHTSA estimate)
- Seat belt technology is the simplest, most proven safety device — no software, no sensors
- This is a manufacturing quality control failure, not a design flaw
- 7 model years of production before discovery
- Stellantis has been in financial turmoil (CEO departure, profit warnings)
