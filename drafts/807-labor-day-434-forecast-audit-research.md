# Research: NSC's 434 Labor Day Forecast Audit (#807, Axle McScatter)

## Angle
NSC forecasts 434 deaths for Labor Day weekend 2026 (Aug 26, 2026 PR). Axle normalizes the forecast to deaths-per-day and audits it against last year's actuals. The forecast implies ~134 deaths/day, ~25% above an ordinary day. But 2024's actual Labor Day toll was 505, 14% HIGHER than this year's forecast. Is the forecast optimistic, or did the window definition change? Plus: 77% of impaired drivers killed were at nearly 2x the legal limit, and 106 of the 434 are seat-belt-savable.

## Original calculations (Axle's contribution)
- Ordinary-day baseline: 39,254 / 366 (2024 leap year) = 107.2 deaths/day.
- NSC 2026 window: Fri 6:00pm to Mon 11:59pm = 78 hours = 3.25 days. 434 / 3.25 = 133.5 deaths/day. Premium: (133.5-107.2)/107.2 = +24.6% above ordinary.
- 2024 actual window: Sun 6:00pm to Thu 5:59am = 84 hours = 3.5 days. 505 / 3.5 = 144.3 deaths/day. Premium: +34.6% above ordinary.
- Forecast vs actual: 434 forecast is 14.1% below 2024's actual 505. Caveat: windows differ (3.25 vs 3.5 days); per-day, forecast is 7.5% below last year's actual rate (133.5 vs 144.3).
- Seat-belt savable share: 106 / 434 = 24.4%.
- Alcohol share 2024: 170/505 = 33.7%.

## Counterargument (strongest)
Holiday estimates are inherently noisy; NSC's model uses historical patterns and the +2% vs last year's *estimate* (not vs actual) is the honest comp. Also the 2024 actual (505) came during a higher-baseline year; 2024 full-year deaths fell 3.8% and Q declines continued, so a lower 2026 forecast is defensible, not necessarily optimistic. Also: enforcement campaigns do move the needle, forecasts aren't destiny.

## Limitations
- NSC does not publish its holiday model coefficients; cannot independently reproduce 434.
- Window definitions differ between NSC forecast and NHTSA actuals; per-day normalization is mine, approximate.
- 2024 annual total (39,254) is final FARS; 2025/2026 baselines are estimates.
- FARS 2024 holiday-window fatality details (hour of day, BAC distribution) not pulled; using NHTSA marketing page aggregates.

## Actionable takeaways
- If driving this weekend: the per-day death rate runs ~25-35% above normal; Friday 6pm-Monday is the window.
- Buckle up: ~1 in 4 forecast deaths are belt-preventable per NSC.
- The drunk-driver profile is not "one beer over": 77% of impaired drivers killed were at .15+, nearly twice the limit. Plan the ride before drinking, not after.
- 18-34 drivers: 40% of those killed last Labor Day were drunk. Rideshare math beats funeral math.
- Check recalls at ChecktoProtect.org (NSC's own tip).
