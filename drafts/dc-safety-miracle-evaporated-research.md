# Research: DC's Safety Miracle Evaporated

## Angle
DC celebrated the best traffic safety improvement in the entire nation in 2025 — a 46.8-52% drop in fatalities. By August 2026, the city had already exceeded its entire 2025 death toll. The national trend continued improving (Q1 2026 rate: 0.99/100M VMT, -4.3% deaths). DC went the opposite direction.

## Novel Analysis

### The Statistical Volatility Problem
DC has tiny numbers. The difference between 25 and 52 deaths is 27 people. In a city of ~700,000 with ~3.8 billion VMT, individual crashes have massive statistical leverage.

Key calculation: DC's 2025 per-capita fatality rate was ~3.6 per 100,000 — roughly a third of the national rate. But through Aug 14, 2026, DC is tracking at ~5.0 per 100,000 annualized, which would be its worst since 2021.

### The Pattern
DC's history shows multiple "breakthrough" years followed by rebounds:
- 2010: 25 deaths → 2011: 32 (+28%)
- 2012: 19 deaths (historic low) → 2013: 29 (+53%)
- 2025: 25 deaths → 2026: on pace for 40+ 

The city has NEVER sustained a sub-30 year for more than one consecutive year in the past 20 years.

### Vision Zero Implications
DC adopted Vision Zero in 2015. Since then: 26, 28, 30, 36, 27, 37, 40, 35, 52, 52, 25. The trend line is UP despite the program. The 2025 dip looks like noise, not signal.
