# Research: Honda's 3.5L V6 — Three Federal Investigations for the Same Engine Killing Itself

## Angle
Honda's J-series 3.5L V6 has been the subject of three separate federal safety actions in three years for connecting rod bearing failures that cause complete engine seizure. The 2023 recall was too narrow. NHTSA keeps finding more.

## Timeline
1. **2020:** Honda first becomes aware of connecting rod bearing failures in 3.5L V6 engines
2. **November 2023:** Honda recalls 248,999 vehicles — root cause: "improper settings of equipment used to manufacture the engine crankshaft" producing "crank pins with a crown or convex shape that are out of specification." 1,450 warranty claims. No injuries reported. Recall scope: specific crankshaft manufacturing batches.
3. **November 2024:** NHTSA opens investigation (PE24-018 or similar) into ~1.4 million vehicles. 173 complaints of connecting rod bearing failures from vehicles NOT covered by the 2023 recall. One crash reported, no injuries. The failures extend beyond the recalled manufacturing batch.
4. **August 2026 (letter dated Aug 20):** NHTSA opens ANOTHER investigation. Now 414 complaints. Previous investigation closed; reopened to "more thoroughly evaluate the scope and severity." The complaint count nearly tripled since 2024.

## Affected Models (all with 3.5L V6)
- Honda Pilot (2016-2020)
- Honda Odyssey (2018-2020)
- Honda Ridgeline (2017-2019)
- Acura MDX (2016-2020)
- Acura TLX (2018-2020)

## The Core Problem
Connecting rod bearing failure → engine seizure → total loss of power while driving. The connecting rods link pistons to crankshaft; bearing failure means metal-on-metal contact. Possible outcomes: engine stall, fire, loss of power steering and power brakes at highway speed.

The 2023 recall blamed a specific crankshaft manufacturing defect (incorrectly ground crank pins). But complaints from vehicles with crankshafts from different batches suggest either:
- The defect was more widespread than Honda determined
- There's a second failure mode in the J-series 3.5L design

## Key Numbers
- 248,999 recalled in 2023
- ~1.41 million total vehicles potentially affected
- 1,450 warranty claims (as of 2023 recall)
- 414 NHTSA complaints (as of Aug 2026) from vehicles outside recall scope
- 3 federal actions in 3 years (recall + 2 investigations)

## FARS Data (all affected models combined)
- Honda Odyssey: 864 deaths, rate 0.93/100M VMT, 15.4% impaired
- Honda Pilot: 514 deaths, rate 0.29/100M VMT, 19.4% impaired
- Honda Ridgeline: 84 deaths, rate 0.24/100M VMT, 19.4% impaired
- Acura MDX: 163 deaths, rate 0.30/100M VMT
- Acura TLX: 33 deaths, rate 0.11/100M VMT
- **Combined: 1,658 deaths across all 5 models in FARS**

Note: FARS doesn't isolate engine failure as a crash cause factor, so these deaths encompass all crash types.
