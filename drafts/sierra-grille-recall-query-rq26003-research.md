## Verified facts

- NHTSA Office of Defects Investigation opened **recall query RQ26003 on October 9, 2026** covering an estimated **535,639 GMC Sierra 1500 pickups (2022-2024 model years)**. Subject: upper grille deflector detaching while driving. (theautowire, motorbiscuit, gmauthority, gm-trucks, 4wdtalk, autopostglobal, automotor.news)
- **118 owner complaints** logged with ODI. **21 of the 118** allege the deflector detached AFTER the truck had received the recall repair under 25V-060. (theautowire; motorbiscuit: "the most notable detail")
- The original recall, **25V-060 (GM campaign N242490120), filed Feb 6, 2025**, covered only **70,768 trucks**: 2022 Sierra 1500s with a chrome deflector built after the mid-cycle refresh (build window Nov 9, 2021 – Sep 29, 2022). Non-chrome 2022 trucks and ALL 2023-2024 Sierras were excluded. The query population is more than **7x** the original recall. (theautowire; automotor.news; 4wdtalk)
- Failure mechanism per GM's filing: **one or more of the eight attachment points** holding the deflector can fracture over time; if all eight break, the deflector separates while driving. Owners might hear a **rattle** first. (motorbiscuit; automotor.news)
- Remedy: dealers reinforce or repair attachment points free of charge; owner notification letters mailed **March 17, 2025**. GM customer service for GMC: 1-800-462-8782. (automotor.news)
- ODI's stated purpose: **"assess the adequacy of the scope and remedy for Recall 25V-060."** A recall query is not a new recall and is not a defect finding; no crashes, injuries, or fatalities are listed in the opening summary. (gm-trucks; theautowire)
- Risk described by ODI: deflector detaches **"without detectability"**; at-risk parties: the truck itself, nearby vehicles, **motorcyclists and pedestrians**. Some complaints describe cracked windshields and nearby drivers swerving. (theautowire; motorbiscuit; autopostglobal)
- Timeline of GM's own file: a GM employee flagged a detached deflector through the internal **Speak Up for Safety** program on **Jan 19, 2024**; GM opened an investigation Feb 27, 2024, **closed it May 23, 2024** after finding no defect trend; **reopened Dec 11, 2024** when warranty claims climbed. GM counted **1,225 complaints and 4 accident allegations by end of 2024**, no known injuries. (theautowire; 4wdtalk)
- Repair completion: roughly **64%** of recalled trucks had the repair as of January 2026 (GM Authority via 4wdtalk). No newer figure published.
- Why 2023-2024 trucks are in the query: GM told NHTSA the later trucks used parts **outside the supplier's suspect window**; the probe tests whether that cutoff was right. (4wdtalk, citing autoevolution)

## Angle for Clara

The consumer-service outrage angle: you did everything right. You got the recall letter, you took the truck in, a dealer touched it, and a year later the part flies off anyway. One in five of the complaints NHTSA has is that exact story. The piece should explain what a recall query IS (most readers have never heard of one), what to do right now: check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls, listen for the rattle, and if you had the 25V-060 repair done, it may not have held.

Actionable takeaway: Sierra 1500 owners 2022-2024 — check VIN, watch for the rattle, and repaired 2022 chrome-deflector trucks are NOT cleared; the fix is under review.
