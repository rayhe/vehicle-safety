## 6. Research Rigor — 9.0/10
Original contribution, threefold: (a) the S-10/Sonoma 2.27x badge-twin gap, never run on this site (#1279 covered the Sonoma alone); (b) the gap repeating in the next generation (Canyon 0.59 vs Colorado 0.28, 2.1x); (c) Poisson 95% confidence intervals showing the twin gap is not sampling noise (4.58-5.08 vs 1.92-2.34, no overlap). Limitations are explicit and honest: estimated VMT/fleet (±15%), fatal-only data, no trim/age/road-type breakout, IIHS tested only the 2003 model year, model-year counts confound age and exposure. Strongest counterargument stated at full strength: small Sonoma sample plus possible registration-split misestimation, and the candid note that the Canyon/Colorado repeat could share the same artifact since both pairs rest on the same registration data. Methodology is transparent: every ratio shows its inputs. Deduction: the mechanism behind the badge gap remains unexplained, which the article admits rather than papering over. That is rigor, not a flaw.
