# Research: Ford F-250 — No Crash Test Required

## Angle
The Ford F-250 Super Duty has 6,754 drivers in FARS fatal crashes and 909 occupant deaths (2014-2023), yet federal law does not require a single crash test for heavy-duty pickups. The IIHS just started preliminary safety evaluations of HD trucks for the first time in June 2026 — and found that basic safety features like airbags aren't even legally required.

## Self-Critique Gate
**Proposed angle:** The F-250 is one of the deadliest vehicles in America by involvement count, yet sits in a regulatory blind spot where no crash test is mandated by federal law.
**Challenge:** Is this genuinely surprising after 634 articles? Yes — we've never profiled the F-250 specifically, and the IIHS's June 2026 announcement that they're starting to test HD trucks is a fresh news hook that validates the gap.
**Verdict:** Proceed.

## Primary Sources

### 1. FARS Data (2014-2023)
- Ford F-250: 909 deaths, 2,499 fatal crashes, 90.9 annual deaths
- Death rate: 0.43 per 100M VMT (fleet: 1,575,000; VMT: 21,262M annually)
- Toxicology: 6,754 fatally-crashed drivers; 20.3% impaired (15.2% alcohol, 8.3% drugs)
- 79.7% of fatally-crashed F-250 drivers were SOBER
- Compare: F-150 has 9,194 deaths but much larger fleet
- F-250 rate (0.43/100M VMT) vs F-150 (not calculated separately but fleet is ~10x larger)
- Ford F-350: 164 deaths, 352 crashes, rate 0.15/100M VMT

### 2. IIHS First HD Truck Evaluation (June 2026)
- Source: IIHS.org, The Drive, Autoblog
- First-ever IIHS evaluation of heavy-duty pickups and cargo vans
- Tested: 2026 Silverado 3500HD, 2025 Ford F-350, 2025 Ram 3500, plus 6 cargo vans
- Only 4 of 9 vehicles met ALL safety criteria
- Federal regs don't require airbags, seatbelt pre-tensioners, or force limiters for Class 3+ vehicles
- "Crashes involving medium or heavy-duty trucks accounted for 6,535 American road fatalities in 2023 — roughly 16% of all traffic deaths"

### 3. FMVSS Regulatory Gap
- Vehicles over 8,500 lbs GVWR (Class 2b+) are exempt from most FMVSS passenger vehicle standards
- F-250 GVWR: ~10,000 lbs (Class 3)
- Not required: FMVSS 208 (occupant protection in frontal crashes — advanced airbag rule), FMVSS 214 (side impact), FMVSS 226 (ejection mitigation)
- Also exempt from fuel economy reporting, AEB mandates
- 2017 F-250 was VOLUNTARILY submitted by Ford for NHTSA 5-star testing — earned it for Crew Cab 4x2, but this is NOT required

### 4. NHTSA 2025 Subcategory Data (July 2026)
- Total fatalities down 6.7% in 2025
- Newer vehicle (<10 yr) PV occupant deaths: DOWN 12%
- Large-truck-involved crash fatalities: DOWN 7%
- BUT: improvement driven by passenger vehicles, not by HD truck safety improvements

## Original Contribution
Cross-tabulating FARS fatality data with FMVSS regulatory scope to show that the F-250 sits in a lethal gap: killing at a rate comparable to much smaller, fully-regulated vehicles, while being exempt from the crash tests and safety mandates that reduced deaths in those vehicles.
