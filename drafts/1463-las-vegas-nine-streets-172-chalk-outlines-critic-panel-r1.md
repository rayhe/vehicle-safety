## 5. Legal Accuracy — 8.9/10
Round-1 catch, fixed before scoring: the draft originally cited the GHSA preliminary projection (7,148 pedestrian deaths in 2024) under a Reuters reference. Verified against NHTSA's final count (DOT HS 813 818, June 2026): 7,080, down 3.9% from 7,367 in 2023, one pedestrian killed every 74 minutes. Article and references now carry the final NHTSA figure with the correct citation. All other claims trace to cited sources: 172 count and street list (PedSafe Vegas via Focal News/News 3), 45 mph commonality (Vegas Daily Wire), October 11 deaths and 8-of-10 after sunset (Vegas Daily Wire Oct 27), 6x DST multiplier (NorthJersey, disclosed as an attorney's FARS analysis in limitations), H1 2026 rate 1.02 (Reuters). Residual deduction: the DST figure's source is a plaintiff attorney's analysis, not peer-reviewed, though disclosed.

## 6. Research Rigor — 8.8/10
Original contribution is real: the 45 mph commonality across all nine streets (no source framed it as the pattern), plus computed rates — one death every 11.7 days across the nine streets, top four streets holding 51.7% of deaths, October at 17.2% of the year's valley pedestrian deaths, 80% of early-October deaths after dark. Counterargument stated at full strength (pedestrian error, exposure vs danger, improving year trend). Limitations are explicit: no per-street traffic volumes, counts from PedSafe Vegas not an independent FARS pull, October count varies by report date, DST multiplier source caveat.

## 7. Data Presentation — 8.6/10
Pull stat (172) with a clear label. Rates are computed in prose with windows stated. Deduction: no table, but only four of nine streets have published individual counts, so a full table would invent data. The prose table (Flamingo 29, Boulder 23, Charleston 22, Lake Mead 15) covers what exists. All numbers carry their window (Jan 2021–Jun 2026).
