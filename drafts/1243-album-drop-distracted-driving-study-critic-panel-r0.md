## 6. Research Rigor — 9.3
Original contribution: the derived scale framing (182 deaths ≈ 1.5 days of national toll; 74% male share of the surge; annualized-equivalent thought experiment labeled as such), and the editorial framing that the sober-driver concentration makes this the mirror image of the site's impairment-floor thesis. Limitations are dedicated and honest (no phone observed; exposure alternative; fatal-only; Spotify top-200 instrument; exploratory subgroups; 2017-2022 window). The strongest counterargument is stated at full strength: an exposure effect (more young men driving on release Fridays) fits every subgroup pattern with zero distracted glances, and VMT was never measured. The authors' response is fairly presented. Methodology is transparent: event study, ±10-day windows, four fixed-effect adjustments, cluster-robust SEs, three falsification tests.
