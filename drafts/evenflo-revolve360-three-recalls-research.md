# Research: Evenflo Revolve360 — Three Recalls in 12 Months

## Core Angle
Evenflo's Revolve360 rotating car seat line has been recalled THREE times in 12 months. The first fix was tape. The company has a documented pattern going back to the 2020 ProPublica investigation of the Big Kid booster.

## Timeline of Revolve360 Recalls

### 1. July 2025 — Canada Compliance Failure
- **What:** Revolve360 Slim failed Canadian chest acceleration requirements in rear-facing mode when installed with seat belt and tether
- **Scope:** 18,813 units (Canada only)
- **Transport Canada recall #2025-372**
- **Source:** Transport Canada TP 14566, Public Notice 2025-P02 E

### 2. September 2025 — Choking Hazard (Foam)
- **What:** 324,000 Revolve360 Slim seats recalled — headrest foam not adequately secured, children could pick off pieces and choke
- **Scope:** 324,000 (US and Canada)
- **Reports:** 11 reports, 8 children ingested foam
- **Remedy:** TAPE. Evenflo mailed adhesive tape seals.
- **Source:** NHTSA recall, Beacon Journal (Sep 30 2025)

### 3. July 2026 — Headrest Shifts in Crash
- **What:** 59,661 Reo by Revolve360 seats recalled — headrest shifts position during crash in positions 10-11
- **Scope:** 59,661 (US, plus Canada)
- **Discovery:** Dynamic testing with 3-year-old dummy
- **Remedy:** UNDER DEVELOPMENT. Parents told to stop using positions 10-11.
- **Source:** NHTSA recall (Jul 17, 2026), USA Today (Jul 23, 2026)

## Historical Context: ProPublica (2020)
- Evenflo marketed Big Kid booster as "SIDE IMPACT TESTED"
- Internal test videos showed dummies thrown far out of shoulder belts
- Company's test: only way to fail was if dummy ended up on floor or seat broke
- Jillian Brown, age 5, 37 lbs, suffered "internal decapitation" in Big Kid booster (2016)
- Congressional investigation followed (2020)
- MDL consolidated in Massachusetts
- NHTSA mandated by Congress in 2000 to create side-impact standards for child seats — still hasn't done it (26 years later)

## Key Claim
383,000+ seats recalled from one product line in 12 months. Three different failure modes. The first remedy was tape. For a child seat.
