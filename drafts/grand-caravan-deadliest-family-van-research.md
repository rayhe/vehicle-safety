# Research: The Grand Caravan — Deadliest Family Van in America

## Angle
The Dodge Grand Caravan is the deadliest minivan in FARS by both total deaths AND per-mile rate. Families trusted it for 30 years. IIHS now can't give ANY minivan a safety award. The within-class safety gap is 7x.

## FARS Data (Primary Source)

### Grand Caravan vs All Minivans
| Vehicle | Deaths | Rate/100M VMT | Crashes | Fleet | Aggressor Ratio |
|---------|--------|--------------|---------|-------|-----------------|
| Dodge Grand Caravan | 1,782 | 1.33 | 3,085 | 1,137,500 | 1.73 |
| Chrysler Town & Country | 1,303 | 1.26 | 2,298 | — | 1.76 |
| Honda Odyssey | 864 | 0.93 | 2,028 | — | 2.35 |
| Toyota Sienna | 430 | 0.49 | 905 | — | 2.10 |
| Chrysler Pacifica | 160 | 0.19 | 327 | — | 2.04 |

### Grand Caravan Toxicology
- 2,627 fatal crash drivers tested
- 15.3% impaired (any substance)
- 11.2% alcohol positive
- 7.0% drug positive
- This is LOWER than average — mostly sober family drivers dying

### Model Year Peak: 2005 Grand Caravan (192 deaths)
- Even MY 2019 had 63 deaths
- Vehicle kept killing right up to its 2020 discontinuation

## IIHS 2026 Context
- Zero minivans earned TSP or TSP+ in 2026
- IIHS President Harkey: "It's disappointing that minivans continue to struggle to provide the best available protection for passengers in the back, considering that these are supposed to be family vehicles."
- 35 of 45 TSP+ winners were SUVs

## Novel Contribution
The within-class minivan rate spread is 7x (Grand Caravan 1.33 vs Pacifica 0.19). This is one of the widest intra-class gaps in FARS. For context:
- Sedan class spread: ~50x (Impala 5.0 vs low-rate sedans)
- SUV class spread: ~30x+ (Land Cruiser 6.27 vs low-rate SUVs)
- But the minivan class is SUPPOSED to be the family-safe choice, making a 7x gap within it especially damning

## Strongest Counterargument
Fleet age bias. The Grand Caravan's fleet includes models from 1996-2020 — many are 20+ years old and lack modern safety features. The Pacifica only launched in 2017. Comparing a vehicle with 25 years of production to one with 6 years introduces systematic bias toward the older vehicle having higher rates. HOWEVER:
- The Sienna has a similarly long production run (1998-present) and still has a rate of 0.49 — 2.7x safer
- The Grand Caravan's 2014-2019 model years ALONE generated 437 deaths — newer vehicles still killing
- The Town & Country (mechanically identical, same platform, different badge) has a similar rate of 1.26

## Limitations
- FARS only captures fatal crashes — the 1,782 deaths are a fraction of total Caravan crash injuries
- Rate calculation uses VMT estimates, not actual odometer data — ±15% uncertainty for high-volume models
- The Grand Caravan was discontinued in 2020; this analysis covers its death toll through 2023, not a currently-sold vehicle
- Pacifica's low rate may partly reflect recency (newer fleet = more modern safety features)

## Actionable Takeaway
If you're shopping for a used minivan on a budget: the Grand Caravan's low price tag hides a 2.7x higher fatality rate than the Sienna and a 7x higher rate than the Pacifica. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — the 2005-2010 Grand Caravans have accumulated multiple recall campaigns.
