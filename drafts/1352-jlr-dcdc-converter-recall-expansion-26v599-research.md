# Research Notes: JLR DC-DC Converter Recall Expansion 26V599

## Slug: 1352-jlr-dcdc-converter-recall-expansion-26v599
## Journalist: Clara Rollover (Consumer Safety Advocate)
## Kicker: Investigation
## Article number: 1352

## Core Angle
In April 2026, JLR recalled 170,169 mild-hybrid SUVs because a DC-DC converter fault could kill drive power AND every exterior light at once. The recall did not cover them all. On September 17, 2026, JLR filed NHTSA campaign 26V599 (JLR number E008) adding 23,677 more vehicles with a later converter specification, after the company used field-data analysis plus AI modeling to find the ones the first recall missed. Combined, nearly 194,000 JLR mild hybrids now carry a defect JLR estimates at 100% prevalence in the population. The failure cascade takes about ten seconds: dash warning, transmission drops to neutral, lights cut out, engine shuts off.

## Kill Test
Is this genuinely newsworthy? YES.
- Novel angle vs. #976 (26V248, April): this is a distinct new federal campaign (26V599), not a rehash. The novel reporting detail is the AI modeling: JLR trained a model on the known failure population and used it to scope a later-generation converter spec, admitting the first recall's population boundary was drawn from an older hardware spec.
- Original contribution: per-model breakdown (Range Rover Sport 10,973; Defender 8,100), the ten-second failure sequence from the federal filing, the interim-vs-permanent remedy split (software now, permanent fix still in development, second letter promised), and the owner-notification timeline (letters Nov. 13, 2026).
- Consumer stakes: 100% estimated defect rate means no "probably fine" cars in the population. If you own an affected 2019-2024 JLR mild hybrid, the interim software update is the only thing standing between you and a dark, powerless highway stop.

## Actionable Insights (required)
- If you own a 2019-2024 JLR mild hybrid (Range Rover, Defender, Discovery, Jaguar F-Pace/E-Pace), run your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls now; do not wait for the Nov 13 letter
- If the red "Stop Safely Electrical Fault Detected" warning appears: pull over and stop safely immediately; you have roughly ten seconds before drive power and lights go

- When the second letter arrives for the permanent fix, treat it as urgent, not routine
- Buying a used 2019-2024 JLR mild hybrid: confirm both 26V248 and 26V599 are closed on that specific VIN before money moves
