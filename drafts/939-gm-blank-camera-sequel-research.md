# Research: Article #939 — GM 300K backup-camera recall (26V539000)

**Journalist:** Clara Rollover (consumer advocate; beat = car-buying guidance)
**Status:** RESEARCH complete 2026-09-19

## The story
On September 17, 2026, NHTSA published Campaign **26V539000**: General Motors is recalling **more than 300,000** compact SUVs because the rearview camera can display a distorted or blank image. Affected: **2024-2025 Buick Envista, 2024-2026 Buick Encore GX, 2024-2026 Chevrolet Trailblazer, 2025 Chevrolet Trax**. Dealer inspect-and-replace, free. Owner letters mailed from September 16, 2026. GM internal number A262554900.

## Verified facts (primary sources)
1. **NHTSA campaign record (api.nhtsa.gov, fetched 2026-09-19):** Component = "BACK OVER PREVENTION: SENSING SYSTEM: CAMERA". Report received 08/20/2026. Consequence: "A rearview image that does not display correctly reduces the driver's view behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash." Remedy NOT over-the-air (overTheAirUpdate: false) — requires a dealer visit. VINs searchable on NHTSA.gov since August 20, 2026.
2. **Same fleet, same symptom, 2023:** NHTSA 23V744000 (Nov 6, 2023) recalled 2024 Encore GX / Envista / Trax for Virtual Cockpit Unit software that could blank the *instrument panel* at startup or while driving. That one could be fixed OTA. Two blank-screen recalls, same entry-level crossovers, three model years apart.
3. **The mandate works — when the screen works:** UTHealth Houston study presented at the AAP National Conference, Denver, September 2025 (researcher Dr. Natalie Drucker): after the 2018 FMVSS 111 backup-camera mandate, pediatric backover trauma cases fell from 7.2 to 2.7 per year (rate ratio 0.38, p<.01), severe injuries halved, and child fatalities dropped **78%** (trauma registry) / 60% (public records) among children under 5.
4. **NHTSA baseline (final rule, March 31, 2014; full compliance May 1, 2018):** ~210 fatalities and 15,000 injuries per year from backover crashes. Children under 5 account for 31% of backover fatalities; adults 70+ for 26%. If the backing vehicle is a pickup, minivan, or SUV, a backover is 4x more likely to be fatal.
5. **Context:** LawyerTrend's Sept 17-18, 2026 PI briefing reports 300,000+ affected VINs, GM has published no repair-completion timeline, and notes 2026 is an unusually dense recall year (44 NHTSA campaigns through Sept 17, first formal defect recall order in decades issued May 2026, Ford attached do-not-drive warnings to two campaigns).

## The angle (kill test)
The backup camera is the single most successful child-safety mandate of the last decade: 78% fewer dead kids. But the safety device is now a failure point *on the exact cheap crossovers young families buy* — the Envista/Trax start under $25K and are first-car / young-family staples. A blank camera returns the driver to 2017-era rear visibility with 2026-era confidence. And this is the second blank-screen recall on the same nameplates. Nobody is reporting on the 2023-then-2026 blank-screen pattern. Passes: fresh (Sept 17), data-backed, genuinely new angle, actionable (VIN check, dealer fix free).

## What we do NOT claim
- No injuries tied to this defect are reported in any source I found — do not invent any.
- Unit count "300,000+" is reported by LawyerTrend's briefing, not stated in the NHTSA API record — attribute as reported.
