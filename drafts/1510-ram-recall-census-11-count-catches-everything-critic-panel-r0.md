## 3. Ethics Reviewer — 9.0/10

No victims to exploit and none are invented: the one potentially related injury in the seat-belt recall is stated plainly and never inflated into drama, and the zero-injury camera and headlamp recalls are never softened into reassurance either. No owner is named. The article tells readers what to do instead of what to fear, and it refuses the easy scare line even though "1.27 million trucks with loose seat belts" would have supported one. The one ethical pressure point — publishing a recall story whose biggest defect estimate is 0.1 percent — is answered inside the article itself by the counterargument paragraph and the "no symptom" framing, which is the honest reason the small percentage still matters.

No case law needed; no statutory claims made beyond FMVSS numbers, which match their filings.
