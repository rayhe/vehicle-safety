# Research Notes: NHTSA Defect Petition Denial Pattern

## Article #761 — "petition-denied-escape-hatch"
## Journalist: Rex Driverton
## Date: 2026-08-16

## Angle
NHTSA denied two defect petitions in two weeks (Jul 24 and Aug 6, 2026), using different bureaucratic escape routes each time. The defect petition process under 49 USC 30162 / 49 CFR 552 is supposed to give citizens a safety check on the agency. In practice, it's a suggestion box with a legal obligation to explain why the suggestion was ignored. Three recent denials, three different excuses — and the legal 4-month decision deadline is routinely missed.

## Primary Sources

### 1. Tesla Door Handle Petition Denial (DP25-002)
- **Filed:** November 7, 2025 by Kevin Clouse
- **Denied:** July 24, 2026 (FR Doc 2026-15010)
- **Federal Register:** Vol 91, No 141, Pages 46827-46828
- **Docket:** NHTSA-2026-1553
- **Subject:** ~180,000 MY 2022 Tesla Model 3 — mechanical door release "not readily accessible and clearly identifiable," fails to comply with FMVSS 206
- **Denial reason:** "unlikely that additional investigation would result in finding that a defect related to motor vehicle safety exists, and the issue is best addressed through the rulemaking process"
- **Key detail:** NHTSA simultaneously GRANTED a separate petition to begin a rulemaking for new egress standard
- **Deaths cited in coverage:** 15 deaths linked to electronic door handle entrapment (broader issue)
- **Time from filing to denial:** ~8.5 months (legal deadline: 4 months per 49 USC 30162)
- Source: https://regulations.justia.com/regulations/fedreg/2026/07/24/2026-15010.html

### 2. Acura AWD Petition Denial (DP26-001)
- **Filed:** January 9, 2026 by Christopher Mammarella
- **Denied:** August 6, 2026 (FR Doc 2026-16019)
- **Federal Register:** Published Aug 6, 2026
- **Subject:** 137,329 MY 2021-2023 Acura TLX and MY 2022-2023 MDX — front drivetrain splines failing, permanent loss of AWD, FWD-only
- **Denial reason:** "not likely to result in a finding that a defect related to motor vehicle safety exists"
- **Key detail:** 48 complaints cited by petitioner. ODI opened defect petition (DP26-001) on March 6, 2026 to evaluate.
- **Deaths/injuries:** Zero crashes, injuries, or fatalities
- **Time from filing to denial:** ~7 months (legal deadline: 4 months)
- Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/06/2026-16019/denial-of-motor-vehicle-defect-petition

### 3. Side Underride Guard Petition Denial (DP21-004)
- **Filed:** September 14, 2021
- **Denied:** ~2022 (FR Doc appeared on transportation.gov)
- **Subject:** Van-type and box semi-trailers lacking side underride guards
- **Denial reason:** "the issues presented by the petitioners will be examined in work undertaken pursuant to congressional direction under the Bipartisan Infrastructure Law"
- **Key detail:** Punted to Congress's existing mandate rather than opening investigation
- Source: https://www.transportation.gov/regulations/federal-register-documents/2022-14165

### 4. AP Review of NHTSA Petition Timeliness (2014)
- AP reviewed all 15 petitions filed since 2010
- Agency missed the legal 4-month deadline 12 times (80%)
- One 2012 petition was STILL unresolved at time of AP report
- In 8 petitions, it took more than a year to resolve
- There is NO penalty when NHTSA misses its own deadlines (but automakers get fined for missing theirs)
- Source: AP/manufacturing.net, 2014

## Novel Contribution
No existing article on vehicle-safety.org covers the defect petition PROCESS as a systemic failure. We have 3 articles touching individual petition denials (door handles, Acura drivetrain) but none analyzing the PATTERN: the process itself is designed to deny.

Three escape hatches:
1. "Best addressed through rulemaking" → punts to multi-year regulatory process
2. "Not likely to result in a safety defect finding" → straight denial
3. "Will be examined pursuant to congressional direction" → punts to Congress

The petition docket numbers (DP25-002, DP26-001) suggest very few petitions are filed per year (~2-5), meaning each one should get serious treatment. Instead, they're all denied.

## Limitations
- Cannot determine the total historical denial rate without a comprehensive dataset
- Docket numbering system doesn't clearly reveal how many petitions have ever been filed
- Some petitions may have been granted and led to investigations/recalls — this article focuses on the denial pattern
- The 4-month deadline may be impractical for complex engineering analysis

## Strongest Counterargument
The petition process DID work in one sense with the Tesla case: NHTSA denied the specific defect petition but granted a rulemaking petition, meaning the underlying safety concern was acknowledged. The process may be designed to route issues to the correct regulatory tool (enforcement vs. rulemaking) rather than simply denying them. However, rulemaking takes years to decades, during which the underlying hazard persists.
