# Research: 1360 — Lexus NX Instrument Cluster Warning-Display Recall

## Story
Toyota (TMMNA) recalled 68,356 model-year 2022-2024 Lexus NX250, NX350, NX350 Hybrid, and NX450h+ crossovers because the instrument panel cluster combination meter may fail to display certain warnings or indicators. NHTSA Campaign ID: 26V687000. Lexus recall number: 26LA10. Reported in "NHTSA Recalls Oct. 26-30" roundup, published October 30, 2026.

## The defect
- The combination meter (the digital instrument cluster) may fail to show warnings/indicators such as engine oil pressure, engine coolant temperature, or electrical charge.
- Consequence: driver is not warned of a fault condition that the vehicle is designed to surface. A loss of oil pressure or overheating can proceed with no dashboard alert.
- Legal hook: noncompliance with FMVSS No. 101, "Controls and Displays" - the federal standard that mandates certain telltales be visible to the driver.

## Remedy / timeline
- Dealer updates the combination meter software, free of charge.
- Owner notification letters expected to be mailed December 7, 2026.
- Owners can contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331 and reference recall 26LA10.
- VINs searchable at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
