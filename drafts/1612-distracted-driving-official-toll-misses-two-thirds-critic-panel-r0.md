## 6. Research Rigor — 8.8

Deductions: the "roughly 6,300" extrapolation is conditional and labeled, but it leans on applying the police-report role split to the naturalistic total, which assumes the split is method-invariant — the article says "that same arithmetic would put", which is honest, but the assumption is not flagged.
