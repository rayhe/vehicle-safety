# Research: 1116 — GM Killed the Impala in 2020. It Was Already the Deadliest American Sedan on the Road.

## Angle
The Chevrolet Impala (3,774 FARS deaths, rate 5.0/100M VMT) is the deadliest American sedan in the FARS dataset and the 6th-worst rate of any model, with only 21.4% of its drivers impaired. Sixty-two percent of its model-year deaths came from the 2006-2014 model years, exactly the cars GM later recalled because the ignition switch could shut off the engine mid-drive and disable the airbags. GM killed the nameplate in February 2020 without ever answering for the body count.

## Primary Sources
1. **NHTSA FARS (2014-2023) via fars_output.js** — Chevrolet Impala: 3,774 deaths, 5,594 crashes, fleet 656,250, rate 5.00/100M VMT. Impairment: 21.4% any-impaired (905 alc / 557 drug of 5,807 drivers). Model-year peak: 2008 (401), 2007 (381), 2006 (355).
2. **NHTSA recall 14V355000 (GM recall 14299)** — Announced June 23, 2014. 3,141,731 vehicles including the 2006-2014 Chevrolet Impala. Defect: key weight or jarring events can move the ignition switch out of run, killing engine power, power steering, power brakes, and airbag deployment. Fix: key-ring inserts and covers.
3. **U.S. Department of Justice** — September 17, 2015: GM paid $900M under a three-year deferred prosecution agreement (conspiracy + wire fraud charges, dismissable on compliance) over the concealed ignition-switch defect; 124 deaths officially attributed. CEO Mary Barra: "We didn't do our jobs."
4. **Detroit News / Hagerty / GM Authority** — The last Chevrolet Impala, a Cajun Red Premier, rolled off the Detroit-Hamtramck line on February 27, 2020. The plant was retooled for EVs.

## FARS Data Points
- Impala: 3,774 deaths, rate 5.0 (6th worst of 337 models; deadliest American sedan)
- Nissan Maxima: 5.11 (only deadlier sedan, already covered in #1002); Chevy Cobalt 5.10 (same family of defect)
- For comparison: Kia Forte (best sedan) 0.40 — the Impala's rate is 12.5x; Toyota Camry 2.03 (2.5x)
- Impairment: 21.4% (sober drivers dying — the car, not the driver, is the story)
- Deaths per crash: 3,774/5,594 = 0.675
- Recalled-years (2006-2014) share: 2,328/3,732 = 62.4%
- Peak years 2006-2008: 1,137 deaths in three model years

## Key Timeline
- 2003-2004: GM documents the ignition-switch defect internally
- 2006-2008: Impala's deadliest model years (355/381/401 FARS deaths)
- June 23, 2014: NHTSA campaign 14V355000 — Impala 2006-2014 recalled, 3.14M vehicles
- Sept 17, 2015: GM pays $900M, enters deferred prosecution; Barra apologizes
- Feb 27, 2020: Final Impala built in Detroit-Hamtramck; nameplate dies

## Actionable Takeaway
If you own or shop a 2006-2014 Impala: check that recall 14299 (14V355000) was actually performed, at nhtsa.gov/recalls with your VIN.
