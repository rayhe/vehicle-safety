- Length (577 words) is the only structural criticism; the final "What to do" paragraph could lose one clause.
- Voice scaffolding ("Start with the part," "Fairness demands the other side") is shared site furniture, not Clara-specific.
- Headline precision: 2004-model cars are 22 years old; only the 2005s are 21. The lede clarifies "the youngest car on the list rolled off the line in September 2005," so the headline reads as the youngest cars' age. Acceptable, but a literalist could object.
- "Six weeks" from Nov 20 to Jan 4 is 45 days, closer to six and a half weeks. Approximation, not an error.
- Research-note nit (not in the article): "21 years and 2 months" for the youngest car to the recall is 21 years, 1.5 months (Sept 21, 2005 to Nov 5, 2026). Rounding; the article itself states no duration.
