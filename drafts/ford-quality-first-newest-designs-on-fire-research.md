# Research: Ford Quality-First Campaign vs July 2026 Recall Reality

## Angle
Ford's spokesman Mike Levine says July 2026 recalls "mainly impact older-designed vehicles." The data says otherwise: 953,602 of the ~1 million recalled in the final week of July are from Ford's CURRENT-generation designs (Bronco 2021+, Explorer 2020+, Aviator 2020+). The "older-designed vehicles" defense is empirically false.

## Primary Sources (3+)

### 1. NHTSA Recall Notices (July 2026)
- **July 24:** 565,691 Ford Bronco/Bronco Raptor (MY 2021-2026) — wiring harness fire risk
  - 15 fire reports between Oct 2024 and Jun 2026
  - 7 fires in Bronco Raptors, 8 in base Broncos
  - No injuries reported
  - Remedy: dealer installs sheathing over wiring
  - Source: NHTSA via Reuters (Jul 24)
- **July 20:** 387,911 Ford Explorer (MY 2020-2026) / Lincoln Aviator (MY 2020-2027) — seats unlatch while driving
  - 14 reports of unintended seat movement while in drive
  - 6 of those 14 were vehicles ALREADY RECALLED AND FIXED ONCE
  - No crashes/injuries reported
  - Remedy: replace second-row easy-entry switch bezel
  - Source: NHTSA via NY Post (Jul 22)
- **Combined week total:** 953,602 vehicles
- **Combined July total:** "close to 2 million" (per USA Today Jul 27)

### 2. Ford JD Power Quality Campaign
- JD Power Initial Quality Survey: Ford #1 among mainstream brands (released June 25)
- Campaign launched July 4: "We put quality first. Now Ford is first in quality."
- NOTE: JD Power IQS measures problems in FIRST 90 DAYS of ownership. Recalls are often for systemic design issues that manifest later.

### 3. FARS Data (2014-2023)
- Ford Explorer: 3,797 deaths, rate 1.54 per 100M VMT, #2 Ford death toll
- Ford Bronco: 25 deaths, rate 0.05 (too new for meaningful data)
- Ford total: 34,954 deaths across 29 models
- Explorer model year breakdown shows 2020+ is still early in the death curve

### 4. Ford Spokesman Quote
- Mike Levine: "despite the recent recalls, Ford is making progress on its quality problems, that mainly impact older-designed vehicles"
- Source: USA Today/Detroit Free Press (Jul 25-27)

## Original Contribution
Computing the "current-generation" percentage of Ford's July 2026 recalls. The Bronco (launched 2021) and current Explorer (6th gen, launched 2020) are both current-generation designs. 953,602 of the ~953,602 recalled in the final week are from vehicles designed in the last 6 years. This directly contradicts the "older-designed vehicles" claim.

Also: the Explorer seat recall is Ford's SECOND recall for the same defect. Six of fourteen complaints came from already-fixed vehicles. When the fix doesn't work, "recall" becomes a hollow word.

## Counterargument
Ford could argue that even current-generation vehicles will have teething issues, and that the JD Power IQS win shows progress in initial build quality. The recall rate may indeed be improving year-over-year as Ford claims. The counterargument has merit — recalls ≠ crashes, and finding problems early can prevent deaths. But the scale (2M in one month) and the repeat failure (6 of 14 already fixed) undermine it.

## Limitations
- FARS data for the Bronco (25 deaths) is insufficient for rate analysis — too new
- JD Power IQS measures different things than recall severity
- Recall volume doesn't directly correlate with fatality risk
- "Close to 2 million in July" includes recalls not detailed here
