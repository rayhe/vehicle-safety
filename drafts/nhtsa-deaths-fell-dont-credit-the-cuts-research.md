# Research: NHTSA Deaths Fell. Don't Credit the Cuts.

## Thesis
NHTSA lost 25% of its staff via DOGE. Fatalities dropped to 0.99/100M VMT in Q1 2026 — a near-historic low. Deregulators will conflate these. The deaths falling now are the harvest of regulations enacted 5-15 years ago (ESC mandate 2012, voluntary AEB adoption 2016+, IIHS-driven design improvements). The staff cuts happening now will show up as increased deaths in 3-5 years, when the rulemakings that didn't happen would have been taking effect. You're eating the crops planted by the previous farmer while salting the fields.

## Primary Sources

- Americans drove 11 billion more miles than Q1 2025
- 30 states + Puerto Rico saw declines
- 2025 speeding fatalities: 10,035 (11% decline, return to pre-pandemic 2019 levels)
- Source: NHTSA NCSA

- IIHS: ESC reduces fatal single-vehicle crash risk by 56%, fatal rollover risk by ~80%
- Lag: 5 years from rule to full fleet penetration, another 5-10 years for fleet turnover
- Current ESC benefit = harvest from 2007 rulemaking

## Novel Contribution
Cross-referencing the regulatory pipeline timeline with the fatality decline curve to show that TODAY's safety gains are the product of PAST regulatory actions, and current deregulation creates a 3-5 year lag before damage appears. Nobody has drawn this specific connection with data points from both sides.

## Strongest Counterargument
It's possible that technology adoption is now market-driven rather than regulation-driven. AEB was already on 90%+ of new cars before the mandate was finalized. Insurance Institute pressure (through Top Safety Pick requirements) may be a more powerful incentive than NHTSA mandates. If that's true, NHTSA's role is less critical than safety advocates claim.

## Limitations
- We can't prove a direct causal link between past regulation and current death declines (multiple factors)
- The 41% investigation drop is a single year of data; could be an artifact
- Q1 data is seasonal (winter = fewer miles in some states)
- The 3-5 year lag prediction is a model, not a proven timeline
