# 7-Critic Panel — GM L87 V8 Seizure (Article #731)

## 1. General Quality (9/10)
Strong headline with specific dollar figure and concrete claim. Lede drops you into the story immediately. Data integration is tight — 225 complaints, 17,227 FARS deaths, specific model years. The "recalled some, said the rest were fine, the rest are not fine" construction is a clean editorial punch. Slight ding: could use one more specific complaint narrative for texture.

## 2. Voice / Mia Crumplezone (9/10)
This is distinctly Mia — technically precise (connecting rods, cascade failure, differential speeds) but accessible, genuinely judgmental about the engineering ("when a federal investigator asks 'what changed between suppliers,' they're not curious"). The "$4,000 engine upgrade" framing is very consumer-reporter-meets-engineer. Could not swap this with Rex or Vin without noticing.

## 3. Ethics (9/10)
Accurately notes that FARS deaths cover all engine variants, not just L87 — the disclaimer is honest. Uses "a subset" and "may lose" appropriately rather than overstating. Directs readers to check their specific VINs. No fear-mongering beyond what NHTSA's own investigation warrants.

## 4. Social Impact (8.5/10)
Actionable: specific VIN check instructions, specific recall number, specific symptom to watch for. The "$150 tow" closing line is practical and memorable. Would benefit from mentioning whether loaner vehicles or alternative transport is available during the investigation period.

## 5. Legal Accuracy (9/10)
Correctly describes Engineering Analysis as NHTSA's "most serious investigation tier before it compels a recall." Accurately references Recall 25V274 and the exclusion. Does not claim GM is at fault — uses "building a case" and "something broke" which are appropriate for an open investigation. References the actual investigation document (EA25-007).

## 6. Rigor (8.5/10)
Three primary sources: NHTSA EA25-007, FARS data, Recall 25V274. Original cross-tab: connecting engine seizure complaint population to FARS death rates for the same vehicle models. Limitation acknowledged (FARS covers all variants, not just L87). Could be stronger on: what exactly Recall 25V274 covered (which model years, what the fix was) to make the "excluded" finding more precise.

## 7. Data Presentation (9/10)
Pull stat (225 complaints) is immediately contextualized. FARS integration adds weight without burying the reader in tables. Specific rates (2.49, 2.55 per 100M VMT) ground the claim. Good inline attribution to source documents.
