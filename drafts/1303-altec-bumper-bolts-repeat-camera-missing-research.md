## Verified facts (3+ primary/primary-adjacent sources)
- **October 2026 peg — CSN-3375:** Altec recalling certain 2023-2024
  Digger Derrick vehicles; front winch bumper may be secured with
  incorrect bolts, which can cause the bumper to detach and become a
  road hazard; bolts can also fail suddenly during winch operation.
  Potentially affects 45 vehicles. Remedy: install correct bolts.
  Owner letters mailed December 4. Altec customer service 877-462-5832.
  Source: Fleet Maintenance, Oct 27, 2026 NHTSA commercial roundup.
- **October 2026 peg — CSN-3376:** Altec recalling certain 2026 Service
  Body, Aerial Device, Digger Derrick, and AC Series Crane vehicles
  manufactured without a rearview camera installed; missing camera
  reduces driver view behind the vehicle. Potentially affects 1,053
  vehicles. Remedy: install a camera or provide a camera kit as
  necessary. Owner letters mailed December 4. Same customer-service
  number. Source: same Fleet Maintenance roundup.
- **August 2025 precedent — 25V529 / CSN-3244:** NHTSA acknowledgment
  letter (static.nhtsa.gov PDF) and Part 573 report: Altec recalled
  certain 2024-2025 Aerial Device and Digger Derrick vehicles, 124 units,
  for the identical defect — front winch bumper secured with incorrect
  bolts, bumper may detach, bolts may fail during winch operation.
  Part 573 detail: M12 bolts were used where 5/8-inch bolts belonged;
  production inspection July 10, 2025 found the error; remedy was bolt
  replacement; letters expected October 10, 2025.
- **Streak context — #1161 (this site, Oct 14, 2026 run):** Altec
  CSN-3374 (improperly welded platform-access step, 6,780 vehicles) and
  CSN-3370 (boom stow wiring, 143 vehicles) filed seven days apart,
  covered in the Oct 12 weekly roundup. New pair (3375 + 3376) arrived
  in the Oct 27 roundup. Four campaigns, 8,021 vehicles: 6,780 + 143 +
  45 + 1,053 = 8,021.
- **Also in the Oct 27 roundup (killed, not covered here):** Autocar
  Xpeditor 26V699 (1,339, Cummins L9 fuel-filter hoses), Ford
  speedometer 26V653 (102,159), Great Dane Champion 26V656 (69,
  do-not-use), Paccar bunk restraint 26PACH (310), Volvo VNL cab bolts
  RVXX2612 (5).

## What the filings do NOT say (limitations, stated in article)
- Neither the roundup nor the 2025 Part 573 report explains why the
  wrong-bolt bumper defect reappears for 2023-2024 trucks after the 2025
  recall covered 2024-2025 trucks: whether the first sweep missed an
  earlier model-year slice, or the same production error recurred. The
  article frames this as an open question, not a proven repeat error.
- No crashes, injuries, or fires are tied to CSN-3375 or CSN-3376 in
  the roundup; none are claimed. The camera recall is framed as a
  visibility gap, NOT as an FMVSS 111 violation: heavy work trucks
  above 10,000 lbs GVWR fall outside the light-vehicle rear-visibility
  mandate, and the NHTSA notice alleges no FMVSS noncompliance.
- NHTSA campaign numbers for CSN-3375/CSN-3376 were not stated in the
  roundup; the article cites Altec's CSN numbers only and does not
  invent campaign numbers.
