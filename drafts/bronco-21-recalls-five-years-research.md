# Research Notes: Bronco 21 Recalls in 5 Years

## Core Angle
The Ford Bronco has been recalled 21 times in 5 years of production. The latest: 565,691 vehicles recalled July 24, 2026 because a wiring harness in the engine compartment lacks adequate protection from abrasion, risking spontaneous fire. The fix: install a plastic sleeve over the wire. Ford discovers a new defect on the Bronco roughly every 87 days.

## Journalist
Mia Crumplezone — Safety Engineering Editor. Vehicle design analysis.

## Primary Sources

### 1. NHTSA / Reuters (Jul 24, 2026)
- Ford recalling 565,691 vehicles (2021-2026 Bronco and Bronco Raptor)
- Engine compartment wiring harness may become damaged from movement/abrasion against surrounding parts
- Lack of protection from abrasion could expose wires, increasing risk of short circuit
- Short circuit can create heat or spark → fire risk
- 15 reports filed between Oct 15, 2024 and Jun 20, 2026 of fires from engine compartment wiring harness
- 7 fires in Bronco Raptors, 8 in base Broncos
- **Zero injuries reported**
- Fix: dealers install sheathing over the wiring, free of charge
- Source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/ford-recall-more-than-565000-us-vehicles-over-engine-compartment-fire-risk-2026-07-24/

### 2. Autosafety.org — 2021 Ford Bronco Recall List
- **21 recalls** for the 2021 Ford Bronco model year
- Components: roof/pillars, transmission, windshield, engine, electrical, fuel system, seat frame
- Production started: September 2020
- Source: https://www.autosafety.org/vehicle-safety-check/2021-ford-bronco/

### 3. Ford Recall Volume Context (CarBuzz / Free Press)
- Ford issued 153 recalls in 2025 affecting 12.9 million vehicles (industry record)
- 2026 YTD through May: 36 recalls affecting ~10 million vehicles
- 2026 July alone: Ford recalled close to 2 million vehicles
- Ford launched "#1 in Quality" ad campaign July 4, 2026 based on JD Power IQS
- Source: https://carbuzz.com/ford-bronco-hardtop-recall-2026/
- Source: https://www.freep.com/story/money/cars/ford/2026/07/25/ford-quality-recalls-1-million-vehicles/91036169007/

### 4. FARS Data (Crash Report internal)
- Ford Bronco (modern): 25 deaths total, rate 0.05 per 100M VMT (lowest Ford vehicle)
- Note: Most of these 25 deaths are from the LEGACY Bronco (pre-1996), not the modern 2021+ model
- The modern Bronco hasn't accumulated significant FARS data yet (too new)
- For context: Ford Mustang rate=6.02, Ranger=2.91, Explorer=1.54, F-150=1.04

## Original Analysis
- Production start: ~September 2020
- Time in production: ~58 months (to July 2026)
- 21 recalls ÷ 58 months = **one new defect discovered every 83 days**
- That's ~2.8 days of production on the line for every recall issued
- Bronco Raptor starts at ~$80,000; base Bronco ~$38,000
- A wiring sheath costs <$1 in automotive supply chain
- The 565,691 vehicles cover essentially the ENTIRE production run (2021-2026)

## Defect Variety
The 21 recalls cover completely different systems:
- Roof panels separating at highway speed (Webasto supplier)
- Windshield detaching
- Transmission-to-transfer-case joint misalignment  
- Engine valve cracking (EcoBoost intake valves)
- Block heater short circuit
- Seat frame pivot bolt loosening
- Infotainment/rearview camera
- **Now: wiring harness fire risk**

This isn't one recurring problem. It's a different system every time. The Bronco isn't failing in one way — it's failing in every way.

## Counterargument
More recalls ≠ less safe. High recall volume can indicate better defect surveillance, not worse engineering. Tesla had similar recall density early on. And the Bronco has zero FARS deaths attributable to the modern model. The recall system is catching problems before they kill anyone. That's the system working.

## Limitations
- The "21 recalls" count is for MY2021 specifically; some recalls cover other Ford models too (shared platforms/suppliers)
- Not all 21 recalls are Bronco-specific; some are shared with Bronco Sport, Explorer, Ranger
- The Bronco is too new for meaningful FARS fatality data
- "One every 83 days" is an average; recall timing is clustered, not evenly distributed
- The comparison to Ford's total recall volume conflates model-specific issues with company-wide problems
