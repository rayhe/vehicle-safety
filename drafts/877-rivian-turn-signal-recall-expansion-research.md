## Angle
Rivian is recalling its turn-signal recall. Fourteen months after filing campaign 25V-387 for Myotek-supplied front turn-signal lamps that could fail to illuminate, Rivian filed campaign 26V-508 (Aug 5, 2026) admitting the supplier failed to properly quarantine the suspect parts — so the defective lamps got installed in another 3,829 vehicles anyway. The containment plan didn't contain. This is the 10th recall for the 2025 R1S and the 2nd for the 2026 R1S. The absurdity loop is the story: a recall existed to contain bad lamps; the containment failed; now you need a recall for the recall.

## Kill test
- **Novel?** Yes. Site has two Rivian stories (rivian-every-link-broke, rivian-toe-link-investigation-115k); zero turn-signal coverage (grep turns up only passing mentions in unrelated pieces). The supplier-quarantine-failure mechanism is new territory for the site.
- **Newsworthy?** Yes. Filed Aug 5, 2026; remedy available Aug 11; owner letters go out by Sept 29, 2026 — the notification wave is ~19 days out. Rivian ranked last among 26 brands in Consumer Reports predicted reliability; raised delivery guidance to 65,000-70,000.
- **Data angle?** Yes. 3,204 R1S (built June 12, 2024-May 26, 2026) + 625 R1T (built Oct 1, 2024-Mar 19, 2026) = 3,829 new vehicles; combined total with 25V-387 = 31,711. Rivian estimates only 0.6% of the expansion population actually has the defect (fewer than 1% per RivianTrackr). 17 field failures identified June 2025-July 2026. No crashes, injuries, or fatalities. Warning: cluster message "Turn signal lights not working. Service it soon"; remaining signals blink at double speed with faster click sounds.
