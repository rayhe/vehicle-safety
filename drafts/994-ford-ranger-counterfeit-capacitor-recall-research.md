# Research: #994 — Ford Ranger Counterfeit-Capacitor Recall (26V605)

## Story
Ford recalled 11,405 brand-new 2026 Rangers because a capacitor on the infotainment
screen's circuit board may be COUNTERFEIT. The fake part cracks, shorts, blows a fuse,
blacks out the SYNC 4 screen, and kills the federally mandated backup camera.
NHTSA says the trucks fail FMVSS 111 "Rear Visibility." The remedy is under
development — Ford has no fix yet. Interim letters mail Oct 5.

## Verified facts (do not invent others)
- Campaign: NHTSA 26V605; Ford 26C42. Components: BACK OVER PREVENTION:DISPLAY FUNCTION.
- Vehicles: certain 2026 Ford Ranger with 10.1-inch center stack; built Apr 21, 2026 –
  Sep 3, 2026. 11,405 units.
- Failure chain per report: cracked MLCC -> electrical short on PCBA -> open display
  fuse -> loss of power -> blank/black SYNC 4 screen -> no rear-view camera image in
  reverse. Result: fails FMVSS 111, "Rear Visibility."
- Internal analysis: MLCC "contains materials suspected to be counterfeit and fails
  to meet engineering specifications."
- Supplier named in report: SUZHOU DONGSHAN DISPLAY INC. (China).
- Ford first aware: August 2026.
- Remedy: under development. Interim owner letters expected mailed Oct 5, 2026;
  additional letters when remedy available. Ford customer service: 1-866-436-7332.
- Context (attributed): Autoblog (Oct 2025) reported Ford paid a $165M NHTSA civil
  penalty in Nov 2024 for delayed backup-camera recalls, and that Ford had already
  broken GM's 2014 annual-recall record in H1 2026. Use as context only, attributed.
