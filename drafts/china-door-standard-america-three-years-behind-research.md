# Research: China Has Already Fixed the Door Problem America Won't Touch Until 2030

## Angle
China finalized its GB 48001-2026 standard in 2025, mandating mechanical door releases on ALL new vehicles starting 2027. Hong Kong is banning new EVs with electronic-only handles. Meanwhile, the US has NO federal standard covering emergency door release labeling or location. NHTSA just agreed on July 24, 2026 to START the rulemaking process — with no compliance date expected before 2030. The result: Chinese consumers will have safer car doors than Americans for at least 3 years.
