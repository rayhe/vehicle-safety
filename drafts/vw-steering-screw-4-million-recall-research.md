# Research: VW steering-screw recall (draft slug: vw-steering-screw-4-million-recall)

## Journalist: Clara Rollover (Consumer Safety Advocate — car-buying guidance, "what this means for you")
## Kill test: PASSES
- Genuinely newsworthy: VW Group's biggest recall since Dieselgate, ~4.64M vehicles across 4 brands, one corroding bolt can take out steering.
- Novel angle: the asymmetry — a single fastener (~1% of cars expected to actually exhibit it) triggers a 4.64M-car precautionary recall; VW knew since Oct 2024 and only classified it safety-relevant in Sep 2026; the ONLY driver warning sign is a metallic rattle.

## Verified facts

1. **Global scale:** ~4.64 million vehicles across Volkswagen, Audi, Skoda, Seat — recall announced early October 2026. Biggest VW Group recall since Dieselgate. (particle.news overview, Oct 2026; Reuters via Handelsblatt, Sep 25 2026)
2. **US campaign:** NHTSA campaign **26V590**, 208,724 US vehicles: 2018 VW Tiguan (85,713), 2018-2019 VW Atlas (82,601), 2019-2021 Audi Q3 (40,410). (Reuters, Sep 18 2026; MotorBiscuit citing NHTSA filing)
3. **Defect:** long-term corrosion of the steering-gear/rack-to-subframe mounting screw. If the screw or its head fails, the steering connection is impaired; in rare cases loss of steering. Corrosion progresses over years. (particle.news; NHTSA notice via Reuters)
4. **Timeline:** first corroded-screw case identified October 2024; VW began investigating then; classified as potentially safety-relevant September 2026; recall decision followed. ~2 years between first case and recall. (Osna.FM citing Handelsblatt/NHTSA report)
5. **Defect rate:** US authorities estimate roughly **1% of covered cars** may actually exhibit the defect (~46,000 of 4.64M worldwide). VW: only a small number of individual damage cases found; **no personal injuries reported to date**. (particle.news; Osna.FM)
6. **Geography:** Germany ~950,000 VW/Audi/Seat + ~370,000 Skoda (Skoda ~1.72M worldwide). KBA estimates >2.16M VW, ~700,000 Audi, >1M Skoda, ~200,000 Seat. Under KBA and NHTSA oversight. (businessday.co.za/Reuters; particle.news)
7. **Warning sign:** NHTSA filing's only warning sign is "unusual metallic rattling or knocking noises originating from the front axle area." The housing fracture "may not necessarily be immediately apparent to the driver." Risk most likely while parking/at low speed when steering loads are highest. Salted winter roads accelerate corrosion, but recall applies nationwide. (mylemonrights.com citing NHTSA filing)
8. **Remedy:** dealers replace the right-side steering rack mounting bolt with a corrosion-resistant polymer-coated one, free. Takes under an hour. VINs searchable since Sep 16, 2026; owner letters mailed Nov 10, 2026. Parts shortages and constrained service capacity expected. (mylemonrights.com; particle.news)
9. **US exclusions:** models like the Golf, Touran and Caddy are not part of the US recall. (mylemonrights.com/Reuters)

## Consumer angle (Clara)
- If you own a 2018 Tiguan, 2018-2019 Atlas, or 2019-2021 Q3: check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls NOW, don't wait for the November letter.
- Listen for the rattle. It's the only warning.
- Salt-belt owners (upper Midwest, Northeast) are at highest corrosion risk.
- Repair is fast but parts will be scarce — book early.
- Second-hand shoppers: these model years in VW's bread-and-butter SUVs are all in scope; run the VIN before buying.

## Story beats for the draft
- Lede: a single screw. Not a battery, not software. A screw. And it's why 4.64 million cars are going back to the dealer.
- The 1% number: roughly 46,400 cars actually at risk; the other 4.6M get fixed as precaution. That's either admirable caution or a very expensive screw.
- The two-year gap: first case Oct 2024 → safety classification Sep 2026.
- The rattle: the only warning sign, per the filing itself.
- Action block: check VIN, book before the November letters, listen for the rattle, salt-belt priority.
