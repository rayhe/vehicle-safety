# Research: Ford F-Series Recall 26V-653 — The Speedometer That Lies

**Slug:** 1333-ford-f-series-lying-speedometer-26v653
**Journalist:** Vin Wreckage (Existential Dread Columnist; paradoxes and counterintuitive findings beat)
**Kicker:** Investigation
**Proposed headline:** Ford Recalled 102,159 F-Series Trucks Because the Speedometer Might Be Making Up Numbers

## Kill test
PASS. Fresh federal filing (NHTSA report filed October 6, 2026; interim owner letters October 26-30), 102,159 vehicles, and a genuinely perverse failure mode: an analog speedometer needle that sticks in place or swings erratically, so the truck's own dashboard invents a speed. The strongest angle is the regulatory paradox: Ford told NHTSA these trucks *meet* FMVSS 101 while simultaneously admitting the needles can display a number unrelated to actual speed. The standard governs where the speedometer sits, how it is labeled, and whether it lights up; it sets no rule for whether the needle points at the right number. A truck can comply with the safety standard and still tell its driver 55 at 70.

A second layer: this is Ford's *other* dashboard problem. In August 2025 Ford recalled 355,656 trucks because the *digital* instrument panel could go blank at start-up (a software defect fixed with a software update, possibly over the air). This time the *analog* cluster lies, and no software can fix it: the remedy is a physical cluster swap, with parts not expected until December. Same company, two dashboard recalls a year apart, opposite failure modes, opposite fixes.

Distinct from prior site coverage: queue grep finds no 26V-653, no instrument-cluster, no speedometer piece. #1325 covers a Ford Explorer eleven-recall story; #1326 covers the Ford Godzilla idle patch; neither touches the F-Series analog cluster. The Ford digital-blank-screen recall of August 2025 is context only.

## Primary sources (3+)

1. **The Auto Wire, October 26, 2026 (1 primary).**
   https://theautowire.com/2026/10/26/ford-speedometer-recall-102159-trucks-fmvss-101-visteon/
   - NHTSA campaign 26V-653; Ford recall number 26C46. Filed with NHTSA October 6, 2026.
   - 102,159 trucks: 91,588 Super Duty (2025-2026) built Dec. 14, 2024 through Apr. 17, 2026, split as 43,300 F-250s, 26,354 F-350s, 6,847 F-450s, 12,260 F-550s, 2,827 F-600s; plus 10,570 2026 F-150s built Aug. 22, 2025 through May 12, 2026.
   - Defect: needles on analog speedometers and tachometers can stick or swing erratically, showing a speed or rpm with no relation to the truck's actual speed. Drivers whose cluster also offers a digital speed readout may see two different speeds on the same dashboard.
   - Supplier: Visteon Corp., Chihuahua, Mexico plant. Ford blamed "supplier process deficiencies"; tool maintenance in January 2026 addressed the root cause going forward, but Ford says it does not fully understand the exact nature of the defect in units already built.
   - Reports: 18 warranty claims and 93 field reports covering 79 distinct trucks, received Jan. 29, 2025 through Aug. 11, 2026. Ford's Critical Concern Review Group flagged the problem May 28, 2026.
   - Ford estimates about 1% of covered trucks (roughly 1,000 vehicles) actually carry the defect.
   - FMVSS 101 paradox: Ford's filing says the trucks meet FMVSS 101's speedometer requirements, because the standard governs placement, labeling, and lighting, not needle accuracy.
   - The Auto Wire's inference (attributed as inference, not Ford's statement): trucks built as late as April/May 2026 carry the defect even though Visteon fixed the tooling in January 2026, suggesting pre-fix clusters kept going into new trucks for months.
   - Remedy timeline: dealer notification October 9; interim owner letters October 26-30 (federal 60-day notice rule 49 CFR 577.7); remedy parts expected December, second letter round December 7-11. NHTSA record carries no do-not-drive or park-outside warning.
   - Ford customer service: 1-866-436-7332.

## Verified facts used in the article
- Population: 102,159. Campaign: NHTSA 26V-653 (26V653000); Ford 26C46. Filed October 6, 2026.
- Models/years: 2025-2026 Super Duty (F-250, F-350, F-450, F-550, F-600), 2026 F-150, one 2026 F-750.
- Component: analog instrument cluster module (L1 module per HotCars), supplied by Visteon, Chihuahua plant.
- Failure: speedometer/tachometer needles stick or move erratically; may disagree with digital speed readout on the same truck.
- Reports: 18 warranty + 93 field = 111 reports over 79 trucks, Jan 2025 to Aug 2026. Flagged internally May 28, 2026.
- Ford estimate: ~1% of recalled trucks defective (~1,000).
- Remedy: dealer replaces the cluster module, free. Interim letters Oct 26-30, 2026; parts expected December; remedy letters Dec 7-11.
- FMVSS 101 (Controls and Displays): governs speedometer placement/labeling/illumination, not accuracy. Ford asserts compliance.
- No crashes or injuries reported so far; no do-not-drive or park-outside advisory on the NHTSA record.
- Prior context: August 2025 Ford recall of 355,656 trucks for blank digital instrument panels; software remedy.
