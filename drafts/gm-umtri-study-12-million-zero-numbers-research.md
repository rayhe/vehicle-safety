# Research: GM-UMTRI Study — 12 Million Vehicles, Zero Numbers

**Slug:** gm-umtri-study-12-million-zero-numbers
**Journalist:** Rex Driverton
**Kicker:** Investigation
**Sources:** 3+ primary

## Angle
GM and the University of Michigan (UMTRI) published their eighth joint study in May 2026, examining ~12 million MY2020-2024 GM vehicles matched against 700,000+ police-reported crashes across 18 states. GM's press release calls the results "striking" and declares "statistically significant" crash reductions from AEB, pedestrian braking, lane keep assist, forward collision alert, and IntelliBeam.

The press release never reveals the actual crash reduction percentages. Not once. Every quote is qualitative: "striking," "meaningful benefits," "high degree of confidence." The infographic was published as an image, not as data. GM funded the study. GM controls the release. And nobody in automotive press challenged the missing numbers.

## Kill Test — Is This Genuinely Newsworthy?
YES. A Fortune 500 company claims its vehicles are measurably safer, cites a university study as proof, and won't publish the numbers. This is a data-hiding story wrapped in a safety story.

## FARS Cross-Reference

### GM vehicles — MY deaths pre-2020 vs 2020+
(FARS 2014-2023 window; note: newer MY = fewer years of exposure)
- All GM brands pre-2020: 45,853 deaths / 872 model-year entries (avg 52.6 per entry)
- All GM brands 2020+: 1,055 deaths / 56 entries (avg 18.8 per entry)
- Apparent reduction: ~64%

### Control brands (same FARS window)
- Toyota pre-2020: 20,848 / 386 entries (avg 54.0) → 2020+: 919 / 35 (avg 26.3) = ~51% reduction
- Honda pre-2020: 17,221 / 174 (avg 99.0) → 2020+: 475 / 21 (avg 22.6) = ~77% reduction

### The problem with these numbers
Newer model years have 1-3 years of road exposure during the FARS 2014-2023 window. Pre-2020 model years have 4-9+ years. The reductions are overwhelmingly driven by EXPOSURE TIME, not safety technology. Every brand shows massive drops. GM's reduction (64%) is not an outlier — it falls between Toyota (51%) and Honda (77%).
