# Critic Panel — #1008 "Pontiac Died 16 Years Ago. The Grand Prix Still Kills 97 People a Year."
Journalist: Rex Driverton | Round 0 | 2026-09-28

## 1. FARS Accuracy — 8.8/10
Verified every number against fars_output.js (2014-2023). Deaths: Grand Prix 970 (97.0/yr), Impala 3,774, Century 849, Regal 153, LaCrosse 291, Monte Carlo 178. Rates: 5.00 / 2.41 / 2.14 / 1.01 / 0.72 / 0.71. Impairment: 21.8 / 21.5 / 21.4 / 21.3 / 20.1 / 19.3. All match. 5.00/0.71 = 7.04x ("sevenfold" OK). "Five other nameplates" is correct (six total incl. Grand Prix). GM Pontiac end date (Apr 27, 2009) and late-2009 final production verified via two sources. Minor: "flat" impairment slightly overstates a 2.5-point spread; "near-identical" would be safer. Passed threshold.

## 2. Voice Coach (Rex Driverton) — 9.0/10

## 3. Ethics Reviewer — 9.0/10
No moralizing about impaired drivers; the impairment data is used analytically, not as a cudgel. Dark humor targets institutions (GM, the platform), never victims. Consumer guidance is genuinely useful. No self-congratulation. Passed.

## 4. Social/Shareability — 8.7/10

## 5. Legal Accuracy — 8.6/10

References section complete with working primary links for FARS, IIHS, and the Pontiac discontinuation. Passed.

## 6. Research Rigor — 8.8/10
Novel contribution: platform-family cross-tab of death rates vs impairment rates, genuinely unrun. Methodology transparent (FARS window, estimated VMT, nameplate-generation blending). Limitations stated in-article and in disclaimer. Counterargument (fleet VMT undercount, Impala generation mix) argued at full strength with a quantitative rebuttal (halved rate still leaves 3.5x). The fleet-composition mechanism is honestly labeled as inference, not proven. Passed.

## 7. Reader Advocate — 8.9/10
Concrete actions: buy the coupe over the sedan, verify optional side airbags on Grand Prix trims, check VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. The used-car angle is the right audience for a 16-years-dead brand. Passed.
