## Key facts (verifiable)
- IIHS tested 9 large SUVs for 2026 (up from 3 in 2024). Only 5 earned awards; the large-SUV segment remains
  the sparsest award category.
- The two TSP+ winners are both fully electric (R1S, EX90). R1S: third TSP+ (2023, 2025, 2026) under
  progressively tougher criteria.
- Tahoe: Acceptable small overlap, POOR moderate overlap. Expedition: Marginal small overlap (driver+passenger),
  Marginal moderate overlap. Sequoia: Marginal moderate overlap, Marginal vehicle-to-vehicle crash prevention.
- R1S is "the only vehicle in the large SUV category built by an American automotive company to receive the 2026
  TOP SAFETY PICK+ designation (as of March 2026)" (Rivian press release via USA Today).
- 2026 criteria require Good moderate/small overlap + side, acceptable+ headlights, Good pedestrian crash
  prevention, and acceptable+ in updated vehicle-to-vehicle 2.0 test for the "+".

## Original contribution (novel calculations)
1. Lab-vs-graveyard cross-tab: Tahoe (no award, 2.49), Expedition (no award, 2.31), Yukon (not tested, 2.55)
   vs Sequoia (no award, 0.83). The "no award" label hides a 3.0x real-world spread (2.49/0.83).
2. Armada paradox resolved: TSP + lowest real-world rate (0.55) — the quiet winner; the lab and the graveyard
   agree on the Armada and disagree on the Sequoia.
3. The two TSP+ winners have ZERO FARS track record — the safest lab rating belongs to vehicles the real world
   hasn't measured. IIHS's top honor is a prediction, not a measurement.
4. Yukon: highest real-world rate of the group (2.55) and wasn't among the 9 tested at all.

## Strongest counterargument
FARS 2014-2023 measures older generations; the IIHS ratings are for 2026 models, many redesigned. Real-world
data lags by design — of course new EVs have no FARS record. Sequoia (136 deaths) and Armada (182) have small
samples, so their rates carry wide uncertainty. Driver demographics differ across nameplates (fleet/commercial
use, geography), which FARS rates don't adjust for.

## Limitations
- Model-year mismatch: FARS 2014-2023 vs 2026 IIHS ratings; generational changes confound the comparison.
- No FARS data for R1S/EX90 (too new); their "safest" status is lab-only.
- Small death counts for Sequoia/Armada (136/182) → ±large uncertainty vs Tahoe (2,592).
- IIHS class summary shows several "not tested" cells (headlights, crash prevention) for Tahoe/Expedition —
  their no-award status partly reflects incomplete testing, not just failed tests.
- FARS rates are not adjusted for driver age, geography, or commercial/fleet use.

## Actionable takeaways
- Shopping a big SUV: the Armada pairs a TSP with the lowest real-world rate (0.55) — the data-backed pick.
- Tahoe/Expedition: no IIHS award AND the highest real-world death rates (2.31-2.49). Know that before you buy.
- Sequoia: no award but a low 0.83 real-world rate — the lab's miss, or a small sample; either way, cheaper
  insurance logic than the Tahoe.
- The EV winners (R1S, EX90): best lab scores, zero real-world history. Great engineering bet; unproven record.
