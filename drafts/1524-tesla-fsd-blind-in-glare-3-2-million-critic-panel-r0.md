## 6. Research Rigor — 9.0/10
Original contribution is real: the 3-of-9 cross-tab (Tesla's own fix covers a third of the crashes it was built for) combined with NHTSA's admission that it does not know which cars carry the fix — fleet exposure is unquantifiable even by the regulator. Limitations are explicit in the disclaimer (open investigation, 9 is an investigation set not a census, Tesla figures self-reported). Counterargument full-strength. Six sources, anchored by the primary NHTSA ODI letter (EA26002-01, July 2, 2026). The "essentially every FSD-equipped Tesla" claim is qualified in-article and the overlap caveat is in the research notes.

One nit: the article never states the model-year range of EA26002's population (2016–2026 S/X, 2017–2026 3, 2020–2026 Y/Cybertruck); the fleet-size number alone carries the point, so this is a footnote, not a gap.
