## 6. Research Rigor — 8.0/10
Genuine original contribution: the FMVSS 226 paradox (rule at 100% compliance since 2017, 4,456 still ejected and killed), the survivor-vs-dead comparison inside the same fatal crashes (48% vs 13%), the scale comparison against distraction deaths (4,456 vs 3,208, +39%), and the pickup rollover skew (39%). Cross-checks done: 4,993 minus 537 = 4,456 reconciles exactly; 9,758 + 10,402 + 2,553 = 22,713 reconciles exactly; 4,456/22,713 = 19.6% matches NHTSA's "20 percent". Limitations stated (ARF subject to revision, no 2024 ejection-by-restraint cross-tab, 71% figure correctly framed as a scale comparison). **But three superlative claims fail verification and must be fixed:**
- "the sharpest percentage improvement among the headline categories NHTSA tracks" is FALSE: the same Overview reports pedalcyclist hit-and-run deaths down 16% and school-transportation deaths down 14%, both larger than ejection's 11%. Ejection's 11% is third-largest among the listed categories, not first.
- "the best year on record for this particular way to die" is UNVERIFIED: the cited sources give only 2023 vs 2024, no multi-year ejection time series.
- "the largest single-year improvement in the recent record" (counterargument paragraph) is likewise UNVERIFIED for the same reason.
All three are fixable by softening to what the sources support ("one of the largest percentage drops in the report", "an 11 percent improvement"). The ESC sentence ("stops rollovers before they start") should also carry NHTSA's actual estimate (roughly half) with its citation.

## 7. Data Presentation — 8.9/10
Pull stat carries the thesis; every percentage is paired with its count (4,456 of 22,713; 537 fewer; 9,758 unrestrained); per-day conversions make abstract counts concrete (12.2/day); the 48/13 comparison is presented as the single variable separating dead from survivors in the same crashes, which is exactly how the Overview frames it. The 71% scale comparison is correctly kept to the disclaimer with its subset caveat, while the body uses the raw counts. One nit: "nearly four times" for 48/13 (3.7x) is fine but the exact ratio would be stronger inline.
