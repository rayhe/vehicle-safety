# Research: Jeep Cherokee PTU — Four Recalls, Same Failure

## News Hook (July 15, 2026)
Stellantis expanded the Jeep Cherokee PTU (power transfer unit) recall for the FOURTH time:
- Recall 1: June 2020 (original batch of older Cherokees)
- Recall 2: April 2023 (expanded to newer model years)  
- Recall 3: April 2025 (further expansion)
- Recall 4: July 15, 2026 — 61,711 2019-2023 Cherokee SUVs

**Defect:** The PTU's internal parts disintegrate, stopping power delivery to the wheels AND making Park gear inoperable. The vehicle can lose all motive power on the highway OR roll away when parked.

**Field reports:** Between January 2020 and May 2026: 9 customer assistance records, 387 warranty claims, 5 field reports, 16 other service records. At least 1 accident, 1 injury.

**Source:** NHTSA recall, motorsafety.org July 15 2026 report

## FARS Data — Jeep Cherokee
- Total deaths: 2,276
- Death rate: 1.73 per 100M VMT
- Fleet: 1,050,000
- Crashes: 3,417
- Impairment: 18.1% (below sedan avg 20.4%, below SUV avg 19.5%)
- Alcohol: 13.7%
- Drugs: 8.1%

## Key Analysis
1. **Impairment is BELOW average** — Cherokee drivers are LESS impaired than the typical SUV driver. This means the Cherokee's 1.73 death rate is driven by vehicle factors (engineering, age, design), not driver behavior.

2. **Death rate is 2.74x the SUV class average** (1.73 vs 0.63). Only the Land Cruiser (6.27), Tracker (7.83), and a few others are worse among SUVs.

3. **Cherokee was discontinued in January 2024** — but ~1M are still on the road. No new safety updates coming. Ever.

4. **The PTU recall keeps expanding** because each round discovers the fix or the scope wasn't sufficient. After 6 years, Stellantis STILL hasn't fully solved the problem.

5. **Novel calculation:** At 1.73 deaths per 100M VMT, and assuming the remaining 1M fleet drives ~12,000 miles/year average, the Cherokee fleet produces roughly 208 deaths/year. Each year the recall drags on with unrepaired vehicles adds to that number.

## Self-Critique
- Is this genuinely surprising after 700+ articles? YES — the serial recall pattern (4 rounds for the same part) combined with the Cherokee's discontinuation and above-average death rate creates a specific narrative: a dead car that keeps killing, with a defect that keeps coming back.
- Is this just another recall article? NO — this is about the failure MODE of the recall system itself. Each expansion means the previous scope was wrong.
- Novel contribution: Cross-referencing the Cherokee's below-average impairment with above-average death rate to show the vehicle itself (not its drivers) is the problem.
