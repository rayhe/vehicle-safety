## 1. General Editor — 8.9
The anniversary hook earns its keep: November 28, 1995 to November 28, 2026 is a real peg, not a manufactured one, and the "fuel law that accidentally became the best safety law" paradox is exactly Rex's beat. Structure holds: lede with the 16.4% drop, the 1974 mechanism, the repeal reversal, the long-term study, the physics, the counterargument, the action close. Two original findings (the v² energy calculations; the 8,856-deaths-vs-modern-speeding-year comparison is implied but could be more explicit). Deductions: the 1974 paragraph repeats the "side effect" framing of the lede; the closing paragraph's "one crash at a time" is a touch sentimental for Rex.

## 3. Ethics Reviewer — 9.0
No victim-blaming; the dead are counted, not judged. The counterargument gets a full paragraph at full strength, including the wide confidence interval and the legitimate travel-time benefit, and is not dismissed afterward. The headline's 12,545 is explicitly fenced as a modeled estimate in the body, the references, and the disclaimer. "More than twice the death toll of September 11" is a legitimate scale comparison, not an exploitation: it is the study's own framing territory and it is used once. No self-congratulation, no position beyond what the data supports.

## 4. Social/Shareability — 8.8
Headline is a built-in share card: a date, a vote, and a five-figure death toll. Pull stat 12,545 is clean and quotable. "A number on a sign did it, at a cost of zero dollars per car" is the piece's best one-liner and survives a skim. Deductions: the headline is long for social and the 12,545 needs its modeled-estimate caveat, which does not fit in a post; the physics paragraph's formula, while correct, will lose casual readers before the payoff line.

## 6. Research Rigor — 8.9
Original contribution: the three kinetic-energy calculations (86%, 139%, 33%) are new arithmetic applied to the policy question, not a restatement of any cited source; the anniversary framing plus the 1974-vs-repeal paired-experiment structure is a novel synthesis. Limitations stated in-body and in the disclaimer: the 12,545 figure is modeled with a stated CI and a 1995–2005 window, no peer-reviewed update to 2026 exists, the 1974 drop is confounded by reduced VMT, and the 3,000–5,000 figure is TRB's modeled attribution. Strongest counterargument gets a full paragraph. Methodology transparent: the v² math is shown with inputs. Deductions: the 33,000 Farmer-2017 figure is cited but its method (association, not causal modeling) is not distinguished from Friedman's causal model; the "12 minutes" travel-time figure is computed but not shown.

## 7. Data Presentation — 8.8

Pull stat matches the thesis. Deductions: the 36,583 injuries figure appears once and is never used again; the 8,856 one-year drop is computed in the lede but the comparison to a modern year of speeding deaths (~11,288) is left implicit rather than stated, which would have strengthened the scale argument.
