# Research: The Car Seat Design Failure

## Angle
Cars.com's 2026 report (released July 30, 2026) found only 2 of 56 vehicles tested earned straight A's for car seat fit and installation. That's a 96% failure rate. Meanwhile, NHTSA has long cited 59% car seat misuse rates, and AAA's 2024 analysis found 74% of inspected car seats were improperly installed. The auto industry blames parents — but IIHS has explicitly stated that "incompatibility between child restraints and vehicle seat designs" is a primary driver of installation failure.

## Novel Contribution
Cross-referencing Cars.com's 2026 car-seat-friendliness rankings against FARS fatality data reveals a pattern: the vehicles parents actually buy as "family vehicles" (Ford Explorer: 3,797 deaths, Chevy Tahoe: 2,592 deaths) are NOT the ones that ace car seat tests. Only 2 vehicles in the entire test — a $96K Mercedes and a VW Tiguan — got it right.

## Key Data Points

### Cars.com 2026 Report (July 30, 2026)
- 56 vehicles tested by certified child passenger safety technicians
- Only 2 earned straight A's: 2025 Mercedes-Benz GLS580, 2026 VW Tiguan
- 10 "Top Finishers" earned A's in all but one category
- Testing: infant seat, convertible seat, high-back booster + LATCH system usability
- Quote: "A vehicle that makes car seat installation difficult creates more than daily frustration: it can affect how securely a child is restrained." — Jennifer Geiger, Cars.com

### NHTSA Misuse Statistics
- 59% of car seats are being misused (NHTSA, cited by multiple agencies)
- 45% of parents who said they were "confident" or "very confident" in their installation still had at least one misuse (NHTSA Research Note DOT HS 812 157)
- 15% of drivers never read any instructions on how to install their child restraints
- Most common errors: loose installation (17%), not using tether with forward-facing seat, loose harness

### AAA 2024 Analysis
- 74% of car seats inspected in 2023 were improperly installed or used (NDCF database)
- 2018-2022: over 4 million children age 11 and under involved in car crashes
- 547,000 injuries, nearly 3,000 fatalities
- 100,000+ children injured in car crashes in 2022 alone
- Child restraints reduce fatalities by 71% for infants <1 year, 54% for children 1-4

### IIHS Position
- "incompatibility between child restraints and vehicle seat designs" is a key factor in misuse
- "85-90 percent of child restraints are installed improperly or used incorrectly" at inspection stations
- "the structural integrity of the vehicle, not the child restraint, is the principal limiting factor in restraint effectiveness"
- LATCH system (required since Sept 1, 2002) was supposed to fix this — it hasn't

### Safe Kids Worldwide
- 64% of car seats are not installed using the top tether
- 2,600+ children under 13 involved in a car crash daily (one every 33 seconds)

### FARS Connection
- Popular "family" vehicles NOT in Cars.com top finishers:
  - Ford Explorer: 3,797 deaths (FARS 2014-2023)
  - Chevy Tahoe: 2,592 deaths
  - Toyota Highlander: 1,106 deaths
  - Chevy Equinox: 1,056 deaths
  - Nissan Rogue: 968 deaths
  - Toyota RAV4: 914 deaths
- Vehicles that DID earn top marks:
  - VW Tiguan: 126 deaths (much lower fleet, but still)
  - Subaru Outback: 707 deaths
  - Honda CR-V: 2,072 deaths
