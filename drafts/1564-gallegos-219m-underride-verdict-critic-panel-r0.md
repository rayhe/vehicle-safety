## 3. Ethics Reviewer — 9.2/10

## 4. Social / Shareability — 8.8/10
Headline is the share: "$21.9 Million" + "garbage truck" + "underride guard" is an unlikely trio that stops a scroll. The defense quote-paraphrase ("too old to follow") is the line people will repeat. Pull stat is clean and screenshot-able. ## 5. Legal Accuracy — 8.9/10
Verdict, fault split (65/20/15), award breakdown ($15M + $6.9M), case number (21STCV42588), court (LA County Superior, Chatsworth trial), and FMVSS 223/224 + 2022 strengthening all check against the cited sources. The apportionment math is shown, not asserted: 35% of $21.9M ≈ $7.7M, $5.25M and $2.4M components. The article correctly distinguishes the jury award from a payment and notes appeals can move it. Quibble: the lede's "the price was $21.9 million" could be read as the payable sum before P3 clarifies; the clarification arrives two paragraphs later, which is fast enough.

## 6. Research Rigor — 8.8/10
Original contribution is real: the apportionment calculation (headline vs. payable), the 8-day pairing of the verdict announcement with the MAC 26V723 recall, and the framing of the defense's argument as a concession (the guard would not protect a modern sedan). Limitations are stated in the disclaimer, including that the guard's specific defect mode is not in the sources and is not invented. Strongest counterargument is stated at full strength (65% driver fault; guard is a second-chance device; 1953-era standard). Every factual claim traces to a cited source. The FARS undercount material is kept in the background where it belongs and not imported as rear-impact counts.

## 7. Data Presentation — 8.7/10
Numbers are the article's spine and they are all clean: $21.9M, 65/20/15, $15M/$6.9M, $7.7M apportioned, 8 days, 2022 rule, 4 years. The pull stat and pull label carry the two numbers that matter. No chart is needed for a verdict story; the fault-split could have been a tiny table, but the prose split is precise. The one soft spot: the pull label repeats "35% at fault" which the body also states, a minor duplication.
