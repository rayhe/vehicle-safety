Fresh federal filing (NHTSA report received Oct 6, 2026; trade coverage Oct 25-26, 2026), a do-not-use advisory (rare and severe), and a genuine pattern, not a one-off: this is the SECOND upper-coupler/kingpin failure on Great Dane's Champion dry van in under two years. The Dec 2024 recall was missing kingpin welds on the same component; the Oct 2026 recall is a wrong bottom plate on the same component. Two different manufacturing escapes, one connection point, one consequence: a 40,000-pound box that can unhitch itself. No prior Crash Report draft covers Great Dane, the Champion, or a trailer coupler/kingpin recall (queue grep for great-dane/kingpin/coupler: zero; the only kingpin/coupler mentions in drafts are unrelated RV and light-tower pieces). Distinct from #1251 (Range Rover rear camera drain holes) and #1245 (Porsche centerlock) — this is the commercial-trailer coupling, the single mechanical link between tractor and trailer.

## Verified facts (3+ primary/primary-adjacent sources)
1. **NHTSA campaign 26V656000, report received Oct 6, 2026** (via AutoMotor News, Oct 25, 2026, checked against NHTSA records; and Overdrive, Oct 26, 2026):
   - Certain **2027 Great Dane Champion dry van trailers**, **69 units** potentially affected.
   - The upper coupler may have an **incorrect bottom plate** installed. The **kingpin may protrude**, preventing the trailer from coupling properly.
   - "An improperly coupled trailer may detach, increasing the risk of a crash."
   - Component: trailer hitches. **Do-not-use advisory:** owners are advised **not to use their trailers until the repair is completed**. No park-outside advisory.
   - Remedy: Great Dane will **inspect and replace the upper coupler assembly as necessary, free of charge**.
   - Owner notification letters expected to be mailed **Dec 4, 2026**. Great Dane customer service **1-877-369-3493**.
   - https://automotor.news/news/great-dane-champion-dry-van-trailer-coupler-recall-26v656/
2. **Overdrive, Oct 26, 2026** (detail on the plate): affected trailers manufactured beginning **Sept 1, 2026**, equipped with **hot-dipped galvanized upper couplers** where the bottom plate **may have been 1/4 inch instead of 5/16 inch**.
   - https://www.overdriveonline.com/life/article/15975442/attorney-gets-prison-for-new-orleans-staged-crashes-great-dane-do-not-drive-recall

## Original calculations (verifiable, show work in article)
- **Plate math:** 1/4 inch = 0.250 in; 5/16 inch = 0.3125 in. The installed plate is 0.0625 in short — **20% thinner than spec** (0.250 / 0.3125 = 0.80). One sixteenth of an inch is the entire margin between "coupled" and "kingpin protrudes."
- **Repeat interval:** Dec 11, 2024 (24V922) → Oct 6, 2026 (26V656) = **~22 months** between Champion upper-coupler/kingpin recalls. Two escapes, same connection point, two different failure modes (missing welds, then wrong plate).
- **Week's commercial batch:** 69 + 310 + 5 = **384** trailers/trucks in the Oct 26 NHTSA commercial roundup; Great Dane is 18% of it (69/384 = 18.0%) but the only one with a do-not-use advisory.
- **Population contrast (stated carefully, no invented totals):** 69 trailers is a small recall — and that is the point. A do-not-use order on 69 units means the defect is not a statistical tail; every one of the 69 is suspect until inspected.

- The exact production window end date and the supplier of the galvanized couplers are not in the coverage found; the article will cite only the Sept 1, 2026 start and the 1/4-vs-5/16 plate detail.

## Strongest counterargument
Sixty-nine trailers is a rounding error in a national fleet of millions, and Great Dane caught this one in weeks (production began Sept 1, NHTSA report Oct 6) with a do-not-use order rather than a quiet letter campaign. That is the system working fast. The honest frame: the severity is not the count, it is the component — the coupler is the only thing between a loaded trailer and the traffic behind it, and this is the second time in 22 months that this exact connection point has failed quality control on this exact trailer.

## Actionable takeaways (required gate)
- If you operate a 2027 Great Dane Champion dry van with a hot-dipped galvanized upper coupler: **do not use the trailer until it is inspected** — that is Great Dane's own advisory, not a suggestion. Call Great Dane at 1-877-369-3493 or check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls (campaign 26V656).
- Fleet managers: pull the 2027 Champions built from Sept 1, 2026 first, and check the coupler plate thickness against the 5/16-inch spec before the Dec 4 letters arrive.
- The fix is free: inspection and upper-coupler replacement as necessary.
