# Research Notes: GM's Quiet Recall Chart, Higher Body Count

## Angle
Ford leads 2026 recalls by a massive margin (60 vs GM's 19 — 3.2x). Ford's VP of Quality (Josh Levine) publicly named the 2013-2020 design era as the source of their recall crisis. But when you run the same 2013-2020 FARS body count calculation for GM, their number is 25% HIGHER than Ford's: 8,206 vs 6,539 deaths.

## Key Data Points

### Recall Volume (2026 YTD, per USA Today / NHTSA)
- Ford: 60 recalls
- Chrysler/Stellantis: 24 recalls
- GM: 19 recalls

### FARS Deaths from 2013-2020 Model Year Vehicles
- Ford: 6,539 deaths (19.0% of all Ford FARS deaths)
- GM: 8,206 deaths (16.2% of all GM FARS deaths)
- Stellantis: 5,962 deaths (25.8% of all Stellantis FARS deaths)

### The Paradox
Recalls are a lagging indicator of DETECTION, not danger. The manufacturer that finds and reports more problems looks worse on the recall chart. The manufacturer that finds fewer problems looks fine on paper but may have more people dying.
