# Research: 1271 Prevost bus exit-label recall

## Topic
Prevost Car (US) Inc. recall of 7,509 buses: the entrance door is missing its
emergency-exit operating instruction label, failing FMVSS 217 (Bus Emergency
Exits and Window Retention and Release). Announced by NHTSA October 15, 2026.

## Kill test
PASS. Novel angle: the cheapest safety device on a 50-seat coach is a sticker,
and 7,509 coaches shipped without the one that matters most — the label that
tells panicked passengers the front door doubles as an emergency exit. Distinct
from prior queue entries: #1041 Volvo Bus 9700 (different maker, 16 model
years, designation-without-instructions) and 24V893 Volvo 9700 label detachment
(labels falling off vs. never installed/unclear). This is the entrance-door
label specifically, on Prevost H3/X3 coaches, with a Canada twin recall the same
day. Full-queue dup check: no "prevost" slug exists in the queue.

## Primary sources
1. NHTSA recall 26V675000 (via OEMDTC recalls list): Prevost Car (US) Inc.,
   2026-10-15, component EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS, manufacturer recall SR26-19,
   7,509 units potentially affected. No do-not-drive, no park-outside advisory.
2. Oct 24, 2026 recalls roundup (beehiiv): entrance door missing its
   emergency-exit operating instruction label; FMVSS 217 noncompliance;
   missing instructions could keep passengers from evacuating quickly;
   Prevost will replace the label free; owner letters December 7, 2026;
   Prevost customer service 1-866-870-2046; 102 associated models on NHTSA page.
3. Transport Canada recall 2026499 (2026-10-15, RA-82824): same defect — the
   instruction label for using the entrance door as an emergency exit may not
   be clear; unclear instructions could delay escape and increase injury risk;
   remedy is a new label plus install instructions, or installation at an
   authorized Prevost service center. Canadian models listed: H3-45, X3-45,
   H3-41 coaches, model years 2014-2023.
4. Context: FMVSS 217 requires emergency exits to be identified and their
   operating instructions marked; prior Volvo Group label recalls (Volvo Bus
   9700, 24V893000, Dec 2024, labels detaching; #1041 Volvo Bus 9700
   designation-without-instructions) show this failure mode recurs inside
   Volvo Group, which owns Prevost.

## Angle
The exit is right there. The instructions aren't. A coach can pass every
mechanical inspection and still fail the one test that matters in a fire:
telling 50 strangers, in smoke and panic, how to open the door in front of
them. The fix costs less than the envelope it arrives in. Actionable: coach
operators check the entrance-door label before the next run; passengers count
exits before departure, and treat the front door as an exit whether or not a
label says so.
