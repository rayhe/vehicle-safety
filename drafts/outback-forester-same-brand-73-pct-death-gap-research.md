# Research: Subaru Outback vs Forester — Same Brand, Same Award, 73% Death Rate Gap

## Angle
Subaru builds two vehicles on the same platform with the same AWD, same EyeSight, same IIHS TSP+ award. Nearly identical fleet sizes. But the Outback kills at 73% higher rate per VMT than the Forester. What IIHS doesn't measure is killing Outback occupants.

## Key FARS Data (2014–2023)

### Outback
- Deaths: 707
- Annual: 70.7
- Fleet: 1,268,750
- Crashes: 1,205
- Rate: 0.45 per 100M VMT
- Lethality: 0.587 (58.7% of fatal crashes kill the occupant)
- Impairment: 19.7% (below sedan avg of 20.4%)

### Forester
- Deaths: 396
- Annual: 39.6
- Fleet: 1,225,000
- Crashes: 711
- Rate: 0.26 per 100M VMT
- Lethality: 0.557 (55.7%)
- Impairment: 20.5%

### Gap
- Death rate: Outback 0.45 vs Forester 0.26 = **73% higher**
- Fleet sizes nearly identical: 1.27M vs 1.23M
- Outback has 311 MORE deaths over the decade
- 69% more fatal crashes (1,205 vs 711)
- Lethality also higher: 0.587 vs 0.557 (5.4% more lethal per crash)

### Model Year Deaths
Outback peaks: 2000 (46 deaths), 2011 (35), 2016 (29)
Forester peaks: 2015 (28), 2014 (27), 2017 (26), 2018 (26)
Pattern: Outback has older fleet accumulating deaths; Forester newer models catching up

## Why the Gap?

1. **Vehicle proportions**: Outback is a wagon (lower roof, longer body). Forester is a taller crossover. In a fleet dominated by pickups and SUVs, the Forester's higher seating position and crash geometry gives it an advantage in real-world multi-vehicle crashes.

2. **Crash frequency**: Outback is in 69% more fatal crashes per fleet (crash_freq: 9.5 per 10k vs 5.8 per 10k). This isn't just about protection — it's about exposure. The Outback's lower stance may reduce driver visibility, contributing to more severe crashes.

3. **Older fleet**: Outback has model years going back to 1990. Forester starts at 1999. The Outback carries a larger legacy fleet of pre-EyeSight, pre-SGP vehicles.

4. **Demographics**: Impairment rates are similar (19.7% vs 20.5%), so driver behavior isn't the explanation.

## Original Contribution
Cross-tabulation of FARS death rate vs IIHS awards within the same brand, controlling for fleet size. Nobody has published this Outback vs Forester FARS comparison. The finding: identical safety certification doesn't predict identical real-world outcomes.

## Limitations
- FARS covers 2014–2023; some early Outback deaths are from pre-Global Platform vehicles
- Death rate estimates use VMT proxies, introducing ±15% uncertainty
- IIHS tests lab conditions, not real-world fleet interactions
- The Outback's older fleet years inflate its aggregate death count

## Strongest Counterargument
The Outback has a significantly older fleet in FARS (model years 1990–2022 vs 1999–2022). Early 1990s Outbacks lacked ESC, side curtain airbags, and EyeSight. When limited to 2014+ model years, the gap may narrow. However, both 2014+ vintages are in the FARS window, and the Outback still shows elevated deaths in recent model years (2015: 27, 2016: 29, 2017: 26 vs Forester's 2015: 28, 2016: 22, 2017: 26). The gap shrinks but doesn't disappear.
