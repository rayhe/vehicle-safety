## 3. Ethics Reviewer — 9.0/10
No moral issues. The article does not blame victims; the pedestrian advice is practical self-protection grounded in the 2%-of-crashes/26%-of-deaths asymmetry, and the risk is framed as concentrated on people on foot, not caused by them. No self-congratulation, no positions taken beyond what the data supports.
