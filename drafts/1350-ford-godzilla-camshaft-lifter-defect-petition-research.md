# Research: Ford 7.3L "Godzilla" V8 defect petition (DP26011) — Article #1350

## The story (Oct 23, 2026)
NHTSA's Office of Defects Investigation opened defect petition DP26011 on October 23, 2026, into camshaft and lifter failures in Ford's 7.3-liter gasoline V8 ("Godzilla"). Estimated population: 54,787 vehicles — 2021-2022 Ford E-350, 2021 F59, 2021 F-750.

## Key facts
- Petition received June 13, 2026; filed as ODI 11764421. Investigator: Jason Williams; reviewer: Joshua Neff; approver: Tanya Topka.
- Alleged defect: insufficient oil lubrication at low idle (~6 psi oil pressure) causes severe wear on lifter rollers and camshaft lobes → catastrophic engine failure with sudden loss of motive power at highway speed.
- Petition alleges Ford knew the root cause and released a software calibration patch raising idle speed to ~1,000 rpm — without notifying owners or issuing a recall.
- ODI tally: ONE incident on file, zero crashes/fires/injuries/deaths. Fleet evidence cited in press: a North Carolina commercial fleet operator (Feb 2024 complaint) — 11 trucks with the 7.3L V8, FIVE needed new engines, failures at 45,745–103,177 miles.
- Status: petition opened "to evaluate the issue and determine whether to grant or deny." Under 49 CFR Part 552, ~120 days from June 13 → decision ~Oct 11, 2026... opened Oct 23; agency has roughly until mid-October to decide grant/deny (note: timelines may vary; check ODI resume).
- No recall. No formal investigation yet. Defect petition is the first rung on NHTSA's ladder.

## Original data angle (The Crash Report's FARS 2014–2023 set)
- Ford E-350: 776 deaths, rate 2.51 — the HIGHEST fatality rate of any van in the dataset (Chevy Express 0.92, Ford Transit 0.14, Sienna 0.49).
- The E-350's rate is ~18x the Transit's. The same vehicle family now under a federal probe for an engine defect that kills motive power at highway speed.
- Caveat: FARS rate data covers 2014–2023 model years broadly; the defect petition covers 2021–2022 with the 7.3L gas V8 specifically. Don't claim the 2.51 rate is caused by the valvetrain defect.

## Actionable takeaways
- Owners of 2021-2022 E-350, 2021 F59, 2021 F-750 with the 7.3L gas V8: check VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls (DP26011 is not a recall — nothing will show until a recall exists); file a complaint with ODI after any engine failure; keep every service record; watch oil pressure at idle.
- Fleet owners: ask the dealer whether the PCM idle-speed software calibration has been applied, and demand documentation of what it was for.

## Limitations / counterarguments
- One incident in ODI's tally vs. 54,787 vehicles. Petition-stage data is allegation, not finding.
- The 5-of-11 fleet record is a single operator's experience, not a failure rate.
- E-350 FARS rate (2.51) reflects all model years 2014–2023, not the 7.3L subset; confounders: commercial use, high mileage, driver demographics, vehicle age.
- The silent-patch allegation is the petitioner's claim; Ford has not confirmed motive or timeline.
