# Research: America Broke Below 1.0 Deaths Per 100M Miles. Norway Is at 0.2.

## Article #753 | Journalist: Axle McScatter | Kicker: The Gap

## Angle
Q1 2026 fatality rate dropped to 0.99/100M VMT — the first time below 1.0 since 2014. Media covered it as a triumph. But international data exposes the celebration as premature: Norway is at 0.2/100M VKT, Sweden 0.3, Iceland 0.2, Australia 0.5. Even after converting units (1 mile = 1.609 km → US 0.62/100M VKT), America's "record" is 3× Norway's rate.

The per-capita gap is even wider: US at ~10.8/100K population vs Norway 1.73, UK 2.26, Sweden 1.98, Japan 2.72. That's 4-6× higher.

**Novel contribution:** Computing "excess deaths" — how many of the 7,770 Q1 deaths would not have occurred at international rates.

## Excess Death Calculation

VMT in Q1 2026 = 7,770 deaths / (0.99/100M) = 784.8 billion miles = 1.263 trillion km

At Norway's rate (0.2/100M VKT):
- Expected deaths = 1,263B × 0.2/100M = 2,526
- Excess: 7,770 - 2,526 = 5,244

At Sweden's rate (0.3/100M VKT):
- Expected: 1,263B × 0.3/100M = 3,789
- Excess: 7,770 - 3,789 = 3,981

At OECD median (~0.4/100M VKT estimated):
- Expected: 1,263B × 0.4/100M = 5,052
- Excess: 7,770 - 5,052 = 2,718

Annualized excess at Norway's rate: ~21,000 deaths/year
