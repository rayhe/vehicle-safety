## Facts (with sources)

### The current recall — NHTSA 26V362
- 17,277 MY 2020-2022 Chrysler Pacifica Plug-In Hybrid EVs, built Aug 5 2020 - May 2 2022.
- Defect: high-voltage battery cells produced on an ALTERNATIVE LG Energy Solution assembly line may fail internally, causing thermal runaway. Fire risk exists even when parked, ignition off.
- Chrysler estimates ~1% of affected vehicles may carry the defect (theevreport).
- 4 confirmed battery fires logged since Aug 2023; no reported injuries; as of May 15 2026: 4 confirmed field reports + 3 customer assistance records, 0 injuries, 0 crashes (calemonlaw citing Chrysler).
- Root cause STILL UNKNOWN: Chrysler and LGES continue investigating; only one recovered battery pack was available for analysis.
- Remedy: Battery Pack Control Module (BPCM) software update + battery pack inspection/replacement as needed, free. Battery warranty extended to UNLIMITED years and unlimited mileage.
- Owner guidance: park outside away from structures, STOP charging. VINs searchable on NHTSA from June 11, 2026; owner notification letters began ~June 23, 2026.
- Sources: theevreport (26V362 details), Consumer Reports ("Recalled Again for Fire Risk"), motoringchronicle (letters/timeline).

### Investigation timeline (calemonlaw citing NHTSA/Chrysler docs)
- Jan 17, 2024: NHTSA opened investigation into battery thermal events in Pacifica PHEVs.
- Mar 27, 2026: investigation expanded to cover 2017-2026 Pacifica PHEV.
- Apr 30, 2026: Chrysler launched internal investigation into 2020-2022 fires.
- May 28, 2026: Chrysler determined a safety defect exists; initiated recall.

### The fire-recall lineage (novel synthesis)
1. 2018: 10,000+ MY 2017-2018 Pacifica Hybrids recalled for fuel-system fire issue (Consumer Reports).
2. ~2022: ~19,516 US MY 2017-2021 Pacifica Hybrids recalled for HV battery fire risk; 7 fires found in warranty data, all with vehicle off, some while charging; park away from structures (insideEVs).
3. 12V connector corrosion: 27,634 Pacifica PHEVs recalled; park outdoors away from vehicles; keep 2nd-row floor dry (wet umbrellas/drinks); <10 fires, 1 minor injury (Consumer Reports).
4. 2026 (26V362): 17,277 MY 2020-2022, alternative LG line cells (this article).

### Stellantis PHEV fire pattern
- Stellantis makes the three best-selling PHEVs in the US: Jeep Wrangler 4xe, Jeep Grand Cherokee 4xe, Chrysler Pacifica Hybrid (insideEVs). Wrangler 4xe and Grand Cherokee 4xe also recalled for HV battery fire risk.
- Park-outside advisories have become a recurring PHEV-family pattern.

## Limitations (must state in article)
- ~1% defect estimate is Chrysler's; actual rate unknown since root cause undetermined.
- Only one recovered pack analyzed; the failure mechanism is still under investigation by Chrysler + LGES.
- Fire counts (4) come from Chrysler's own field reports; underreporting possible.
- FARS data not used here (this is a recall story); the safety-gap framing (#1052: 86% fatality-rate drop vs Grand Caravan) is crashworthiness, not fire risk — different axis, say so.

## Strongest counterargument (must state full-strength)
- 17,277 vehicles, ~1% with the defect, 4 fires in 3+ years, zero injuries: the absolute risk to any given owner is small. The park-outside/do-not-charge guidance is maximally cautious. Stellantis acted promptly once it determined a defect (May 28, 2026), offered an extraordinary unlimited warranty, and the software-detection remedy is proportionate. Fires in garages attached to homes are the scary scenario, but no house fire has been reported in this campaign.
