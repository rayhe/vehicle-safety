# Critic panel — #1012 Odyssey airbag petition denied (round 0)

## 1. Safety Engineer (technical accuracy)
The 150ms lede is correct (deployment decision window ~10-30ms sensing, full inflation ~30-50ms driver bag; "first 150 milliseconds" as the bag's whole career is defensible). DTC/SRS allegation characterized fairly as the petitioner's claim, not established fact. One nit: "a chemical inflator detonates" — inflators deflagrate (rapid burn), not detonate; "detonate" is the wrong energetic regime and an engineer reader will flag it. Fix: "fires" or "ignites."
Score: 8.5 (fix required: detonate → ignite)

## 2. Data Skeptic (FARS methodology)
864 deaths / 0.93 rate / 15.4% impairment all trace to fars_output.js. "Roughly 20 percent fleet-wide" for impairment — verify: the claim needs a fleet-wide tox number from the same dataset. Not verified in research notes. Either compute it or soften. Also the model-year file (165 deaths for 2011-2017) isn't used in the article — fine, no overclaim. "Its rate is nearly double the Sienna's" — 0.93/0.49 = 1.90x, correct; "almost five times the Pacifica's" — 0.93/0.19 = 4.89x, correct.
Score: 8.0 (fix required: verify or soften the ~20% fleet-wide impairment figure)

"the denial is triage, and triage is the system working" — fair, even generous to NHTSA. No implication that the denial clears the recalled pothole defect: the article explicitly separates generations. Good.

The article implies the agency looked only at complaints. Soften or add one clause: "NHTSA says it reviewed the complaints and other reports." It's already in ref [1] summary ("reviewed consumer complaints and other reports"). Add "and other reports" to the body.
Score: 8.0 (fix required: acknowledge "other reports" basis, one clause)
