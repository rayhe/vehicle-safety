# Research: Stellantis Recall-to-Sales Ratio 2026

## Thesis
Stellantis has recalled more than 3.27 million vehicles in the US in 2026 alone. It sold 1.26 million in all of 2025. That's a 2.6x ratio — for every vehicle Stellantis sold last year, it has recalled 2.6 this year. The defects aren't exotic engineering failures. They're bolts not tightened, wires not routed, parts not installed. This is an assembly-line quality crisis.

## Primary Sources

### Stellantis 2025 US Sales
- 1,260,344 vehicles sold in 2025 (Beacon Journal, Jan 5 2026)
- 3% decrease from 2024 (which was already down 15%)
- Jeep: ~593K, Ram: ~374K, Chrysler: ~126K, Dodge: ~100K

### 2026 Stellantis Recalls (US)
1. **Ram 1500 seat belt anchors** (Jul 31): 1,271,294 — second-row seat belt buckle anchors not properly attached to body structure. Assembly defect. 0.1% estimated defect rate.
2. **Jeep Wrangler/Gladiator fire** (Jun 9): 1,076,999 — power steering pump wiring may overheat, fire risk even when parked. 51 fires, 1 injury.
3. **Side airbag deployment** (May 2026): ~419,000 — improper deployment
4. **Ram/Jeep trailer lights/brakes** (Feb 2026): ~460,000
5. **Chrysler Pacifica Hybrid fire** (Jun 10): 17,277 — battery pack fire when parked
6. **Ram 1500 headlamp wiring** (Jul 11): 12,592
7. **Jeep Grand Wagoneer** (Jul 12): 11,980
8. **Ram pickups stability/ESC** (Apr 28): ~6,600
9. **Jeep Wrangler/Gladiator wheel** (Jul 25): 641
10. Various smaller: rearview cameras, shifter module software, etc.

**Total: ~3,276,000+ vehicles recalled in 2026**

### Nature of Defects (Novel Analysis)
The distinguishing feature of the 2026 Stellantis recall wave: these are not design defects. They're assembly defects.
- **Seat belt anchors not bolted to body** (Ram 1500) — assembly
- **Wiring not routed properly** (Wrangler/Gladiator fire) — assembly
- **Battery pack defect** (Pacifica Hybrid) — manufacturing
- **Side airbags not deploying correctly** — assembly/calibration
- **Trailer light connections** — assembly

Compare to typical recalls: software bugs, design flaws, supplier part failures. Stellantis's problems are people on the line not finishing the job.

### Counterargument
Stellantis estimates only 0.1% of the Ram 1500 recalled vehicles actually have the defect. Recalls are precautionary. The actual number of affected vehicles is much lower than 3.27 million. Additionally, recall-to-sales ratios above 1.0 are increasingly common across the industry (29.3M recalled vs 15.9M sold in 2025).

### Kill Test
Is this genuinely newsworthy? YES. While the industry-wide ratio exceeds 1.0, Stellantis's 2.6x ratio is specific, recent, and the defect pattern (assembly errors, not design) is a novel finding that connects to their financial distress and manufacturing quality crisis.

### Actionable
If you own a 2019-2026 Ram 1500, a 2021-2025 Wrangler or Gladiator, or a 2020-2022 Pacifica Hybrid, check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls immediately. The Wrangler/Gladiator recall carries a "park outside" warning due to fire risk.
