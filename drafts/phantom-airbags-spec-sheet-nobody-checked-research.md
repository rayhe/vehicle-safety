# Research Notes — #785: Phantom Airbags / Self-Certification Gap

## Core Story
KGM (formerly SsangYong) Musso EV sold in Australia from Nov 2025 with spec sheet claiming 8 airbags. ANCAP discovered during crash test that front-centre airbag and driver knee airbag were NEVER installed. Only 6 airbags present. ~306 vehicles sold before correction on Jul 27, 2026.

## Key Facts
- **Vehicle:** 2026 KGM Musso EV (battery-electric dual-cab ute)
- **Market:** Australia only
- **Sold:** ~306 units (Nov 2025 – Jul 2026, VFACTS data)
- **Missing airbags:** Front-centre airbag (deploys between front occupants in side impacts) + driver knee airbag (below steering column, controls driver movement in frontal)
- **Actual airbag count:** 6 (not 8 as advertised)
- **Discovery:** ANCAP independent crash test — centre airbag didn't deploy → post-crash inspection found it was never installed
- **Manufacturer response:** "Unintentional administrative error" — marketing material corrected Jul 31, 2026
- **Retrofit possible?** NO. KGM confirmed airbags CANNOT be retrofitted to existing MY26 vehicles.
- **Compensation:** Under review. "Dealers have been requested to refer customer requests for remedy or other assistance to KGM Australia for review."

## Broader Angle: Self-Certification
- In the US, manufacturers self-certify compliance with FMVSS standards
- NHTSA does NOT independently verify every vehicle's safety equipment
- NCAP crash-tests only a fraction of models
- ANCAP found this BY ACCIDENT during routine testing
- If ANCAP hadn't decided to test this specific vehicle, nobody would know
- No equivalent verification process exists in ANY country — Euro NCAP, ANCAP, IIHS, NHTSA NCAP all test subsets
