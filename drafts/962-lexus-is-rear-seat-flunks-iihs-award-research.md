## Angle
Lexus engineers revised the 2026 IS's driver seatbelt load limiter specifically to survive IIHS's updated moderate-overlap front test. It worked: the driver dummy scored Good in every injury measure. The car still earned a **Marginal overall** and missed both Top Safety Pick awards because the **rear-seat passenger's chest** rated Marginal (chest index 36, shoulder belt tension 6.5 kN) with Acceptable rear restraints/kinematics. The updated IIHS test, introduced to reflect real-world rear-seat injuries, is now the gate every car must pass — and it is disproportionately flunking older platforms (IS redesigned for 2014 MY, third facelift). Irony bonus: the paid Premium Triple-Beam LED headlight upgrade rates **Acceptable** while the standard LED projectors rate **Good** — paying more buys worse headlights.
