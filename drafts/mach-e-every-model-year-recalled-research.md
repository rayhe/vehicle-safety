# Research Notes: Mach-E Every Model Year Recalled

## Angle
Every model year of the Ford Mustang Mach-E (2021–2025) has been hit with at least three recall campaigns. Two dropped in July 2026 alone: a pinion shaft fracture (BorgWarner) and glass trim detachment (Saint-Gobain México). Both trace to supplier-side speed/cost decisions. Cross-referencing total U.S. sales (~215K) against recalled units shows roughly 40% of the entire fleet is under the glass recall alone.

## Original Finding
The 2023 Mustang Mach-E model year sits under four simultaneous recall campaigns: pinion shaft fracture, glass trim detachment, wiper motor failure, and electronic door latch malfunction. No other model year in the Mach-E's five-year production run has fewer than three active recalls. Ford's "clean-sheet EV" has generated more recall campaigns per model year than the ICE Mustang.

## Primary Sources

### NHTSA Recall 26V487 (Jul 28, 2026) — Glass Trim Detachment
- 86,543 units, 2023–2025 MY
- Built Feb 17, 2023 – Oct 8, 2024
- Supplier: Saint-Gobain México
- Root cause: New assembly jig shortened primer cure time
- 219 reports of loose/detached trim (as of July 2026)
- Fix introduced Sep 2024 (30-second timer on jig)
- 0 crashes, 0 injuries
- Owner notification: Sept 10, 2026
- Source: Ford Authority, glassBYTEs, Autoblog, Mustang Specs

### NHTSA Recall 26V417 (Jul 7, 2026) — Pinion Shaft Fracture
- 42,784 units, 2021–2023 MY (RWD drivetrain)
- Supplier: BorgWarner
- Root cause: Pinion shaft undersized for loads
- 62 warranty claims, 14 quality reports
- Remedy not available until December 2026
- 0 crashes, 0 injuries
- Source: NHTSA, electrek, USA Today

### Other Mach-E Recalls (partial list)

## Strongest Counterargument
Zero fatalities across all eight recall campaigns. The Mach-E earns strong IIHS safety ratings. Recall volume can indicate corporate transparency — Ford is finding and disclosing problems rather than burying them. The ICE Mustang's FARS fatality rate (~6.02/100M VMT) dwarfs anything the Mach-E has produced. Quality and safety are different axes.

## Limitations
- Not all recalls are equally severe. Glass trim detaching ≠ pinion shaft fracture.
- Multi-vehicle recalls (park module, wipers) include the Mach-E alongside other Ford platforms; the defect isn't Mach-E-specific.
- "Active" recall status can be misleading — some of these have available fixes that most owners may have completed.
- Sales figures vary by source (Ford Authority vs. carfigures vs. CEIC). Used most conservative estimates where possible.
