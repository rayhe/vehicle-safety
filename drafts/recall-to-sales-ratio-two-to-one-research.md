# Research: The 2:1 Ratio — America Recalls More Cars Than It Sells

## Angle
In 2024, the US recalled 29.3 million vehicles but only sold 15.9 million new ones. The recall-to-sales ratio: 1.84:1. For every new car that rolled off a dealer lot, nearly two were recalled. Q1 2026 accelerated further: 11.6 million recalled in a single quarter. This ratio is a metric nobody routinely publishes, and it reveals just how normalized massive recalls have become.

## Kill Test
- Is this genuinely surprising after 638 articles? YES — we've never cross-tabulated industry recall volume against sales volume to compute the ratio. We've covered Ford-specific recall paradoxes, software recall epidemics, and individual manufacturer records, but never the industry-wide "recalls exceed sales" metric.
- Novel original contribution: The recall-to-sales ratio as a named metric. Computing and presenting it as a time-trend.

## Computation
- 2024: 29.3M recalled / 15.9M sold = **1.84:1 ratio**
- Q1 2026: 11.6M recalled / ~4.0M sold (quarterly pace of ~16M annual) = **~2.9:1 ratio**
- For context: US registered vehicles ~290 million. 29.3M recalled = ~10% of entire fleet recalled in a single year.

## Counterarguments to Address
1. "Recalls don't mean the car is dangerous" — true, many are minor. But NHTSA data shows 94.6% of Q2 2025 recalls posed crash or injury risk.
2. "More recalls = more safety consciousness" — partially true. OTA makes it easier to issue recalls for software. But the volume increase far outpaces any definitional expansion.
3. "The same car can be recalled multiple times" — yes, the 29.3M figure counts recall-events, not unique vehicles. Some vehicles appear in multiple recalls. This actually makes the story worse: some cars are recalled 3-4 times before their first oil change.

## Limitations
- The 29.3M and 15.9M are not directly comparable populations: recalls affect vehicles from many model years (not just new), while sales count only new. This makes the ratio illustrative, not a direct 1:1 comparison. Must acknowledge this explicitly.
- Quarterly sales estimates for Q1 2026 are approximate.
- Recall counts include all vehicle types (heavy trucks, buses); sales figure is light vehicles. Minor distortion.
