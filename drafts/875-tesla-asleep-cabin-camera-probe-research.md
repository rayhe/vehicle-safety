## Angle
Tesla says the cabin camera monitors driver attentiveness while Full Self-Driving (Supervised) is engaged. Videos circulating this week show drivers flat-out asleep with FSD on. IIHS already proved the monitoring was the worst of 14 systems back in 2024 (they literally tested it with a cheesecloth over the driver's face). A congressman is now urging NHTSA to investigate the system itself. The babysitter camera is asleep on the job.

- **Newsworthy?** Yes. Congressional probe push + open NHTSA engineering analysis + viral videos. The monitoring layer is the load-bearing safety claim of every "Supervised" mile.
- **Data angle?** Yes. IIHS's 2024 safeguard ratings are hard test data: Tesla FSD Beta rated poor in 6 of 8 categories, worst overall; 11 of 14 systems poor.
