# Research Notes — 1124: Toyota Grand Highlander Grille Debris / Heat-Exchanger Defect Petition (DP26006)

**Journalist:** Mia Crumplezone (engineering/design beat — judgmental about bad vehicle design)
**News peg:** NHTSA opened Defect Petition DP26006 on Aug 20, 2026 (petition filed July 20, 2026) into 2024-2026 Toyota Grand Highlander.
**Angle:** Not a bad part. A design choice: big open grille, no screen or mesh, leaves heat exchangers in the line of fire. Ordinary highway gravel can punch through and kill propulsion.

## Facts (with sources)

1. **Petition filed July 20, 2026; ODI opened DP26006 on Aug 20, 2026.** Component: Engine and Engine Cooling / Cooling System. ODI number 11754975 for the petition text. [oemdtc.com/investigation/DP26006]
2. **Scope:** ~87,000 Grand Highlander Hybrids, 2024-2026 model years (per Autoblog); petition covers 2024-2026 Grand Highlander generally. [autoblog.com, Aug 2026]
3. **The alleged chain:** large open grille apertures with no protective screen/mesh between grille and heat exchangers → rocks/debris during normal driving strike exchangers → coolant/refrigerant leakage → sudden, unexpected loss of propulsion. [lionlemon.com; oemdtc.com]
4. **Three exchangers in the line of fire:** hybrid inverter radiator, A/C condenser, main radiator. (Hybrid-specific: a punctured inverter radiator faults the hybrid system, not just the engine.) [autoblog.com]
5. **Complaint volume:** 13 existing ODI complaints + petitioner's own = 14 total under consideration at opening. ODI will evaluate causal relation and run a technical review under 49 CFR Part 552. This is the earliest formal stage: NHTSA decides only whether to grant or deny the petition; grant leads to PE, then EA, then possibly recall. [oemdtc.com; lionlemon.com]
6. **Zero crashes, injuries, fires, or fatalities tied to these incidents so far** (NHTSA, per Autoblog). Damage occurred during "totally normal driving," not off-roading. [autoblog.com]
7. **Related but distinct:** July 7, 2026 Canadian recall of 61 2026 Grand Highlander/Lexus TX SUVs for improperly manufactured rear axle carriers (knuckles) — different defect, do NOT conflate. [topclassactions.com]

## Counterargument (full-strength, for the panel)

- 14 complaints across ~87,000 vehicles is a 0.016% report rate. That is noise-adjacent.
- Rock damage is classically a "road hazard," not a defect; insurers and automakers have excluded it forever.
- No crashes, injuries, or fires. NHTSA denies most defect petitions; opening is not a finding.
- Grand Highlander is Toyota's flagship 3-row with strong reliability reputation; hybrid system otherwise praised.

## Why it survives the kill test

- Novel mechanism never covered on this site: a styling decision (open grille) as the defect, not a bad part.
- Hybrid-specific amplification: inverter radiator puncture kills the hybrid system, and the Grand Highlander Hybrid is the high-volume variant.
- Fresh (petition < 3 months old), federal, 87k vehicles in scope, zero mainstream coverage beyond auto press.
- Clean full-queue dup check: no prior "grand-highlander", "dp26006", "heat-exchanger" entries (verified 2026-10-10).

## Actionable takeaway (required gate)

- Owners of 2024-2026 Grand Highlander: after gravel-road or construction-zone driving, check under the front bumper for coolant smell or wet spots; heed coolant-temp warnings immediately (pull over, shut down).
- Check NHTSA's complaint/investigation pages for DP26006 updates; if you've had this failure, file a complaint at nhtsa.gov (it feeds ODI).
- Aftermarket grille mesh screens exist as a mitigation; verify warranty implications with your dealer before installing anything that touches the cooling airflow.
