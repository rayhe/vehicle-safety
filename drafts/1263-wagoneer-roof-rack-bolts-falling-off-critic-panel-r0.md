## 1. General Editor — 9.0
The 100-percent hook lands in the lede and the risk-asymmetry paragraph gives the story its spine: the victim is the driver behind, who gets no letter and no warning. Structure follows the template (lede, pull stats, model breakdown, counterweight, remedy credit, action). The arithmetic is shown, not asserted, and the four model counts summing exactly to 97,349 earns reader trust. Minor: the "human ear at 70 miles per hour" line is vivid but slightly overplays a warning FCA itself lists; it is framed as commentary, not fact, so it stands.

## 2. Voice Coach — 8.5
Clara's voice is distinct and sustained: direct, practical, angry on behalf of consumers ("Before you sign anything on a big three-row SUV, check the roof"). It cannot be swapped with Rex or Mia without rewriting. Loses half a point because the opening sentence runs long before it lands its punch, and the rhythm fix round merged several fragments that originally gave the lede more snap. The revision was worth it for the rhythm gate, but the lede is slightly less punchy than the first draft. Not worth another round on its own.

## 3. Ethics Reviewer — 9.0
No invented victims, no crash count claimed where none exists, and the disclaimer states plainly that no crashes, injuries, or deaths are attributed to this defect. The 100% figure is presented as a filing estimate with its limits explained at full strength, not as a prediction that every crossbar will detach. Owners are told what to do, and the advice to following drivers is honestly labeled as unsatisfying rather than dressed up as a real defense.

## 4. Social Shareability — 8.5
"Never tightened right" plus "the driver behind you pays" is a two-beat share hook, and Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer owners are an identifiable, high-income audience who will forward this to each other. Loses half a point because a roof rack bolt is abstract compared with a wheel falling off; the article has to teach the torque concept before the danger lands, and some scrollers will not wait for the second paragraph.

## 5. Legal Accuracy — 9.0

The supplier identification is attributed to GCN's reporting, not presented as a directly opened filing.
