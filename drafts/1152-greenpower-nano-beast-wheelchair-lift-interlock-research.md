# RESEARCH — #1152: greenpower-nano-beast-wheelchair-lift-interlock

## Story candidate
Greenpower Motor Company recall 26V643000: certain 2021 Greenpower NANO BEAST school buses can move with the wheelchair lift deployed — a federal safety interlock (FMVSS 403/404, in force since Dec 27, 2004) defeated.

## Kill test
- Newsworthy: YES. Recall announced week of Oct 12, 2026. Electric school buses bought with public money (EPA Clean School Bus program; Greenpower's Nano BEAST went to West Virginia districts in a $15M deal). The victims in this scenario are wheelchair-using schoolchildren.
- Novel angle: YES. A "software interlock error" class of failure is spreading — the same week's commercial recall roundup also lists a Greenpower EV Star / EV Star Mobility Plus recall (2018–2024 EV Star, 2020–2023 EV Star Mobility Plus, 94 units) for a control module interlock software error allowing the bus to move while the wheelchair lift is deployed. Pattern, not one-off.
- Data-backed: recall campaign, FMVSS 403/404 text, prior interlock recalls (e.g., Collins Bus 08V498000, 8 school buses, same failure mode).
- Not covered: no greenpower wheelchair-lift story exists in drafts/queue (checked: no "wheelchair" drafts; #1136 was brake hose; #1143 was E-One fire trucks).
