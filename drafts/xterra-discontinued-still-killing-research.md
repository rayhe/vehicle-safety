# Research: Nissan Xterra — Discontinued in 2015, Still Killing at 1.39 per 100M VMT

## Core Data (FARS 2014-2023)
- **Deaths:** 456
- **Crashes:** 853 (fatal crash involvements)
- **Rate:** 1.39 deaths per 100M VMT
- **Lethality:** 53.5% (occupant dies in 53.5% of fatal crash involvements)
- **Fleet:** ~262,500
- **Annual deaths:** 45.6/year
- **Impairment:** 21.4% any (125 alcohol, 72 drug, 169 any of 788 drivers) — BELOW average

## Model Year Deaths
- MY 2000: 51, MY 2001: 45, MY 2002: 71, MY 2003: 56, MY 2004: 66
- MY 2005: 37, MY 2006: 24, MY 2007: 40, MY 2008: 25
- MY 2009: 5, MY 2010: 9, MY 2011: 6, MY 2012: 12, MY 2015: 5
- First gen (2000-2004): 289 deaths (63.4% of total)
- Second gen (2005-2015): 163 deaths

## Key Angles
1. **Discontinued but deadly:** Last built in 2015, still kills 45.6 people/year
2. **Platform sibling gap:** Pathfinder (same Nissan F-Alpha platform) kills at 0.93 — 33% lower rate
3. **Not a behavior problem:** 21.4% impairment is below SUV average. The vehicle is the problem.
4. **First-gen dominance:** 63% of deaths come from 2000-2004 models (20+ years old, pre-ESC)
5. **No IIHS Top Safety Pick ever earned** — Xterra was never rated highly for crashworthiness
