# Research: Ford Expedition Chrome Trim Recall

## News Hook
- **Recall:** NHTSA campaign 26S38, announced June 9, 2026
- **Vehicles affected:** 548,463 Ford Expeditions (2018-2024 model years)
- **Defect:** Chrome plating on center console bubbles and peels, creating sharp edges
- **Injuries:** 65 reported injuries (hand/finger lacerations), some requiring medical attention
- **Cause:** Suppliers (Xin Point and Forvia) manufactured chrome trim with "parameters that do not meet Ford specifications"
- **Fix:** Dealers replace the console trim, free of charge

## FARS Data
- Ford Expedition: 1,515 deaths, 2.31 rate per 100M VMT, 525,000 fleet
- 3rd highest death rate among full-size SUVs (behind Yukon 2.55 and Tahoe 2.49)
- 151.5 deaths per year average

## Angle
The Expedition is already one of the deadliest full-size SUVs on American roads (2.31 rate, 1,515 FARS deaths). Now its INTERIOR is attacking its occupants. Chrome trim that peels into blades. The supplier didn't meet Ford's specs. Ford didn't catch it for 7 model years (2018-2024). The recall covers essentially every Expedition sold in the last 6 years.

The absurdity: a $55,000 family hauler whose center console can slice your hand while you're reaching for the climate controls. Not in a crash. Not at speed. Just sitting in your parked Expedition, adjusting the temperature.
