# Research: Ford's 60 Recalls Tell the Same Story FARS Already Told

## Angle
Ford leads all manufacturers with 60 recalls in 2026 — 3× more than second-place Chrysler (24). Ford's VP of Quality Jeff Levine says "our newest 2026 and 2027 models make up just 3.3% of this year's recall volume." He's right. FARS data confirms it from the body count side: Ford's 2013-2020 model year vehicles have 30× more deaths than their 2021+ fleet. Both the recall system and the morgue are processing the same design era.

- **Total Ford deaths (all-time FARS):** 34,954

## Novel Contribution
This is the first analysis connecting Ford's 2026 recall concentration (96.7% from pre-2026 designs) with FARS fatality data showing the same design era accounts for 30× more deaths per vintage year. The recall system and the fatality database are independently processing the same engineering legacy.

## Strongest Counterargument
The 30:1 ratio is partly an exposure artifact. 2021-2023 model year vehicles have been on the road for far fewer total miles than 2013-2020 vehicles. Fleet exposure naturally concentrates deaths in older vintages. The ratio will narrow as newer vehicles accumulate mileage. Ford's death RATE (not count) for newer models may or may not be lower — we don't have VMT-adjusted rates by model year in our data.
