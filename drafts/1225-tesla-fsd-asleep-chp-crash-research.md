## Core facts (verified)

- **Crash:** Just before midnight Tuesday Oct 13, 2026, eastbound I-80 in Fairfield, CA, west of Air Base Parkway. Officers were on scene for an earlier fatal crash (about an hour prior: a 19-year-old from San Pablo lost control of a Honda Civic and was struck by a Chevrolet Tahoe; he died). Four lanes already shut down behind flares and cones when the Tesla entered at freeway speed and struck the parked Dodge Charger patrol car. Dashcam footage (Charger + another scene vehicle) shows the Tesla driving through the cordon before impact. [Electrek, Oct 16, 2026]
- **Driver:** 22-year-old from Sacramento. Told investigators he was asleep with Tesla's Full Self-Driving engaged. CHP has not released model or software version. Driver's statement is the main evidence; FSD engagement unconfirmed; vehicle logs will settle it. [Electrek; Digital Trends]
- **Injuries:** Both officers seriously injured, later released from hospital. Driver minor injuries (some reports say no injuries; minor is Electrek's account). No charges confirmed; investigation open. [Electrek; tech-insider.org]
- **Federal response:** Federal regulators opened an inquiry (per NBC News/KQED via tesery, Oct 17). [tesery]
- **History — the regulatory loop:**
  - Aug 2021: NHTSA opens Autopilot probe after series of Teslas crashed into stopped police cars/fire trucks (many at night with flares/cones/flashing lights). Started with 11 crashes, ~765,000 vehicles. [Digital Trends]
  - Dec 2023: Tesla recalls 2,031,220 US vehicles under 23V-838 for insufficient Autopilot misuse controls; OTA remedy added driver-attention alerts. [Digital Trends]
  - Apr 2024: NHTSA opens recall query RQ24009 on whether the 23V-838 fix was adequate; preliminary analysis found post-remedy crashes. [Digital Trends]
  - Oct 2024: NHTSA opens FSD reduced-visibility evaluation, ~2.4M vehicles.
  - Aug 2025: Florida federal jury finds Tesla 33% responsible in 2019 fatal Autopilot crash, ~$243M awarded; federal judge upholds Feb 2026. [Digital Trends]
  - Oct 2025: NHTSA opens FSD traffic-violation investigation, 2.88M vehicles.
  - Mar 2026: NHTSA escalates FSD visibility probe to engineering analysis EA26002, est. 3,203,754 vehicles, after 9 crashes (1 fatal). [Digital Trends]
- **Driver-monitoring angle:** FSD (Supervised) uses a cabin camera to check driver attention, but Tesla's own manual says it won't activate when covered, in poor lighting, or when the driver wears sunglasses/hat. Electrek reported Tesla drivers can bypass it with a dollar bill; Electrek also reported Musk's Dec 2025 claim that FSD drivers can "text and drive depending on context." July 2026: driver filmed apparently asleep at ~100 km/h in BC with two kids in car, wearing large sunglasses. A US congressman cited 43 videos of sleeping drivers in pressing DOT. [Electrek; Digital Trends]
- **Precedent:** June 2024, Fullerton CA: Tesla whose driver said "self-drive" engaged while on his phone hit a parked police car at a fatal-crash scene; officer moved aside. FSD/Autopilot engagement never confirmed. [Electrek; Digital Trends]
- **Name fight:** Tesla agreed to drop "Full Self-Driving" name in Europe (Germany called it misleading); offered "Tesla Assisted Driving." US name unchanged. [Electrek]
- **Comparisons:** GM Super Cruise / Ford BlueCruise restrict hands-free to mapped highways, use IR cameras; NHTSA escalated BlueCruise probe after 2 fatal night crashes into stationary vehicles. Waymo: IIHS July 2026 study found 68% fewer crashes per mile than human drivers in studied cities (lidar+radar, mapped roads). [Digital Trends]
