## Verified facts (3+ primary/secondary sources)
1. **NHTSA campaign 26V685 (26V685000), Subaru WRI26**, report received Oct 19, 2026. Covers certain 2026 Subaru Crosstrek and Forester vehicles, 8 units total. Component: suspension, front control arm, lower ball joint. Defect: castle nut on the left-front lower control arm ball joint may be improperly fastened; ball joint may separate from the lower control arm, causing loss of vehicle control. Remedy: inspect and replace the left-front lower control arm ball joint assembly as necessary, free of charge. Owners advised not to drive until remedy completed. Owner letters expected Dec 16, 2026. Subaru customer service 1-844-373-6614.
2. **Autoblog (Oct 2026):** 4 Crosstreks built Aug 4–6, 2026, and 4 Foresters built Aug 5–13, 2026, identified via supplier records and traceability. Root cause: some assemblies were placed in a finished-goods rack before the final tightening procedure was completed, then shipped to the assembly line; some affected assemblies lacked a recorded inspection pass result. Trigger: field report Sept 15, 2026, of a separated ball joint; Subaru began 100% inspection of castle nut fastening Sept 16 and investigated through Oct 9. No preceding warning before failure, per Subaru.
3. **KBB (Oct 2026):** confirms 8 vehicles, do-not-drive instruction, free repair, towing provided, owner notification mid-December.
4. **CarPro weekly recalls roundup (Oct 2026):** same campaign details, VINs searchable on NHTSA.gov Oct 20, 2026; lists the week's other do-not-drive (Porsche centerlock expansion, +548 cars) for scale contrast.
5. **Contrast scale:** Porsche's centerlock do-not-drive expansion the same week added 548 cars to 1,851 already recalled — 68x the Subaru population — yet both warnings read identically. Subaru's 8 cars are 0.4% of one percent of Porsche's expansion alone.

## Actionable takeaway (required)
- Own a 2026 Crosstrek or Forester built in early August 2026? Check the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls now, not when the December letter arrives. If yours is one of the eight, do not drive it; Subaru provides towing and a free fix. Build date is on the driver-door certification label.
