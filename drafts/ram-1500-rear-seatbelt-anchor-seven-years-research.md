# Research: Ram 1500 Rear Seatbelt Anchor — Seven Years of Assembly Failures

## Angle
One of America's best-selling vehicles was assembled for seven consecutive model years (2019–2026) with rear seatbelt buckle anchors that may not have been properly attached to the vehicle body. That is 1.27 million trucks in the U.S. where the seatbelt — in the seat where your kids sit — might physically detach from the frame in a crash.

## Kill Test
**Pass.** This is not just another recall story. The angle is the duration (seven model years), the location (back seat, where children ride), the scale (1.27M), and the timing (months after IIHS exposed the entire industry's rear-seat protection gap). The irony: parents who chose trucks over minivans for "safety" may have been strapping their children into seatbelts anchored to nothing.

## Primary Sources
1. **NHTSA Recall 67D / Stellantis** — 1,271,294 vehicles in U.S., 2019–2026 Ram 1500, second-row center and driver-side seatbelt buckle anchors not properly attached to body structure during assembly
   - Source: NHTSA recall database, Stellantis voluntary recall disclosure (July 31 / Aug 1, 2026)
   - Reuters: https://www.reuters.com/world/stellantis-recall-15-million-ram-1500-pickup-trucks-over-seat-belt-issue-2026-07-31/
   - MotorTrend: https://www.motortrend.com/news/ram-1500-pickup-truck-recall-seat-belt-anchor

2. **IIHS 2026 Rear-Seat Protection** — Updated moderate overlap front crash test with rear-seat dummy (12-year-old/small woman). Zero minivans earned a safety award. Fewer than half of new vehicles have advanced rear-seat restraint systems.
   - IIHS: https://www.iihs.org/news/detail/new-crash-test-spotlights-lagging-protection-for-rear-passengers
   - Autoblog: https://www.autoblog.com/news/no-minivans-make-iihs-safety-list-as-rear-seat-safety-falls-short

3. **NHTSA FARS Rear-Seat Fatality Data** — IIHS research shows risk of fatal injury is now HIGHER for belted rear-seat occupants than for front-seat occupants, because front-seat technology (pretensioners, force limiters, airbags) has advanced while the back seat largely hasn't.
   - IIHS: "the risk of a fatal injury is now higher for belted occupants in the second row than for those in front"

4. **Ram 1500 Sales Data** — Ram 1500 is consistently a top-3 best-selling vehicle in America (behind F-150 and Silverado). ~500K+ annual sales.

5. **Stellantis Recall Track Record** — Stellantis has faced multiple recent large recalls (Dodge Hornet/Alfa Romeo Tonale twisting seatbelts Aug 2026, Pacifica fire recalls, Takata airbag do-not-drive orders).

## Key Data Points
- 1,271,294 affected vehicles (U.S.)
- 1.5 million+ globally
- Seven model years: 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 (actually 8 model years)
- Assembly plants: Sterling Heights Assembly Plant (Michigan) primary, Saltillo (Mexico) supplemental
- One injury reported, zero fatalities
- Voluntary recall (not NHTSA-ordered)
- Recall number: 67D
- Remedy: dealers inspect and re-attach if needed, free of charge
