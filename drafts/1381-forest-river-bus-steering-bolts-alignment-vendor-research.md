# Research: The Alignment Vendor Recall (Article #1381)

**Journalist:** Mia Crumplezone (Safety Engineering Editor)
**Kicker:** Investigation
**News peg:** NHTSA campaign 26V705, filed October 23, 2026. Forest River Bus recalls 490 transit buses (2026-2027) because front caster bushing bolts may be loose or missing after third-party front-end alignments done before shipping. Forest River estimates 100% of the recalled buses carry the defect.
**Kill test verdict:** PROCEED. Newsworthy: a routine pre-delivery service (wheel alignment) became the defect vector; a 100% defect estimate is rare against the 1-3% typical of most recalls; the flag-to-recall-vote took 3 days; and no prior article on this site covers 26V705 (verified: zero matches for 26V705, caster, or caster bushing across all drafts, including the Nov 2 commercial-recall wave pieces #1369-#1373). Novel angle: the vendor as the defect, plus the torque-spec mechanics of what a caster bushing bolt actually holds.

## The facts

### The recall (26V705, Oct 23, 2026)
- Forest River Bus, LLC (Goshen, IN) filed NHTSA campaign **26V705** (manufacturer number **05-2110**).
- Population: **490** transit buses, model years **2026-2027**, across **27 vehicle lines**: Starcraft Starlite, Starcraft Allstar, Starcraft Allstar XL, Senator II, Senator II HD, P/S2, Glaval Universal, Glaval Titan II, Glaval Primetime, Glaval Legacy, Glaval Entourage, Glaval Concorde II, Glaval Commute, Elkhart Coach, Eldorado, Champion, and StarTrans Candidate II.
- Estimated percentage with defect: **100%** (per the Part 573 filing).
- Defect: "Aftermarket caster bushing and retaining hardware may not have been torqued to the proper specification by the third-party vendor." The chronology adds buses "may have been improperly torqued or missing retaining hardware and bushings."
- Safety risk: "If the front caster bushing and bolt is loose or missing, there is an increased risk of loss of vehicle control, leading to a crash."
- Warning sign (per filing): "Steering control and the front end of the vehicle may shake severely."
- Cause (per filing): "Vendor Error."
- Scope method: production records of which vehicles had been sent to a front-end alignment vendor.

### Chronology (per Part 573)
- **Oct 16, 2026:** Forest River's Office of Corporate Compliance (OCC) made aware of the matter.
- **Oct 19, 2026:** OCC opened a research item AND the Corporate Committee voted to recall (same day).
- **Oct 23, 2026:** Part 573 filed with NHTSA.
- Flag-to-vote: 3 days. Flag-to-filing: 7 days.
- The filing's chronology starts with an internal compliance flag, not a crash report, and it names no crashes, injuries, or field reports.

### Remedy and owner actions
- Remedy: dealer inspection to determine whether the front caster bushing and bolt are present and properly torqued; install missing hardware and apply correct torque. All parts and labor free.
- Owner notification letters expected **December 21, 2026** (per NHTSA-derived recall record).
- Forest River customer service: **1-800-348-7440**, reference recall 05-2110.
- VIN check: nhtsa.gov/recalls.

## What caster is (for the article's explainer)
Caster is the forward/backward tilt of the steering axis. Positive caster makes the wheels want to track straight and self-center after a turn. The caster bushing and its bolt hold that geometry at the front end. A loose or missing bolt lets the geometry wander, which is why the filing's warning symptom is severe front-end shake: the wheel is no longer held at its designed angle.
