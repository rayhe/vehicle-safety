# Research: Dodge Journey — 11 Years, No AEB, 317 Dead

## Angle
Clara Rollover consumer safety piece. The Dodge Journey was sold for 11 model years (2009–2020) on a 2006-era Mitsubishi GS platform. It never received automatic emergency braking. IIHS rated its small overlap front crash protection **Poor**. 317 people died in it. When Chrysler finally killed it, they replaced it with the Dodge Hornet — which was just recalled (Aug 10, 2026) for seatbelt retractors that don't retract.

## Kill test
- Genuinely surprising? YES — an $80B parent company sold a crossover for 11 years without ever adding the single most impactful crash-avoidance technology (AEB). IIHS estimates AEB reduces rear-end crashes by 50%.
- Novel angle on data? YES — the model year death distribution shows the 2018 model year had MORE deaths (43) than 2009 (35), despite being nearly a decade into production. The vehicle got deadlier with age as the rest of the industry advanced.
- Would a reader share this? Absolutely — "they replaced the death trap with a different kind of broken."

- Moderate overlap front: Good
- Side: Good
- Roof strength: Good

## NHTSA Ratings
- Overall: 4/5 stars
- Frontal driver: 5/5
- Frontal passenger: 4/5
- Side barrier: 5/5
- Rollover: 4/5 (18.5% risk)

## Platform
- Built on Mitsubishi GS platform (2006 design, Mitsubishi Global Engine)
- Fiat Freemont was the European version
- The platform predates all modern crash avoidance requirements
- 2011 refresh updated interior/engine but NOT structure

## AEB Gap
- IIHS began recommending AEB as standard in 2015
- 20 automakers signed voluntary AEB commitment in 2016 (target: standard by Sept 2022)
- Chrysler was a signatory but the Journey never got AEB
- The Journey was discontinued Dec 2020 — 2 years before the deadline
- Honda CR-V had standard AEB from 2017
- Toyota RAV4 had standard AEB from 2018
- Even the Nissan Rogue had standard AEB from 2018

## Dodge Hornet Recall Connection
- Aug 10, 2026: 40,386 Dodge Hornets recalled (NHTSA 26V510)
- Issue: rear outboard seatbelt retractors may become twisted, preventing retraction
- Also: 8,391 Alfa Romeo Tonales (same platform — Stellantis Small Wide 4x4)
- This is the Journey's spiritual successor — same price bracket, same market position
- Supplier: Autoliv ASP Inc. — design weakness identified

## Comparison Vehicles
- Honda CR-V: 2,072 deaths, rate 0.53, but with standard AEB from 2017
- Toyota RAV4: 914 deaths, rate 0.19
- Ford Escape: 2,284 deaths, rate 0.95
- Nissan Rogue: 968 deaths, rate 0.35
- Journey's rate (0.41) looks acceptable until you realize it had NO crash avoidance tech
