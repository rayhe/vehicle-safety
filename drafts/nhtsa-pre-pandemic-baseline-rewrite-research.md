# Research: NHTSA's "Pre-Pandemic Return" Claim vs. Actual Numbers

## Core Finding
NHTSA's July 2026 press release claims 2025 speeding fatalities (est. 10,035) represent "a return to pre-pandemic numbers last seen in 2019." But 2019 FARS-confirmed speeding deaths were 9,478. NHTSA is calling 10,035 "pre-pandemic" when it's 5.9% (557 deaths) ABOVE the actual 2019 baseline.

## The Math
- 2019 speeding deaths (actual): 9,478
- 2025 speeding deaths (NHTSA estimate): 10,035
- Difference: +557 deaths (+5.9%)
- NHTSA calls this "a return to pre-pandemic numbers"

For total deaths:
- 2019: 36,096
- 2024 (latest final): 39,254
- Difference: +3,158 deaths (+8.7%)

For fatality rate:
- 2019: 1.10 per 100M VMT
- 2025: 1.10 per 100M VMT
- These ARE equal — but only because VMT increased proportionally

## Novel Finding
NHTSA achieves the "pre-pandemic return" narrative in two ways:
1. Comparing RATES (which are equal) instead of COUNTS (which are higher)
2. Rounding 10,035 down to "pre-pandemic level" when the actual 2019 number was 9,478

The rate being equal at 1.10 means the RISK per mile is back to 2019 levels. But Americans drive more miles than in 2019, so more people die. NHTSA highlights the rate, buries the count.
