# Research: Hyundai/Kia's Year-Long Seatbelt Crisis

## News Peg
In the 12 months from September 2025 to July 2026, Hyundai Motor Group (Hyundai, Kia, Genesis) issued FOUR separate seatbelt recalls covering ~916,700 vehicles. Each recall involves a different model, a different defect mechanism, and a different supplier failure — but they all hit the same safety component.

## The Four Recalls (chronological)

### 1. Hyundai Palisade — Sep 2025
- **Vehicles:** 568,580 2020-2025 Palisades
- **Defect:** Seatbelt buckle assemblies with out-of-specification components; interference in latch channel causes increased friction, especially in cold temperatures
- **Supplier issue:** Out-of-spec components from unnamed supplier
- **NHTSA awareness:** Industry reporting

### 2. Hyundai/Genesis — Apr 2026
- **Vehicles:** 294,128 (Ioniq 6 2023-2025, Genesis G90 2023-2026, Santa Fe/Hybrid 2024-2026)
- **Defect:** Front seatbelt snap-on anchors may detach from seat frame
- **Details:** Damaged anchor NOT caused by user or assembly error (per Hyundai investigation). Problem replicable only in certain rear-end crashes. New anchor design introduced in production March 2026.
- **Source:** NHTSA, Reuters

### 3. Kia Telluride — Jun 2026
- **Vehicles:** 6,264 2027 Tellurides & Telluride Hybrids (built Mar 24 - May 12, 2026)
- **Defect:** Driver seatbelt emergency locking retractor (ELR) may lock, preventing webbing from extending — rendering the belt UNUSABLE
- **Supplier:** Samsong Mexico — incorrect vehicle sensor installed
- **Note:** Fails FMVSS 209

### 4. Hyundai Kona — Jul 2026
- **Vehicles:** 47,749 (2026 Kona: 47,733; 2025 Kona Electric: 16)
- **Defect:** Rear center seatbelt buckle manufactured outside specification; may fail to properly restrain occupant in crash
- **Supplier:** Joyson Safety Systems — stamping die not properly maintained, worn from overuse
- **Advisory:** Do not use rear center seating position until repaired
- **Joyson connection:** Joyson acquired Takata's assets. Previous Joyson recalls include inflator rupture issues.

## Total Tally
- **916,721 vehicles** across 4 recalls
- **4 different defect types:** buckle friction, anchor detachment, retractor lockup, out-of-spec buckle
- **3+ suppliers:** unnamed (Palisade), Samsong Mexico (Telluride), Joyson Safety Systems (Kona), plus Hyundai's own design (Ioniq 6/G90/Santa Fe)
- **Multiple assembly plants:** South Korea, Alabama, Mexico

## FARS Seatbelt Data Context
- FARS 2014-2023 data: ~9% of fatal crash occupants are unbelted
- Unbelted occupants account for ~47% of all fatalities
- Seatbelts reduce fatality risk by ~45% in cars and ~60% in SUVs/pickups (NHTSA)
- An estimated 14,955 lives saved by seatbelts in 2017 alone (NHTSA)
- Rear center seat has lowest seatbelt compliance (~55-60%)

## The Systemic Pattern
This is NOT a single supplier failure or a single design oversight. Four different seatbelt mechanisms, across different model lines, different plants, and different suppliers — all from the same corporate group, all in one year. The common factor is Hyundai Motor Group's quality assurance and supplier oversight process.

## IIHS Irony

- Palisade: Just earned TSP+ in May 2026 (after rear seatbelt buckle improvements)
- Telluride: Multiple years of TSP+
- Ioniq 6: TSP+ winner
- Genesis G90: TSP+

Every single recalled model has been an IIHS safety award winner.
