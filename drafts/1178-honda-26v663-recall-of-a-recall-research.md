# Research Notes — #1178: Honda 26V663, the Recall of a Recall

**Slug:** 1178-honda-26v663-recall-of-a-recall
**Journalist:** Rex Driverton (Investigation kicker — deadpan paradox beat)
**Date:** 2026-10-15

## The story in one line
Honda issued a recall of its own recall remedy: 4,804 already-"fixed" Accords and CR-Vs need to come back to the dealer because the supplier shipped defective pre-countermeasure tire-kit nozzles AS the fix parts.

## Verified facts (primary sources only)

1. **NHTSA Recall 26V663 / Honda campaign PPW** (Honda Part 573 report, published Oct 14, 2026, hondanews.com):
   - Population: **4,804 vehicles** — 2,058 Accord Hybrid (2023-2026), 2,745 CR-V Hybrid (2023-2026), **1** CR-V Fuel Cell EV (2025).
   - Estimated percentage with the defect: **1%**.
   - Defect: tire repair kit nozzle with a one-way valve; if connected improperly to the tire valve, pressure builds in the sealant bottle; if the relief valve fails, the bottle cap can detach (projectile / injury risk).
   - Cause: "During preparation of countermeasure parts for the remedy of NHTSA Recall ID 26V-366, the supplier did not implement adequate change-point management which resulted in the absence of appropriate disposal controls for pre-countermeasure parts, allowing those parts to be mixed with countermeasure parts in the supplier's warehouse. Subsequently, the supplier incorrectly shipped pre-countermeasure parts."
   - Remedy: dealers inspect and replace the nozzle; the remedy component "does not have a one-way valve" — the fix is deletion of the valve.
   - Dealer notification ~10/9/2026; owner letters ~11/16/2026.
2. **Parent recall 26V-366** (June 2026): 1,049,883 vehicles (744,350 CR-V Hybrid, 305,013 Accord Hybrid, 340 CR-V Fuel Cell EV). Carscoops reported 8 injuries from the kit before the original recall.
3. **NHTSA/oemdtc summary of 26V663**: VINs searchable on NHTSA.gov starting **October 17, 2026**; Honda customer service 1-866-784-1870; confirms vehicles "previously repaired under recall 26V366 will need to have the new remedy completed."
4. Press (Oct 14-15): Autoblog, MotorBiscuit, Car and Driver (via scoopfeeds) — all confirm second-recall framing.

## Original contribution (novel math)
- 4,804 is **0.46% of the 1,049,883** parent-recall population.
- Honda estimates only 1% of those 4,804 actually carry the defective nozzle → roughly **~48 vehicles** nationwide hold a bad kit. Honda must recall all 4,804 because the warehouse mix-up destroyed traceability: nobody knows WHICH remedy boxes contained the pre-countermeasure nozzles.
- Contrast with this week's other failed remedies: GM's L87 (EA26005, ~1M vehicles — engineering fix didn't fix engines) and Sierra RQ26003 (remedy didn't hold). Honda's is the only one where the *engineering* fix was correct and the *supply chain* ate the homework. Three flavors of "the fix didn't fix it" in one month — Honda's is the purest supply-chain farce.

## Counterargument (stated at full strength)
The honest case against the farce framing: this is the recall system working as designed. A supplier caught a warehouse contamination, traced the population to 4,804, disclosed it, and re-recall
ed. The 1% defect estimate means ~4,756 owners get a precautionary letter. Honda did the responsible thing. The comedy is in the optics, not the conduct — and 8 people were hurt before the first recall, so a second bite at the remedy is cheap insurance, not bureaucracy.

## Limitations
- We don't know the supplier's name (Honda's report doesn't name it).
- 1% defect estimate is Honda's own figure, unverified externally.
- No data on how many of the 4,804 have already been remedied under 26V663.
- FARS data not used here — this is a recall-story, not a fatality-rate analysis; methodology note will say so.

## Actionable takeaway
If you own a 2023-2026 Accord Hybrid or CR-V Hybrid and already had the June 2026 tire-kit recall done: check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls starting October 17, 2026. You may owe the dealer a second visit. And if you get a flat before then, don't improvise with the sealant kit.
