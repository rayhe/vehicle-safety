## 3. Ethics Reviewer — 9.0/10
Fair to older drivers: the advice is to remove one specific trip (night driving on unfamiliar divided highways), not to stop driving, and the article explains the volume-vs-rate distinction (young drivers supply the volume, old drivers the rate) rather than letting the 20x odds ratio do the stereotyping. The Thanksgiving-night hook is explicitly labeled a calendar coincidence, not a statistic, which is the honest way to use it. No victim-blaming, no self-congratulation, and the safety advice (move right, call 911, pull over, hazards) is standard and safe.
