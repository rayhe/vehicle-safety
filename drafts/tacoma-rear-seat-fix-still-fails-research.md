# Research: Toyota Tacoma Rear-Seat Fix Still Fails

## Angle
Toyota redesigned the Tacoma for 2024 — the safest generation ever. For 2026, Toyota specifically modified the rear seat pan and seat belt load limiters to improve rear-seat protection after the 2025 model earned a Poor rating in the IIHS updated moderate overlap front test. Despite those targeted improvements, the 2026 Tacoma crew cab still earned only a Marginal rating overall — with the rear passenger restraints and dummy kinematics rated Poor. The dummy's head nearly struck the seatback during the crash.

Toyota tried to fix the back seat. The fix wasn't enough.

## Key Data Points

### IIHS 2026 Tacoma Crew Cab - Updated Moderate Overlap Front Test
- Overall: Marginal (M)
- Structure and safety cage: Good (G)
- Driver injury measures: All Good
- Rear passenger injury measures: Head/neck Good, Chest Good, Thigh Good
- **Rear passenger restraints and dummy kinematics: Poor (P)** ← dragged entire rating to Marginal
- Test vehicle: 2026 Toyota Tacoma SR Double cab 4wd
- Note: "Beginning with 2026 models, changes were made to the rear seat pan and rear seat belt load limiters"

### IIHS 2025 Tacoma - Same Test (earlier version)
- Tacoma was rated POOR overall in the updated moderate overlap front test
- Source: IIHS "Small pickups fall short when it comes to rear-seat safety" article

### How Competitors Did (Updated Side Test, 2025 cycle)
- Chevrolet Colorado: Good (side), Marginal (moderate overlap front, 2026)
- GMC Canyon: Good (side) 
- Honda Ridgeline: Good (side), Poor (moderate overlap front, 2026)
- Nissan Frontier: Acceptable (side), Good (moderate overlap front, 2026)
- Ford Ranger: Acceptable (side), Marginal (moderate overlap front, 2026)

### FARS Data Context
- Toyota Tacoma: 2,274 deaths, rate 0.80 per 100M VMT, fleet 2.1M
- For reference: Ford Ranger rate 2.91, Chevy Colorado rate ~0.5 (estimated from data)
- Average model year of Tacoma in fatal crash: ~2006 (from existing article)

### Why the Rear Seat Matters
- IIHS launched updated moderate overlap front test after research showed rear-seat occupant fatality risk now EXCEEDS front-seat risk in newer vehicles
- Front seats improved dramatically with advanced airbags and load-limited belts
- Rear seats lag behind — load-limited seatbelts are still rare in rear positions
- The rear dummy simulates a 12-year-old child or small woman (108 lbs)
- IIHS quote: "the rear dummy's head came very close to striking the seatback, increasing the risk of head injuries"

### Consumer Context
- Tacoma crew cab: specifically bought by families who need the back seat
- Best-selling midsize truck in America
- Crew cab is 4-door, designed for passengers
- Toyota marketed the 2024 redesign as a major safety upgrade with Toyota Safety Sense 3.0
- Despite marketing, the structural crash protection for rear passengers remains deficient
