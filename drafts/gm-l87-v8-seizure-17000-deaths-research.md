# Research: GM L87 6.2L V8 Engine Seizure — NHTSA EA25-007

## Angle
NHTSA opened an Engineering Analysis (highest investigation level) into GM's L87 6.2L V8 engine for sudden seizure/loss of motive power. The affected vehicles were EXCLUDED from Recall 25V274, meaning GM already knew some L87 engines had problems, recalled a subset, but these 225+ complaints are from vehicles GM said were fine.

## Kill Test
- Genuinely surprising: GM's most expensive engine option ($4,000+ upgrade) in its most profitable vehicles is seizing without warning at highway speed.
- Novel data cross-tab: these vehicles (Silverado, Tahoe, Sierra, Yukon, Suburban, Escalade) collectively account for 17,226+ deaths in FARS data.
- The investigation specifically asks about connecting rods, crankshafts, lifters, valve springs, pistons, bearings — internal component failure.
- Model years 2019-2021 and 2024 are covered (skip 2022-2023 — those may be under Recall 25V274).

## Primary Sources
1. **NHTSA EA25-007** — Engineering Analysis opened March 10, 2026. 225 reports of L87 engine failure in vehicles NOT subject to Recall 25V274.
   - URL: https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/inv/2025/INIM-EA25007-40036.pdf
   - Subject vehicles: MY 2019-2021 and MY 2024 Silverado 1500, Suburban, Tahoe, Sierra 1500, Yukon, Yukon XL, Escalade, Escalade ESV
   - "Engine seizure due to internal engine component failure"
   - NHTSA requesting supplier data for: connecting rod, crankshaft, lifters, valve springs, piston, piston rings, rod bearings, main bearings
   
2. **FARS Data (2014-2023)**
   - Chevrolet Silverado: 9,591 deaths, rate 1.25/100M VMT, fleet 5,687,500
   - Chevrolet Tahoe: 2,592 deaths, rate 2.49/100M VMT
   - Chevrolet Suburban: 593 deaths, rate 1.36/100M VMT
   - GMC Sierra: 3,337 deaths, rate 1.01/100M VMT
   - GMC Yukon: 1,114 deaths, rate 2.55/100M VMT
   - Combined: 17,227 deaths across these five models
   - Note: Not all of these run the L87 6.2L — it's an option/top trim, not base engine

3. **NHTSA Recall 25V274** — A prior recall that covered SOME L87 engines. The EA25-007 investigation covers the vehicles NOT included in that recall, meaning the defect is broader than GM initially recognized.
