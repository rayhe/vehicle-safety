# Research Notes: Ford Explorer Ghost Seat Recline

## Story Angle
Ford's 2026-2027 Explorer and Lincoln Aviator have a driver's seat that reclines by itself when the owner walks up to the car, remote-starts it, or presses a memory button. 79,579 vehicles affected. 133 reports. Ford has no fix. This is the Explorer's SECOND seat recall in July 2026 (the first covered 387,911 vehicles for second-row seats tipping/sliding while driving). Together: ~467,000 Explorer/Aviator SUVs with seat issues in one month.

## Sources
1. NHTSA recall 26V-470 / Ford recall 26S53 (July 28, 2026)
   - 79,579 vehicles: 64,409 2026-2027 Explorers, 15,170 2026-2027 Aviators
   - Built Nov 24, 2025 – Jun 27, 2026
   - Triggers: remote unlock, remote start, approach with fob, memory button
   - Root: recline motor misinterprets seat position during memory recall
   - Obstacle detection/bounce-back "may not function as intended"
   - Risk: impingement or entrapment of second-row passengers, especially children
   - 133 reports, no injuries/crashes
   - NO FIX YET — "remedy currently under development"
   - Source: NHTSA, Autoblog, Carscoops, CarBuzz, TFLcar

2. Prior Explorer seat recall (26V-393 expansion, July 2026)
   - 387,911 2020-2026 Explorers and 2020-2027 Aviators
   - Second-row easy-entry switch bezel misaligns → seat tips/slides while driving
   - RE-DO of 2025 recall (25V393) — Ford found 5 more cases AFTER cutoff, plus 4 cases in vehicles that had already been "fixed"
   - 14 reports of unintended seat movement
   - Source: Carscoops

3. Hyundai Palisade power seat child death (March 2026)
   - 2-year-old killed in Ohio on March 7
   - Power-folding second/third-row seats failed to detect child
   - 61,093 Palisades recalled + 568 Tellurides
   - Global stop-sale
   - Source: Reuters, Autoblog, NHTSA

4. Ford 2026 recall volume
   - 13,518,991 vehicles recalled through July 28, 2026
   - 61 campaigns
   - More than 50% of all industry recalls (26.7M across 226 recalls)
   - Source: Carscoops

5. FARS data (2014-2023)
   - Ford Explorer: 3,797 deaths, 6,626 fatal crashes, rate 1.54/100M VMT
   - Death/crash ratio: 0.573 (57.3% of the time, the Explorer occupant dies)
   - Impairment rate: 19.5% any_pct (close to SUV average)

## Novel Contribution
- Cross-referencing the ghost seat recline with FARS Explorer crash data to show the Explorer is already involved in ~380 fatal crashes per year
- The "no fix" angle: Ford is selling vehicles it can't fix yet, and the remedy schedule is TBD
- The pattern: powered convenience features (memory seats, power-fold, easy-entry) creating new injury modes that didn't exist in manual-seat vehicles
- Two separate seat recalls in ONE month for the same vehicle platform
