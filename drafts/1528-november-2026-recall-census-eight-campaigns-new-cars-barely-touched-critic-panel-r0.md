## 3. Ethics Reviewer — 9.0/10
Counterargument is stated at full strength (by campaign count the split is 8 of 15, 53 percent, not 8 percent; populations are full-campaign totals, not 2026-only slices; the month is not over) and not strawmanned, then answered honestly rather than dismissed. No crash or injury counts are claimed where the filings state none. Scope is defined (filings November 1–12, equipment and tire campaigns excluded) and the census is not oversold: the article says plainly that 68,666 is the size of campaigns touching a 2026 vehicle, not the number of 2026 vehicles. Nothing tells the reader what to think about any manufacturer as a company, only what to do as an owner.
