# Research Notes — #1011: Range Rover Sport rear subframe cracks (26V613)

## Story thesis
The Range Rover Sport is cracking in places a luxury SUV should never crack. In August 2025, NHTSA campaign 25V514 recalled 2014-2017 Range Rover/Range Rover Sport for front suspension knuckle joints that could crack and detach suspension arms (121,509 vehicles per press). Thirteen months later, on Sept 24, 2026, NHTSA campaign 26V613 opened on the NEW generation: certain 2024-2026 Range Rover Sport vehicles can develop cracks in the REAR SUBFRAME, reducing structural integrity and crashworthiness. The remedy is not ready; interim owner letters go out Nov 20, 2026. Meanwhile the 2026 RRS has now drawn five separate NHTSA campaigns in 2026, including a recall covering exactly one vehicle (26V607, PCM circuit board misaligned during production).

## Kill test — PASS
- Novel? Yes. 26V613 is five days old with no press coverage found. The knuckle-to-subframe structural-cracking pattern across generations is an original frame. Site has zero coverage of 26V613 or 26V607.
- Newsworthy? Yes. Structural integrity recall on a flagship luxury SUV with no remedy available and owners driving unaware for ~8 more weeks.
- 3+ primary sources? Yes: NHTSA recalls API records for 26V613, 26V607, 25V514 (primary), NHTSA Part 573 RCLRPT-26V599-8186.pdf (DC-DC context), ConsumerAffairs Sept 28 2026 recall roundup, USA Today/Carscoops on 25V514 (121,509 figure).
- Data-driven? Yes: recall counts, campaign dates, interim-letter timeline arithmetic, the 2026 RRS five-campaign count.

## Primary sources
1. NHTSA recalls API — 26V613000, report received 24/09/2026. Component: STRUCTURE:FRAME AND MEMBERS. Summary: "certain 2024-2026 Range Rover Sport vehicles. Cracks may develop in the rear subframe." Consequence: "A cracked subframe may have reduced structural integrity, increasing the risk of injury during a crash. A crack may also diminish handling and control, increasing the risk of a crash." Remedy: dealers inspect and replace rear subframe as necessary, free. Interim letters expected Nov 20, 2026; additional letters once final remedy available. JLR customer service 1-800-637-6837.
2. NHTSA recalls API — 26V607000, report received 23/09/2026. Component: POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:CONTROL MODULE. "Recalling ONE 2026 Range Rover Sport vehicle. The circuit board in the powertrain control module (PCM) was misaligned during production and may be deformed." Consequence: loss of drive power. Interim letter Nov 20, 2026; final remedy pending.
3. NHTSA recalls API — 25V514000, report received 08/08/2025. "Certain 2014-2017 Range Rover and Range Rover Sport vehicles. The front suspension upper knuckle joint may crack, which can result in suspension arm detachment." Owner letters mailed May 11, 2026.
4. NHTSA Part 573 RCLRPT-26V599-8186.pdf — DC-DC converter recall 26V599 (23,677 vehicles, 2019-2024 models), Sept 17, 2026. Context for JLR's 2026 recall volume.
5. ConsumerAffairs recall roundup, week of Sept 28, 2026 — lists 26V607 (PCM loss of drive power, 2026 RRS) among the week's recalls; does NOT yet list 26V613, confirming freshness.
6. USA Today / Carscoops — 25V514 knuckle recall: 121,509 vehicles.

## Computations
- 2026 Range Rover Sport NHTSA campaigns in 2026: 5 — 26V005 (Jan 9, certification label weight), 26V097 (Feb 19, sunroof trim detach), 26V297 (May 12, seatbelt chime FMVSS 208), 26V607 (Sept 23, one-vehicle PCM), 26V613 (Sept 24, rear subframe cracks).
- Gap between structural-crack campaigns: 25V514 (Aug 8, 2025) to 26V613 (Sept 24, 2026) = ~13.5 months.
- Remedy gap for 26V613: Sept 24, 2026 to Nov 20, 2026 interim letters = ~8 weeks of owners driving with a known defect and no fix; the final remedy date is unspecified.
- 26V607 population: exactly 1 vehicle — the smallest possible recall population.

## Strongest counterargument
The knuckle recall (2014-2017) and the subframe recall (2024-2026) are different components on different vehicle generations, likely different suppliers and different root causes. Treating them as one "cracking pattern" may be narrative convenience rather than engineering fact. Additionally, the one-vehicle PCM recall arguably shows JLR's quality monitoring WORKING — catching a single misaligned circuit board before it caused harm. And NHTSA lists no crashes or injuries tied to the subframe cracks.

## Limitations
- 26V613 population size unknown — NHTSA recalls API does not include it and the Part 573 PDF was not fetched. Story must say "certain" vehicles without a count.
- No crash/injury data tied to the subframe defect in the sources reviewed.
- Root cause of the subframe cracking not stated in the API summary; the Part 573 chronology was not reviewed.

## Actionable for readers
- 2024-2026 Range Rover Sport owners: check VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls now; expect an interim letter around Nov 20, 2026; ask the dealer specifically about 26V613; a subframe is structural — do not defer the inspection once letters arrive.
