# Research: NHTSA Recall Query RQ26003 — GM Sierra Grille Deflector
**Article #1188** | Journalist: Rex Driverton | Date: 2026-10-16

## The Event
NHTSA's Office of Defects Investigation opened **Recall Query RQ26003** on October 9, 2026, examining whether GM's earlier recall for GMC Sierra 1500 upper grille deflector detachment (25V-060, filed Feb 6, 2025) was adequate in both **scope** and **remedy**.

## Key Numbers (verified)
- **535,639** — estimated population in RQ26003 (2022-2024 Sierra 1500)
- **70,768** — trucks covered by original recall 25V-060 (2022MY only, chrome deflector only)
- **7.6x** — scope expansion ratio (535,639 / 70,768)
- **118** — owner complaints in ODI's file (detachment while driving)
- **21** — complaints alleging detachment AFTER the recall remedy was performed (17.8% of complaints)
- **1,225** — complaints + 4 accident allegations GM counted by end of 2024 in its recall filing; no known injuries
- **64%** — repair completion rate on 25V-060 as of Jan 2026 (GM Authority)
- **8** — attachment points holding the deflector; GM's filing says they can fracture over time, and if all eight fail, the piece detaches at speed
- **0** — crashes, injuries, fatalities in ODI's opening resume

## Timeline (from GM's 25V-060 filing + press)
1. **Jan 19, 2024** — GM employee flags a deflector that came off a driven 2022 Sierra via internal "Speak Up for Safety" program
2. **Feb 27, 2024** — GM opens internal investigation
3. **May 23, 2024** — GM **closes** it: no defect trend found
4. **Dec 11, 2024** — GM **reopens** as warranty claims climb
5. **Feb 6, 2025** — Recall 25V-060 filed: 70,768 trucks (2022MY, chrome deflector, built Nov 9, 2021 - Sep 29, 2022). Non-chrome 2022s and ALL 2023-2024s excluded — GM said later trucks used parts outside the supplier's suspect production window.
6. **Oct 9, 2026** — ODI opens RQ26003: 118 complaints, 21 post-remedy. Scope now 2022-2024, chrome and non-chrome.

## Hazard detail
- Detachment "without detectability" per ODI; at-risk parties: the truck, nearby vehicles, **motorcyclists and pedestrians**
- Owner reports: cracked windshields, evasive maneuvers by other drivers, front-end rattle before separation

## What's NOT established (limitations)
- RQ is not a defect finding and not a recall; ODI may close it after review (cf. Honda Ridgeline camera probe closed Oct 2026 after VRTC testing)
- 21 post-remedy complaints ≠ 21% remedy failure rate — denominator is all complaints, not all repaired trucks (~64% of 70,768 ≈ 45k repaired)
- No injury/crash causation established; GM's own filing listed 4 accident allegations, none linked
- Cause of post-remedy detachments unexplained — neither NHTSA nor GM has stated a theory

## Kill test: genuinely newsworthy?
**Yes.** A recall query is NHTSA's formal mechanism for "your recall didn't work." It challenges BOTH dimensions of a recall at once: the fix failed on trucks that got it (21 cases), and the population was drawn 7.6x too narrow. Plus the buried lede: GM closed the internal probe once, reopened it when warranty data overrode the engineering call. That's the noir beat Rex owns.

## Novel angle (not in any existing story)
- **The math the coverage skipped:** 21/118 is an 18% post-remedy share of complaints; against ~45k completed repairs the implied per-vehicle rate is unknown, but ODI clearly thinks the numerator matters more than the denominator math GM will offer.
- **The counterfactual:** the 2023-2024 trucks were excluded on GM's supplier-window claim. ODI's complaint file now includes those model years. The query is, in effect, an audit of GM's scoping decision.
- **Recall-completion gap:** 64% completion means ~25k of the originally recalled trucks were still unrepaired as of Jan 2026 — driving around with a known detachable grille.

## Actionable takeaway
- 2022 Sierra 1500 owners: check VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls for 25V-060 / GM campaign N242490120; listen for front-end rattle (GM's own warning sign).
- 2023-2024 Sierra 1500 owners: you have NO recall and NO remedy right now — RQ26003 is still an open question. If your deflector rattles, file an ODI complaint (that complaint file is what opened this query).
