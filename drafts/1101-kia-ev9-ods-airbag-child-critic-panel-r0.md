## 3. Ethics Reviewer — 9.0

## 6. Research Rigor — 8.5
Original contributions, plural: (a) the population finding — this is essentially the entire Georgia production run, not a bad batch; (b) the 6% arithmetic — ~1,277 actually defective vs 21,290 needing inspection, making the inspection burden the economic story; (c) the silent-degradation framing — a federally mandated sensor destroyed by ordinary seat adjustment. Limitations stated explicitly (6% methodology not public, harm potential not realized, sales-as-fleet-proxy caveat). Strongest counterargument given full strength (self-reported, no injuries, front-seat kids already against guidance). Methodology transparent: 0.06 × 21,290 = 1,277.4. Deducted 1.5: the article leans on topspeed for the "certain 2026s" population boundary without a second source confirming the 2026 cutoff, and the goodcarbadcar sales denominator is presented in references but not used in the prose, leaving the "entire production run" claim resting on one secondary source.
